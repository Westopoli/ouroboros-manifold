## Q1. StarCraft and StarCraft II: Scanner Sweep / Comsat, Observer, Overseer, Oracle Revelation, Sensor Tower, cloak and detection; documented opportunity cost; how pros describe scan usage

### Takeaway
SC2 Scanner Sweep costs 50 energy from an Orbital Command, the same 50 energy as a MULE, so every scan is a documented forgone mineral income (a MULE yields about 200-225 minerals). It reveals a radius-13 circle anywhere on the map for 12.3 s including cloaked, burrowed and hallucinated units. Brood War's Comsat is the same 50-energy, 200-cap design (four scans stored) with a roughly 7-10 s reveal.

### Cited Findings

Scanner Sweep (SC2):
- Scanner Sweep: "scan a target location on the map revealing cloaked, burrowed, and hallucinated units temporarily"; cost 50 energy from an Orbital Command; range whole map; duration 12.3 s; radius 13; hotkey C; no research required. — [Liquipedia Scanner Sweep (API read)](https://liquipedia.net/starcraft2/api.php?action=parse&page=Scanner_Sweep&prop=wikitext&format=json) and a second read via search of the same page [Liquipedia Scanner Sweep](https://liquipedia.net/starcraft2/Scanner_Sweep); VERIFIED (two reads of the same wiki; no Blizzard primary found for these numbers). The Fandom page confirms only the visual history: the animation changed from a bright blue ring (Beta Patch 8) to a dull orange circle with a counter-clockwise radar line, the only documented change on that page — [StarCraft Wiki (Fandom) Scanner sweep](https://starcraft.fandom.com/wiki/Scanner_sweep); REPORTED.
- Orbital Command (LotV): starting energy 50, max energy 200, Calldown: MULE costs 50 energy. — [Liquipedia Orbital Command (LotV), API read](https://liquipedia.net/starcraft2/api.php?action=parse&page=Orbital_Command_(Legacy_of_the_Void)&prop=wikitext&format=json); REPORTED (energy cost of Scanner Sweep is not listed in the summarized text of this page; 50 comes from the Scanner Sweep page above and the MULE page statement that scan "costs the same amount of energy").
- MULE: costs 50 energy, lasts 64 s of game time, "will return its cargo nine times providing the player with 225 (25x9) minerals" on near/far patches, with the page also stating 8-9 trips and 200-225 minerals; as effective as about 3.45 SCVs on near patches and 3.94 on far patches; energy regenerates 50 in about 63.5 s. — [Liquipedia MULE (LotV), API read](https://liquipedia.net/starcraft2/api.php?action=parse&page=MULE_(Legacy_of_the_Void)&prop=wikitext&format=json) and [Liquipedia Orbital Command (LotV)](https://liquipedia.net/starcraft2/api.php?action=parse&page=Orbital_Command_(Legacy_of_the_Void)&prop=wikitext&format=json); VERIFIED (two pages agree on 225 and 3.45/3.94).
- Documented opportunity cost, verbatim from the MULE page: "There are few instances where doing so in order to scout or repair damaged units would be worth the loss of economy, especially with Scanner Sweep costing the same amount of energy." The Scanner Sweep page states each scan "effectively costs them minerals by denying them the ability to call down MULEs" (same wording on Fandom). — [Liquipedia MULE (LotV)](https://liquipedia.net/starcraft2/api.php?action=parse&page=MULE_(Legacy_of_the_Void)&prop=wikitext&format=json); [StarCraft Wiki Scanner sweep](https://starcraft.fandom.com/wiki/Scanner_sweep); VERIFIED that the wiki says this, with the caveat that the wording is community-authored, not Blizzard.

No Blizzard rationale for the 2.5.5 removal and reversal was found in search results.
- Pro-sourced usage claim on the Scanner Sweep page: "Trump claimed that you should nearly always scan your opponent's base at 6:30 to see what build order they are using". The page also lists scan uses by matchup (vs Protoss: detect Dark Templar tech and kill Observers to stop Feedback on Ghosts; vs Terran: cloaked Banshees, nukes, Siege Tank positions; vs Zerg: mid-game Lair-timing scan to read tech for Mutalisks) and an alternative of lifting off a hidden Barracks to scout "without depleting scan charges, though this trades unit production time for resource efficiency". — [Liquipedia Scanner Sweep (API read)](https://liquipedia.net/starcraft2/api.php?action=parse&page=Scanner_Sweep&prop=wikitext&format=json); REPORTED (attributed quote to a player, no original source cited on the page).
- Community guidance (forum and search summaries, not read in full): early game most energy goes to MULEs except to check tech; scan becomes more attractive mid-game as mineral banks rise and patches drain faster; scan is correct "when the information or detection changes the game state: hidden tech, invisible units, creep clearing for a timing, or pre-fight confirmation". — [TeamLiquid "MULES vs scans" thread (listed in search; page returned 403)](https://tl.net/forum/starcraft-2/114445-mules-vs-scans) and [SC2 Replay AI Coach Terran guide](https://www.starcraft2.ai/en/guides/terran); REPORTED, low weight (the second is a commercial tool page).

### Inferences
- ESTIMATED opportunity cost of one scan in SC2: one scan forgoes one MULE, which per the Liquipedia MULE page returns 200-225 minerals over 64 s (9 trips x 25 = 225). Because 50 energy regenerates in about 63.5 s on that page, a scan is roughly one minute of Orbital energy, so scanning every minute continuously would forgo a MULE's worth (about 225 minerals) each minute. This is my arithmetic from the cited figures, not a source claim.
- The price of a scan is paid in a shared, fungible pool (Orbital energy) that also funds the economy, so the trade is scan against income. Brood War's Comsat pool is separate from economy and instead caps stored scans at four, so there the limit is a stockpile, not a trade. This follows from the two wikis' pages but is not stated by any source as a design intent.
- The Sensor Tower's "senses but does not detect" wording shows a distinct class of tier: a persistent, passive, low-information radar (ping marks only) as opposed to the active, high-information, short, targeted Scanner Sweep.

### Gaps
- No Blizzard, developer or pro-player primary source on why Scanner Sweep is 50 energy and 12.3 s; no patch-history entry for scan radius or duration was found (Fandom lists only the animation change).
- Original TeamLiquid and Reddit threads on scan-vs-MULE value (HTTP 403) were not read; no pro quotation other than the unsourced "Trump" line was verified. No Day[9] / Artosis / Husky quote about scan skill expression was found despite a targeted search.
- Brood War Comsat scan radius in game units and exact version for the 6.8-11 s figure not found.
- Oracle Revelation cast range and radius; Observer detection range figure inconsistencies (Liquipedia text says sight 11 with an upgrade of +4 and Surveillance 13.75 to 15) not reconciled.
- Liquipedia SC2 "Scouting" page and Overlord (LotV) page returned HTTP 429 and were not read.

## Q2. Dota 2 and League of Legends: Scan, Observer and Sentry wards, Smoke of Deceit, dewarding; trinkets, control wards, sweepers, vision score; cost and contestation of vision

### Takeaway
Both games treat vision as a priced, timed, contestable resource: placed vision has a gold or charge cost and a lifespan, enemy vision denial earns a reward (dewarding bounty, vision score credit), and Dota adds a team-wide, free, long-cooldown Scan that only reports whether enemy heroes are present, not who or how many. LoL splits the verbs across trinkets (place, sweep, long-range one-shot ward).

### Cited Findings

Dota 2 Scan:
- Introduced in patch 6.87: "Each team now has a Scan ability, available on the minimap UI." "Scans a targeted 900 AoE for 8 seconds. Indicates whether there are enemy heroes in that area during the 8 seconds." "Starts on cooldown and has a global team-wide cooldown of 4.5 minutes." "Does not consider units inside the Roshan Pit, but does consider Smoked units." "Does not show how many heroes there are, just if there are any enemies." "Enemies do not know when your team casts it." The patch notes give no developer rationale. — [Dota 2 Gameplay Update 6.87](https://www.dota2.com/687); VERIFIED.
- Later changes: patch 7.03 reduced the cooldown from 270 s to 210 s; patch 7.34 gave Scan 2 charges (initial charge 1, 210 s charge replenish). — [Dota 2 patches and wiki per search summary: Dota Patch Notes 7.34](https://www.dota2.com/patches/7.34) and [Liquipedia Scan](https://liquipedia.net/dota2/Scan); REPORTED (patch note page returned no usable text to the fetch tool; summary is from search results).
- Current Liquipedia entry: 210 s per charge, 2 charges, shared team-wide; radius 900; checks "at 1-second intervals over 8 seconds, beginning immediately"; detects enemy heroes only, and also those under Smoke of Deceit, invisible, or illusions; "Does not show how many heroes there are, just if there are any enemy heroes"; excludes invulnerable, hidden and creep-hero units; enemies get no notification; teammates communicate scan status via Alt+click; spectators see both teams' cooldowns; a 2025-05-21 patch moved the visual effect into the game world alongside the minimap. — [Liquipedia Dota 2 Scan, API read](https://liquipedia.net/dota2/api.php?action=parse&page=Scan&prop=wikitext&format=json); VERIFIED for the 6.87-origin facts (the official note agrees), REPORTED for the later detail.
- A guide summary says Scan "gives a moment of confirmation" while wards give longer-lasting information, and recommends using it to check Roshan, confirm presence, protect a smoke move, or test whether a dangerous area is occupied. — [Dota 2 vision guides via search (Boosteria / dota2gamers.gg)](https://boosteria.org/guides/dota-2-vision-guide-ward-placement-dewarding-map-control); REPORTED, low quality commercial guide.

Dota 2 wards, smoke, dewarding:
- Observer Ward: free (stocked), 360 s duration, vision radius 1600, 1 charge per ward with 1 s between plantings, stock 2 initial / 4 maximum with 135 s restock; dewarding bounty "scales with game time at 4 gold per minute" (equivalently 1 gold every 15 s per a second source); initial bounty value not stated. — [Liquipedia Observer Ward, API read](https://liquipedia.net/dota2/api.php?action=parse&page=Observer_Ward&prop=wikitext&format=json) and [Liquipedia Observer Ward search summary](https://liquipedia.net/dota2/Observer_Ward); VERIFIED (two reads agree on 360 s / 1600 / 4 max / 135 s). The wiki states "Does not cost any gold", which reflects a later version than the older paid-ward model; no patch number for that change was found.
- Sentry Ward: 50 gold; 420 s duration; True Sight passive that "reveals nearby invisible enemy units and wards around it"; destroyed by 2 hero attacks or 10 non-hero attacks; True Sight radius not stated in the read. — [Liquipedia Sentry Ward, API read](https://liquipedia.net/dota2/api.php?action=parse&page=Sentry_Ward&prop=wikitext&format=json); REPORTED.
- Smoke of Deceit: 50 gold per charge; 45 s invisibility; application radius 1200; dispel radius 1025; hides the user and allied player-controlled units from the minimap and grants True Sight immunity; invisibility breaks if enemy heroes, Spirit Bear or towers are inside the 1025 radius, on attack or ability use, or on entering the enemy fountain; certain abilities (Bloodseeker Thirst, Bounty Hunter Track, Slardar Corrosive Haze) can show the silhouette. Scan still reports smoked heroes (see 6.87 above). — [Liquipedia Smoke of Deceit, API read](https://liquipedia.net/dota2/api.php?action=parse&page=Smoke_of_Deceit&prop=wikitext&format=json); REPORTED.
- Guide-level framing: "Dewarding is the difference between 'we have wards' and 'we control the map.' ... Removing that information is often worth more than placing an additional observer." — [Dota 2 vision guide via search (Boosteria)](https://boosteria.org/guides/dota-2-vision-guide-ward-placement-dewarding-map-control); REPORTED, low quality, an assertion not backed by data. A quantitative "Dewarding analysis" on Medium exists but returned HTTP 403 and was not read: [Medium: Dewarding analysis in Dota 2](https://medium.com/@hoangduyanh30/dewarding-analysis-in-dota-2-2befe0c44c69).

League of Legends:
- Stealth Ward trinket (Warding Totem): ward duration 90-120 s (scales with average champion level), 2 charges, recharge 210-90 s (scales), sight radius 900, cast range 625; every player has it; patch V26.03 raised the recharge from 170-90 to 210-90. — [LoL Wiki Stealth Ward](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Stealth_Ward); VERIFIED (official-community wiki read directly).
- Control Ward: 75 gold (sell 30, 40 after Support Quest), vision 900, "reveals and disables enemy wards" and reveals stealthed traps and camouflaged champions via periodic scans (not persistent detection); limit 1 Control Ward placed per player and 2 carried; 4 health, regenerating 1 per 3 s after 6 s without damage; no inherent expiry. History: V6.22 introduced it (replacing Vision Ward); V8.23 carry limit 3 to 2; V26.03 now starts in the role quest slot. — [LoL Wiki Control Ward](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Control_Ward); VERIFIED.
- Oracle Lens (sweeper): 8 s sweep (6 s to 8 s in V26.01; 10 s to 6 s in V13.10); 5 s cooldown between charges, 2 charges, recharge 160-100 s (scales); radius 600-750 by level; detects stealthed enemies, gives true sight on stealthed wards and traps, and temporarily disables revealed wards; from V13.10, attacking a ward reveals it to the team for 2 s. — [LoL Wiki Oracle Lens](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Oracle_Lens); VERIFIED.
- Farsight Alteration (long-range one-shot ward trinket): cast range 4000; sight radius 500 (rises to 800 when it detects an enemy champion); lasts indefinitely, self-destructs 3 s after detecting a champion; cooldown 198-99 s (scales); auto-pings revealed champions on the minimap; V13.10 added terrain vision, brush penetration, champion detection and auto-ping. — [LoL Wiki Farsight Alteration](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Farsight_Alteration); VERIFIED.
- Vision score formula: 1 point per minute of ward lifetime provided plus 1 point per minute of ward lifetime denied (1.5 min for permanent wards), with fractional credit for vision abilities (0.33 per champion revealed) and reductions for staleness, redundancy, safety and pointlessness. — [LoL Wiki Vision score](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Vision_score); VERIFIED.
- Riot stated design intent (Riot GMang, Vision Designer, 2017): "Vision Score is a new tool to help players in this effort. It's more complex and comprehensive than Wards Placed." and "Vision Score is less trying to be Champ Mastery and more trying to be CS or Damage Dealt - it's a useful metric to evaluate performance, but nowhere near a 'final score.'" — [Surrender at 20 Red Post Collection, April 20, 2017](https://www.surrenderat20.net/2017/04/red-post-collection-gameplay-thoughts_20.html); VERIFIED (read directly; Riot staff quoted by a fan aggregation site).
- A 2020 academic chapter "WARDS: Modelling the Worth of Vision in MOBAs" exists (Springer) but its content could not be accessed (paywall redirect), so no quantitative claim is made here. — [Springer chapter](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-030-52246-9_5); not read.

### Inferences
- ESTIMATED: at 360 s duration, 135 s restock and 4 max stock in Dota, a team can sustain placing about one ward per 135 s per player slot before stock empties; the relevant cap is stock and lifespan, not gold. Source figures are Liquipedia's; the sustained-rate reading is my arithmetic.
- Across both games vision is paid for in multiple currencies at once (gold, charges, restock time, risk of exposure when placing) and denial is separately rewarded (bounty, vision score), which is the documented sense in which it is "contested". The sources document the rewards but no source states this framing explicitly except the dewarding guide above.
- Dota Scan is a deliberate downgrade in information resolution relative to a ward (binary presence per area, no count, no identity), in the same game where wards give full vision for 6 minutes. This is read from the 6.87 text; no designer states the intent.

### Gaps
- No Valve developer statement on the rationale for Scan, the 7.03 cooldown change, or the 7.34 second charge; the 7.34 patch page did not return text to the fetch tool.
- No patch number found for observer wards becoming free in Dota, or the Sentry True Sight radius.
- No Riot dev post found on why trinket durations scale with average champion level, or on the Vision Ward to Control Ward change (V6.22) beyond the wiki changelog.
- No quantified data on dewarding frequency or vision value (Medium analysis HTTP 403, Springer paywalled).

## Q3. Other RTS: Company of Heroes, Wargame / WARNO / Steel Division, World in Conflict, Age of Empires, Supreme Commander, Homeworld, Halo Wars

### Takeaway
Of these, Homeworld Remastered's Sensors Array ping and Company of Heroes' munition-priced recon sweep and flares are the closest documented analogues to "spend an action to see a place briefly". Homeworld Remastered turned a passive sensor into a paid ping showing all ships for about 5-10 s. Wargame and Supreme Commander instead put information in persistent unit and structure ratings (optics, stealth, radar and omni) that the opponent counters by killing recon or adding stealth and jamming.

### Cited Findings

Homeworld:
- In Homeworld Remastered the Sensors Array "now requires that you select it and press a button to 'ping' and then you can see enemy units on the map but only for a few seconds, in the original games you could see them all the time with a sensors array." — Steam user Jack9579, Feb 25, 2015, [HW1 Remastered - Areas of Major Differences](https://steamcommunity.com/app/244160/discussions/0/617328967244650904/); VERIFIED that the post says this (read directly); REPORTED as fact about the game (player post).
- A reply in a multiplayer fog of war thread: "Use ping on sensors arrays and hiig. scouts (with 'enhanced sensors' research). It costs 1000 and shows all ships for 5-10 seconds." — [Fog of War thread, Homeworld Remastered Collection](https://steamcommunity.com/app/244160/discussions/0/530646080858298060/); REPORTED (player post; cost unit is RU per search-result summaries; 1000 cost and 5-10 s duration not found in an official source).
- Search-result summaries add a 10 s cooldown between pings, a scout ping costing 500 RU, and that the Taiidan sensor array ping was enabled in multiplayer in a beta update, none confirmed by a primary source read. — [Gearbox forum "Vaygr needs PING!"](https://forums.gearboxsoftware.com/t/vaygr-needs-ping/246380?page=2) (host did not resolve; summary only), [Gearbox update information](https://support.gearboxsoftware.com/hc/en-us/articles/205281840-Homeworld-Remastered-Collection-Update-Information); AMBIGUOUS/low confidence (cooldown and scout cost come from one unverified summary).

- HW2 Remastered Proximity Sensor "improves your abilities to detect cloaked ships in the nearby area" (search-result summary of a fan-run unit guide). — [Homeworld 2 Hiigaran Proximity Sensor](http://hw2.standardof.net/p/homeworld-2-hiigaran-proximity-sensor.html); REPORTED, summary only.

Company of Heroes (2):

Wargame (Red Dragon; the other two games in the title not covered):

Both are community opinions on specific commander or aid options, not generalizable facts.

## Q4. Free or near-free expendable scouts: Age of Empires IV, Northgard, They Are Billions, Zerg overlords and changelings: costing and counters

### Takeaway
Documented expendable scouts are cheap but not free in a resource that matters: the AoE IV Scout costs 65 food, Northgard's scouts need a 50-wood camp plus recurring upkeep, and the SC2 Changeling is truly free in minerals, gas and supply but gated by an Overseer (50/50 plus 12 s morph) and Overseer energy. Counters documented are killing the scout, recognising a disguised scout, or detecting it.

### Cited Findings
- AoE IV Scout costs 65 food (60 at launch, 70 at patch 11009, 65 at patch 12.2.3227); it is a light cavalry unit used for exploring, hunting and herding sheep. Mongol Outpost: 70 wood. — [Fandom: Scout (Age of Empires IV)](https://ageofempires.fandom.com/wiki/Scout_(Age_of_Empires_IV)), [Outpost (Age of Empires IV)](https://ageofempires.fandom.com/wiki/Outpost_(Age_of_Empires_IV)); REPORTED.
- Northgard: Scout Camp costs 50 wood to build; upkeep 15-20 Krowns per year depending on difficulty (28 Krowns after a 50 wood, 50 Krowns, 5 stone upgrade, which is needed to scout enemy territory); Scouts have 50 health and 0 attack; they are the only units that can search Ruins and Shipwrecks; revealing a tile takes 20 s (closest ring), 60 s (second ring), 80 s (third ring) depending on distance from the Town Hall. — [Northgard Wiki Scout Camp](https://northgard.fandom.com/wiki/Scout_Camp), [Northgard Scout](https://northgard.fandom.com/wiki/Scout) (listed; content from search summary); REPORTED.
- SC2 Changeling: 0 minerals, 0 gas, 0 supply; lasts 107 s; disguises as an enemy basic unit and adopts enemy colour so it is not auto-attacked; countered by selecting it ("Changeling" appears), by it lacking a selection circle, by it ignoring orders, by force-attacking it, by long-range units and by cloaked units. — [Liquipedia Changeling (LotV)](https://liquipedia.net/starcraft2/api.php?action=parse&page=Changeling_(Legacy_of_the_Void)&prop=wikitext&format=json); REPORTED.
- SC2 Overseer is a morphed Overlord: 50 minerals / 50 gas, 12 s morph, sight 11, detects cloaked and burrowed; an Overlord-origin unit is slow and not free. — [Liquipedia Overseer (LotV)](https://liquipedia.net/starcraft2/api.php?action=parse&page=Overseer_(Legacy_of_the_Void)&prop=wikitext&format=json); REPORTED.
- Supreme Commander T1 Air Scout has a 42-radius vision and no radar; countered by interceptors (assumed; not stated in the read source). — [Supcom Fandom T1 air scout](https://supcom.fandom.com/wiki/T1_air_scout); AMBIGUOUS (see Q3).
- SC2 Observer: 25/75, permanently cloaked, so opponents need detection to kill it. — [Liquipedia Observer (LotV)](https://liquipedia.net/starcraft2/api.php?action=parse&page=Observer_(Legacy_of_the_Void)&prop=wikitext&format=json); REPORTED. Scan is the Terran tool that removes it (see the Scanner Sweep page cited in Q1).
- They Are Billions: no scouting-specific source was found.

### Inferences
- The unit costs span "meaningful food or wood" (AoE IV, Northgard) to "free but gated" (Changeling); the counters are unit-based (kill it, spot the disguise) and not UI-based. This is a pattern in the retrieved items, not a source claim.
- The Changeling's counterplay is attention-based: the defender must notice an oddity (missing selection circle, unresponsive unit), so the scout's cost is paid by the opponent's attention. This reading is mine from the Changeling counters list.

## Q5. Design commentary: why active vision adds depth or busywork; reworks and removals

### Takeaway
I found little direct designer commentary on active reveal abilities. What exists is mostly patch-note sequences (which show repeated retuning of cost, cooldown, duration and range) and general RTS design writing on attention as a resource; no source was found where a developer states that an active scouting ability was removed for busywork. The documented rework or conversion cases are Homeworld Remastered (passive to paid ping), SC2 Revelation and Sensor Tower retuning, LoL Oracle Lens and Control Ward changes, and Dota Scan charges.

### Cited Findings
- General principle: "The player themselves have a limited supply of an implicit resource - time. Any well designed RTS game will take into account the number of actions, and indeed perhaps more importantly the number and difficulty of each decision that a player will be forced to make within a given window in which to be effective"; and "either player is capable of forcing their opponent to misallocate or waste time to their own advantage. Indecision, fear and distraction are powerful tools." (Brandon Casteel). — [Wayward Strategy: Random thoughts on resource management in RTS (Dec 18, 2014)](https://waywardstrategy.com/2014/12/18/random-thoughts-on-resource-management-in-rts/); VERIFIED (read directly; design blog). The post does not discuss scouting directly.
- Same post: "StarCraft and StarCraft 2 are perhaps the most mechanically demanding RTS games currently being played. These games are designed to respond about as quickly as the user is able to input commands." — [Wayward Strategy](https://waywardstrategy.com/2014/12/18/random-thoughts-on-resource-management-in-rts/); VERIFIED.
- Search-result paraphrase of RTS design writing: players can invest money in scouting instead of APM or multitasking potential, and some games strain player attention without APM demands. — [RTS Game Design - Part 1 (Medium)](https://medium.com/@jeremie.stamand/rts-game-design-part-1-903f4057e281) and [Wayward Strategy RTS Design Thought](https://waywardstrategy.com/2016/02/17/rts-design-thought-control-of-tactical-outcomes/) listed in search; REPORTED (summary only).
- Liquipedia's own framing of SC2 scan: use a scan against specific hidden information (tech, cloaked units, army composition) and weigh it against MULE income. — [Liquipedia Scanner Sweep](https://liquipedia.net/starcraft2/api.php?action=parse&page=Scanner_Sweep&prop=wikitext&format=json); REPORTED.
- Information-for-attention design in a different genre: Riot's vision score counts "ward lifetime provided" and "denied" and applies "staleness, redundancy, and safety" modifiers so low-effort placement earns less (Riot GMang, 2017). — [Surrender at 20 Red Post Collection](https://www.surrenderat20.net/2017/04/red-post-collection-gameplay-thoughts_20.html); VERIFIED.
- Reworks and conversions documented in patch records:
  - Homeworld Remastered: passive Sensors Array replaced by an activated paid ping showing enemies for a few seconds; a player describes this as a downgrade. — [Steam HW1 Remastered differences thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/244160/discussions/0/617328967244650904/); REPORTED, no developer rationale.
  - SC2 Revelation: duration 43 to 30 s (4.0), then cost 50 to 25 energy, cooldown 2 to 10 s and duration 30 to 15 s (4.12.0, a cheaper, more spammable but shorter and rate-limited ability), then duration 15 to 20 s (5.0.2 BU). — [Blizzard 4.0 notes](https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/21183638/starcraft-ii-4-0-patch-notes), [Blizzard 4.12.0 notes](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/starcraft2/23442717/starcraft-ii-4-12-0-patch-notes), [Liquipedia Oracle](https://liquipedia.net/starcraft2/api.php?action=parse&page=Oracle_(Legacy_of_the_Void)&prop=wikitext&format=json); VERIFIED for 4.0 and 4.12.0.
  - SC2 Sensor Tower: cost 125/100 to 100/50 and range 30 to 27 to 22 across 5.0.11 and 5.0.14. — [Liquipedia Sensor Tower](https://liquipedia.net/starcraft2/api.php?action=parse&page=Sensor_Tower_(Legacy_of_the_Void)&prop=wikitext&format=json); REPORTED.
  - SC2 MULE: removed in 2.5.5, returned as autocast with no energy cost, then reverted to energy-based within about a month. — [Liquipedia Orbital Command (LotV)](https://liquipedia.net/starcraft2/api.php?action=parse&page=Orbital_Command_(Legacy_of_the_Void)&prop=wikitext&format=json); REPORTED. This changes the energy-trade that makes scanning costly; no rationale found.
  - LoL Oracle Lens: 2-charge system added and duration 10 to 6 s in V13.10, with attacking a ward now revealing it for 2 s, then duration 6 to 8 s in V26.01. — [LoL Wiki Oracle Lens](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Oracle_Lens); VERIFIED.
  - LoL Control Ward: Vision Ward replaced in V6.22; carry limit 3 to 2 in V8.23; role-quest slot V26.03. — [LoL Wiki Control Ward](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Control_Ward); VERIFIED.
  - Dota Scan: began on cooldown with 4.5 min team-wide cooldown (6.87), cooldown cut in 7.03, second charge in 7.34. — [Dota 2 6.87](https://www.dota2.com/687); VERIFIED for 6.87, REPORTED for 7.03 and 7.34.
  - LoL (2026 season) is reported to be adding "Faelights", temporary vision points that empower placed wards (superward with 25% more sight radius and a bonus reveal region for 45 s). — [Inven Global season 1 2026 dev update, via search](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/19939/league-of-legends-reveals-major-season-1-2026-overhaul-in-final-dev-update-of-the-year); REPORTED (not read in full).

### Inferences
- The patch sequences show designers repeatedly tuning cost, cooldown, duration and range of reveal abilities, but not removing them, which is consistent with the abilities being kept but difficult to price. This is an inference from retrieved patch history only; the retrieved set is not a systematic survey.
- The Casteel passage supports the claim that attention itself is a costed resource (and that forcing the opponent to spend attention is an attack), but does not address vision abilities specifically, so applying it to scan or ping design would be my extension.

### Gaps
- No GDC talk, Game Developer article, developer blog or interview was found that directly discusses active vision/scan abilities as a design topic, or says one added "busywork". Search for pro and caster commentary on scan skill expression (Day[9], Artosis, Husky) returned only index pages.
- No developer statement on why Homeworld Remastered changed the Sensors Array, why SC2 removed then returned the MULE, or why Revelation or Sensor Tower were retuned.
- Did not find a documented case of a shipped game removing an active reveal ability outright for attention-tax reasons.
- The Dota 2 7.41 patch coverage that surfaced in search ("U-turns on one of Valve's biggest additions") was not read and its relevance to vision is unknown.
- The `docs/legacy/games/` cards give no overlap with this topic.
