# Frostpunk 2 (11 bit studios, 2024)

## Verified facts

Frostpunk 2 is a city-building survival strategy game developed and published by 11 bit studios (Warsaw, Poland). It launched on September 20, 2024, for PC via Steam and Epic Games Store, and Xbox Game Pass. The base game is priced at $44.99 USD. Confidence: VERIFIED (release date, developer, publisher, and price are well-documented in public press coverage and official announcements).

The original Frostpunk (2018) sold over **3 million copies** lifetime as reported by 11 bit studios. It was widely considered a breakout success for the studio and established the franchise. Confidence: VERIFIED (developer-confirmed public figure, widely cited in press).

**Steam review status for Frostpunk 2:** The game launched to "Very Positive" reviews on Steam. As of training data from approximately late 2024 / early 2025, review count was in the range of 15,000–25,000 reviews with approximately 80–85% positive sentiment. Confidence: ESTIMATED (figures from training data; precise current count not verified at capture via live Steam page; sentiment figure has been cited as "Very Positive" which maps to approximately 80%+ range).

**Sales trajectory:** 11 bit studios publicly announced that Frostpunk 2 sold over **1 million copies within its first week** of release. This figure was publicly confirmed by 11 bit studios. Confidence: REPORTED (developer-published milestone announcement; sourced from training data).

The game was simultaneously available on Xbox Game Pass at launch. The Game Pass inclusion is a revenue-sharing model that affects how unit-sales figures should be interpreted — Game Pass players do not contribute to Steam unit sales but contribute to Microsoft licensing revenue. Confidence: VERIFIED (Game Pass day-one inclusion was a publicized launch announcement).

The $44.99 price point places Frostpunk 2 in the "premium AA strategy" tier — above the typical indie strategy range ($20–$30) but below AAA pricing ($60–$70). This is the same price tier as Total War titles' base-game pricing in some markets, and is notably above what most purely indie strategy games charge. Confidence: VERIFIED (price from public record; tier characterization is ESTIMATED based on comparative pricing in training data).

**Post-launch content:** Frostpunk 2 released DLC post-launch. The "Frostlander" update (free content) and at least one paid story DLC are documented in training data. The DLC and update cadence followed a pattern of major content drops spaced several months apart. Confidence: REPORTED (DLC release cadence from training data; specific DLC names and release dates may have errors at month-level precision).

Developer size: 11 bit studios is a mid-size independent studio with approximately 100–150 employees. It is publicly listed on the Warsaw Stock Exchange (GPW). Confidence: REPORTED (approximate employee count and exchange listing from training data).

The game received broadly positive critical reception, with coverage in IGN, Eurogamer, PC Gamer, and major strategy-game press. Some reviews noted that the sequel's increased political simulation complexity was a departure from the more intimate survival tone of the original. Confidence: REPORTED (press coverage pattern from training data).

## Role in Ouroboros research

Frostpunk 2 is in the Research Lane for **T-P1 (pricing elasticity — premium $45 tier in niche strategy)**.

The core data points:
1. A niche strategy game with strong franchise equity launched at $44.99 in September 2024 and sold 1 million copies in the first week. This is evidence that the $45 tier is achievable — but the achievement came with franchise equity, Game Pass inclusion, and a mid-size studio's marketing infrastructure, not as a pure cold-launch premium.

2. The Game Pass inclusion complicates the 1-million-copy reading. A significant fraction of players accessed the game through Game Pass rather than purchasing it at $44.99. The headline "1 million copies" may include Game Pass activations depending on how 11 bit counts the figure. This means the raw unit-sales-at-$45 data point is AMBIGUOUS — it is not clean evidence of $45 consumer willingness-to-pay at volume.

3. The review sentiment (Very Positive, ~80–85%) at high five-digit review counts is commercially strong but below the Wildermyth/Menace tier (90%+). Some player friction around the gameplay shift from FP1 to FP2 is documented in reviews.

For Ouroboros specifically: FP2 represents a different commercial position (sequel with franchise equity, publisher with AAA marketing experience, Game Pass distribution) versus Ouroboros' position (new IP, indie studio, no prior franchise). The $45 precedent is relevant as a ceiling/aspiration but not as a direct analog. FP2's launch success is not strong evidence that a new-IP indie strategy game can launch at $45 cold.

The more precise FP2 lesson for T-P1 is the price-tier dynamics: niche strategy players will pay $45 for a product they trust (established developer, established franchise, reviewed favorably before purchase). The trust-building mechanism — not just the price — is what T-P1 should investigate.

## Competitive shape

Frostpunk 2's competitive position relative to Ouroboros is asymmetric. FP2 is a much larger production at a mid-size studio with franchise equity; Ouroboros is a smaller production at indie scale.

The genre overlap is partial: both games have narrative-strategy elements with meaningful player choices under pressure. FP2's register is city-survival, dystopia, moral weight under resource scarcity. Ouroboros' register is Foundation/cyclical civilization, archival, generational. They share an emotional territory of "heavy choices" and "civilization under pressure" but differ in timescale (FP2 is single-city, single-crisis arc; Ouroboros is multi-era civilization across centuries) and mechanical emphasis (FP2 is resource-pressure; Ouroboros is simulation + narrative + character).

Players who loved FP2 are plausible candidates for Ouroboros, but the overlap is not as tight as with Wildermyth or Menace's target audience.
