# Press Outlet Map

## What this brief is

Press outlet map for niche-strategy/sim launch outreach. Tier breakdown (Tier-1 mainstream gaming press, Tier-2 niche-strategy specialist press, Tier-3 long-tail community / Substack / written-form YouTube), per-outlet niche-strategy posture, editor-contact discovery patterns, embargo norms, review-copy turnaround norms, preview-vs-review distinction, pitch-success rates, anti-patterns.

Non-rec disclaimer: surfaces inputs only. No recommendation of outlet target list, outreach order, embargo window, or asset package. Final pick is Design Lane's.

Bible context: Ouroboros targets 80k-200k generational/legacy strategy primary tribe plus 20k-80k Foundation/hard-SF thematic-differentiator tribe per `docs/legacy/topics/lieutenant_pivot_niche_brief.md`. Press matters most for Tier-1/2 cluster validation — Steam algorithm reads outlet coverage as quality signal. Primary tribe self-selects more via creator/curator surfaces (Quill18, SsethTzeentach, Many a True Nerd, Hooded Horse halo, /r/CrusaderKings, /r/grandstrategy) than Tier-1 review traffic — but Tier-2 niche-strategy review often tips curator/creator pickup. Press is cluster-validation lever feeding creator/curator lever, not direct sales driver.

## Tier-1 outlets

Tier-1 = mainstream English-language gaming press, millions monthly readers. Niche-strategy posture varies. Cluster validation per Tier-1 review large; cold-pitch conversion low.

**Rock Paper Shotgun (RPS).** Strongest Tier-1 fit. Strategy tag, long history of long-form strategy — CK, Stellaris, Old World, Wildermyth, Manor Lords all received feature writeups. "Have you played..." and "Premature Evaluation" early-access columns niche-strategy-friendly. Editors (REPORTED): Alice Bell, Edwin Evans-Thirlwell, Brendan Caldwell held senior strategy roles; Matt Cox covered early-access. Pitch: tips@rockpapershotgun.com plus direct editor outreach via Twitter/Bluesky.

**PC Gamer.** Moderate. Strategy section but beat rotates among general writers. Reviews major launches (Stormgate, Tempest Rising, Manor Lords) shorter than RPS. Sister site PCGamesN covers strategy deeper. Editors (REPORTED): Robert Zak, Jody Macgregor; Fraser Brown covered grand strategy. Pitch: pcgamer.com contact, Twitter DM. Strong Steam-algorithm signal, lower per-review conversion than RPS.

**Eurogamer.** Light. Reviews major launches (Frostpunk 2, Manor Lords, Civilization VII), no dedicated strategy beat. Console-adjacent. Editors (REPORTED): Christian Donlan, Edwin Evans-Thirlwell historically; Bertie Purchese. UK/EU territory algorithm signal meaningful.

**Polygon.** Occasional. Covers strategy at major-release tier only (Civilization, CK, Stellaris expansions). Narrative/accessibility framing. No dedicated strategy lead. Pitch: tips@polygon.com. Coverage infrequent enough that Polygon writeup hit-or-miss.

**Ars Technica.** Light but high-trust. Occasional deep technical/design writeups — Lee Hutchinson, Kyle Orland on simulation and historical strategy. Single Ars writeup drives disproportionate trust signal in technical/hard-SF audiences. Kyle Orland senior gaming editor. High cluster-validation for Foundation/hard-SF tribe due to overlap with literary-SF and tech readers.

**IGN.** Surface-level. Reviews major launches but rarely below AAA tier. Short scored reviews. No dedicated strategy lead. Mainstream Steam-algorithm signal meaningful; per-review niche-tribe conversion low. Stormgate and Tempest Rising both got IGN reviews; neither saved them.

## Tier-2 niche outlets

Tier-2 = strategy/wargame specialist press, 50k-500k monthly readers, much higher niche-tribe density than Tier-1. Per-review niche-tribe conversion much higher; cold-pitch acceptance much higher; embargo looser. Primary press lever for Ouroboros' primary tribe.

**Strategy Gamer (strategygamer.com).** Directly aligned. 4X, grand strategy, wargames, RTS, tactical, historical. Dense — Old World, FoG: Empires, Manor Lords, CK3 expansions all feature-reviewed. In-depth format suits chronicle/generational pitch. Editors (REPORTED): Marcello Perricone held senior roles; rotates. Editor email listed publicly. Cold-pitch acceptance high for clear hook — anecdotally 30%+ from post-mortems.

**Wargamer (wargamer.com).** Directly aligned, wargame-leaning. Grand strategy, wargames, 4X, hex-and-counter, historical. Sci-fi lighter — Stellaris, Endless Space, Battlefleet Gothic. Editors (REPORTED): Joe Robinson (founder), Joe Parlock. Editor email plus press contact form. Cold-pitch acceptance moderate-to-high; may need "history-feel strategy" framing.

**Explorminate (explorminate.net).** 4X-focused, Stellaris-adjacent. Small (low-five-figure monthly) but highest niche-density in strategy press. Editors (REPORTED): Oliver Mellet plus rotating pool. High acceptance for 4X-adjacent; overlap with Ouroboros' primary and thematic-differentiator tribes strong.

**RPS Strategy tag.** Premature Evaluation column functions as Tier-2 surface within Tier-1 outlet — strategy-tag-scoped pitches have higher acceptance than cold-pitching front page.

**GrogHeads (grogheads.com).** Hardcore wargame community site with editorial on forums. Wargame-heavy. Small, devoted. Rotating contributors plus forum admins. Pitch via site contact and forum DM. High acceptance for wargame/grand-strategy; lower for sci-fi unless framing emphasizes simulation depth.

**PCGamesN Strategy.** Aligned. Frequent news, list features, reviews. SEO-friendly listicles over long-form. Editors (REPORTED): Ed Smith plus rotating. Reach sizable; per-review conversion lower than Strategy Gamer due to listicle format.

**Adjacent Tier-2.** Eurogamer Recommended badge (rare, high-impact). Hooded Horse publisher pipeline quasi-press-surface — adjacent strategy press cascades into coverage smaller devs cannot organically generate. Slitherine similar in wargame press. SpaceSector (spacesector.com) covers space-strategy/4X, overlaps Foundation/hard-SF. Multiplayer.it, Eurogamer.it cover strategy in Italian markets where Hooded Horse drove uplift.

## Tier-3 long-tail

Tier-3 = community blogs, Substack writers, niche YouTube channels with written output, Discord-driven review surfaces, forum-embedded review threads. Per-outlet reach small (low-four to low-five-figure) but extreme-niche-density. Cold-pitch acceptance high. Embargoes looser still. Aggregate Tier-3 often where primary-tribe word-of-mouth originates before Tier-2 picks up.

**Substack writers covering strategy/sim.** Growing class. Names from post-mortems (REPORTED): Game Maker's Garage Strategy & Wargames, Possibly Correct, Vintage Strategy, plus writers attached to specific games (CK3, Stellaris, Manor Lords). Pitch via direct email through Substack profile. High cold-pitch acceptance; many writers explicitly seeking under-covered strategy.

**Niche YouTube channels with written companion blogs.** Several creators maintain written content — review posts, dev-diary roundups, "what I'm playing this week." Overlaps `docs/legacy/topics/audience_overlap_map.md`, feeds T-C1. Notable (REPORTED): Quill18 older blog content, Many a True Nerd blog posts, SsethTzeentach written notes attached to reviews, channel-adjacent Discord servers with pinned reviews.

**Community blogs.** /r/CrusaderKings stickied posts, /r/grandstrategy weekly threads, /r/Stellaris dev-diary discussion, /r/Wildermyth review threads, Paradox Plaza forum posts function as quasi-Tier-3 press surfaces. /r/asimov and /r/printSF recommendation threads serve Foundation/hard-SF.

**Hooded Horse Discord and adjacent publisher Discords.** Semi-private Tier-3 surfaces — pinned reviews and recommendations from active members carry weight inside publisher audience.

**Cross-cutting note.** Tier-3 turnaround faster, discipline looser, audience most niche-dense. Trade-off: alone does not generate Steam-algorithm cluster-validation — drives word-of-mouth feeding Tier-2 review interest. Seed surface. REPORTED for posture, ESTIMATED for reach and pitch-success.

## Embargo norms

**Tier-1.** Strict date-and-time default — embargo lifts at specific UTC time on specific date several days before or on launch day. Discipline strongly enforced; embargo-break is publisher relationship-killer. Review code sent under formal embargo agreement. Press tour content (preview builds) often under separate earlier embargo.

**Tier-2.** More flexible. Most accept date-only embargo without enforced time; some publish day-of without explicit embargo if asked. Discipline generally good but enforcement informal. "Review goes up no earlier than launch day" common Tier-2 framing rather than strict UTC-time.

**Tier-3.** Launch-day only or "publish whenever after launch." Strict date-time embargoes rare and often resisted. Tier-3 writers often publish several days after launch due to playtime requirements and small-team capacity.

**Anti-pattern all tiers.** Long embargo window with no early-access build often refused — outlets need playtime before lift; 24-hour window between code-arrival and lift widely refused. 2-4-week pre-launch turnaround norm exists to give outlets enough playtime by embargo. REPORTED.

## Review-copy turnaround norms

**Tier-1.** 2-4 weeks pre-launch typical; deep-coverage Tier-1 (RPS feature, Ars longread) may request 4-6 weeks. Requests below 2 weeks commonly declined or result in no-review / post-launch only. Often request press kit with builds, asset packs, dev-quote material, beat sheet.

**Tier-2.** 1-3 weeks pre-launch. Strategy Gamer, Wargamer, Explorminate noted in published roundups that 1-2 weeks often sufficient for focused niche-strategy review where reviewer already familiar with genre conventions.

**Tier-3.** Launch-day code or just before. Impressions within 1-2 weeks of arrival, reviews go up post-launch.

**Tactical implication.** Code distribution ~3-4 weeks pre-launch covers Tier-2/Tier-3 with margin and offers Tier-1 lower end of preferred window. Hooded Horse / Slitherine playbook widely-described as Tier-2-and-Tier-3 wave first, Tier-1 wave a week later with early Tier-2 quotes in hand. REPORTED for playbook, ESTIMATED for timing.

## Preview vs review

**Preview.** Build access for feature article ahead of launch, typically not scored, focused on "what this game is" rather than "is it good." Press tour events, hands-on demos at expos (PAX, Gamescom, Steam Next Fest), pre-launch press-day builds feed preview. Lower-commitment pitch for Tier-1 — many run feature preview where they would not run review. Also path to "I tried the demo and was surprised" narrative driving wishlists pre-launch.

**Review.** Scored, final-build coverage after embargo. More cluster-validation for Steam algorithm and curator trust. Harder pitch, slower turnaround. Tier-1 cold-pitch acceptance low.

**Niche-strategy preference.** Per GameDiscoverCo and Chris Zukowski plus multiple post-mortems, niche-strategy outlets often prefer preview pipeline — previews allow reviewer to surface hook and depth without committing to verdict, which matters for slow-burn strategy where first 5 hours do not represent late-game arc. Strategy Gamer, Wargamer, RPS have all published preview-format coverage where scored review at launch would have under-rated late-game depth.

**Tactical implication.** Ouroboros' generational/centuries-scale chronicle and lieutenant-system depth fit preview-format at launch and review-format 2-3 months later. Tier-2 outlets above suited to preview-at-launch, review-later cadence. REPORTED for framing, ESTIMATED for Ouroboros fit.

## Pitch-success rates

Figures ESTIMATED from post-mortems, Chris Zukowski, GameDiscoverCo Plus mentions, inferred ranges. Hard public data rare. Directional only.

**Tier-1 cold pitch.** ~5-10% acceptance with no prior relationship per multiple post-mortems. Climbs sharply (30-50%) with prior relationship, notable angle, exclusive asset, or publisher attached. Cold-pitching Tier-1 without hook is most-cited indie marketing failure mode.

**Tier-2 cold pitch.** ~25-50% with clear niche-strategy fit. Tier-2 actively seeking under-covered niche strategy when framing matches audience.

**Tier-3 cold pitch.** ~60-80% to aligned Tier-3 writer. Tier-3 writers often actively seeking content. Trade-off: individual acceptance much lower-impact per piece.

**Lift from relationship.** Across all tiers, prior relationship (writer covered dev's previous game; writer interacted with dev on social; writer played demo) lifts significantly — often doubling Tier-1, pushing Tier-2 toward 70%+.

**Lift from exclusive or angle.** Genuinely exclusive feature angle (first-look reveal, deep-dive on specific mechanic, dev-interview format) lifts Tier-1 cold-pitch acceptance materially. Tier-1 often accepts feature-format pitch where they would refuse review-format.

## Anti-patterns

Failure modes documented in post-mortems and indie marketing blogs. REPORTED across multiple sources.

**Cold-pitching Tier-1 without relationship or hook.** Most-cited failure. Generic press release yields ~5-10% and burns dev's standing for future pitches.

**Embargo-break.** Tier-3 writer publishing three hours before Tier-1 embargo lifts is relationship-killer. Even unintentional breaks have ended publisher relationships. Mitigation: clear embargo language, time-zone-explicit dates, Tier-3 first wave with no Tier-1 conflict.

**Asset-incomplete pitches.** Pitching Tier-1 without complete press kit (final build code, key art, capsule art, screenshots, B-roll trailer, fact sheet, dev-quote material, contact info) gets pitch ignored. Tier-2/Tier-3 more forgiving but still benefit.

**Unrequested follow-ups.** Follow-up under 5-7 business days, or multiple follow-ups to same editor, relationship-burning. Norm: one follow-up at 5-7-day mark, then silence.

**Stale editor contact.** Contacts decay; staff turnover at Tier-1 2022-2025 heavy due to layoffs. Pitching writer who left 6 months ago flags dev as not having done research. Verify via outlet's recent byline list.

**Over-pitching same outlet.** Multiple distinct features (preview, review, dev-interview, feature, news beat) in same week to same editor exhausts goodwill. Norm: one pitch per cycle.

**Demanding scored coverage.** Demanding review (vs. offering build access for whatever coverage outlet finds appropriate) off-putting. Norm: offer build access plus press kit, let outlet decide format.

**Marketing-speak.** "Next evolution of strategy gaming," "most ambitious 4X ever" get filtered. Norm: plain factual copy, hook in one sentence.

**Skipping Tier-2.** Devs pitching only Tier-1 see less total coverage than devs pitching Tier-2 first and letting Tier-2 quotes feed Tier-1 pitch.

**Bad timing.** Pitching during heavy launch week (Civilization launch, Crusader Kings expansion, Total War launch) crowds out coverage. Check launch calendar.

## Cross-cutting findings

- Tier-2 niche-strategy outlets (Strategy Gamer, Wargamer, Explorminate, RPS Strategy tag) are primary press lever for Ouroboros' primary tribe. Tier-1 mainstream press is secondary cluster-validation lever, not primary sales driver.
- Press is creator-and-curator feeder. Tier-2 review tips Quill18 / SsethTzeentach / Many-a-True-Nerd-style creator pickup; creator lever drives wishlists. Press alone insufficient; creator alone insufficient; two reinforce.
- Tier-2 accepts 1-3 week turnaround. Tier-1 often wants 2-4 weeks short-format, 4-6 weeks long-format features. Code distribution ~3-4 weeks pre-launch covers comfortable middle.
- Preview-format coverage better suited to slow-burn strategy than scored-review at launch. Niche-strategy outlets often prefer preview pipeline.
- Editor contacts decay rapidly. Any contact list needs verification before each outreach cycle. Press portals and tip lines stable fallback when contacts go stale.
- Hooded Horse and Slitherine catalogs are press-pipeline force multipliers — publisher relationship translates into Tier-2 review coverage smaller devs cannot organically generate. Self-publishing without one of these pipelines costs Tier-2 coverage breadth on average.
