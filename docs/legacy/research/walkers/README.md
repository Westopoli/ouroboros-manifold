# Walking and flying units on generated land (mockup)

Spec: `docs/northstar/plans/archive/specs/2026-10-05-proto-walkers-spec.md`. Built 2026-10-05 on branch `push/proto-walkers`, `scenes/mockups/walkers/`. Run line in `docs/northstar/ROADMAP.md` section 0.

**Approved 2026-10-05.** Westley, verbatim: "Walkers/flyers looks great, approved." **Final, 2026-10-06:** "Walkers prototype approved, no need for any more iteration that one can be integrated as is."

What the approval covers: walkers route over walkable ground only (slope at or below 35 degrees), take the route of least time, stop short of unreachable places; the layer of a walker is the band of the ground under it, crossings marked at the midline; flyers fly on a chosen layer and cannot be below the ground; a mixed group moves at its slowest member's pace; the read-out of route length and seconds. This is what E-01 R-001-05 to R-001-07, R-004-06, R-004-07, S-200 and E-13 R-207-05 describe, now seen.

Not decided by the approval (the mockup's on-screen choices; he picked none): whether a slope slows a walker (G: none / uphill slowed to half at the limit / and downhill faster), whether a blocked flyer goes around or climbs (C), and the walk speeds against map size (B). Scale is a feeling he wants to see more of before giving numbers (Q-188-01, Q-189-04 stay open). These go to the register when the substrate is planned.

Numbers the mockup measured (pass 6 generator, canvas 16,000, seeds 11 to 13): 33 to 46 percent of the map is walkable; 23 to 37 percent is reachable from the centre start; corner to corner on foot about 8 minutes at speed 40, 4 at 80, 2 at 150.
