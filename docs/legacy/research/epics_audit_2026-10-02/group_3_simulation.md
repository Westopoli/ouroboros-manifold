# Epics audit, group 3: the strategic simulation (E-02 to E-08)

Audited 2026-10-02 against the brief in `BRIEF.md`. Read-only; no bible, register, test or code edits. Epics read in full: E-02, E-03, E-04, E-05, E-06, E-07, E-08. Cross-read for the other side of each contact: E-19 S-159 (R-159-01 to R-159-21), E-17 S-166 and S-115, E-10 S-074 and S-078, E-14 S-125, the hub's "The Player's Toolkit", `economy_design/current_system.md`, `economy_design/README.md`, `economy_design/settlement_generation_options.md`, `economy_design/baseline_and_buildons.md`, the register rows Q-159-04 to Q-159-11 and Q-166-01.

## Where Set L touches these epics

Almost nowhere. The layer, combat and movement decisions live in the tactical engine; none of E-02 to E-08 has a rule that names a layer, a firing count, a formation or a ping. Two real but light contacts are in the table (row 17, unit tier and the "same mix" assumption of the measure; row 8, a region's landscape needing relief for the five layers). Nothing else in these epics meets Set L, and I have not manufactured contact.

## Decisions with no home in these epics

- The region carries a climate, a landform and a specific environmental trouble (E-17 R-166-03, R-159-12). E-02 and E-03 define no such record; E-04 has no template for a trouble.
- A named "supply distance in the simulation" (R-159-15). No epic from E-02 to E-08 defines it; E-03 holds the graph and the edge types it would be read from.
- A region's "situation" handed to a mission: how much resource there is (limited or abundant), the kind of base, and the attacker-or-defender slant (R-159-15, R-159-16). E-02 supplies fields and composites; E-10 S-074 reads capacity for force parity only. No record or story names the richness or base-kind input.
- Which simulation numbers feed the density of self-built settlements, and whether civilization age is one of them (R-166-02, R-166-03).
- Shipments from the civilization whose contents follow its state. R-159-11 decides only that they are blind; "contents depend on the civilization's state" is in an offered build-on (baseline item 9, tied to Q-159-10), not a decision. Nothing in E-02 to E-08 says what the civilization can send, so the home is unclear (E-10 S-074 "what the civilization can spare" is the nearest).
- What a mission leaves in the region: a base occupied or retaken, the original main base lost, where the contested populace ended up, persistent damage and the pace it rebuilds (R-159-14, R-159-10, Q-159-07 sentence).
- Technology choice in a mission against the faction's technology number (R-159-08 against E-06).
- What a pair carries forward between missions and how it meets E-08's one-commander chapter and time jumps (Q-159-07, open, flagged in rows 20 to 23 and 26).
- Doctrines for the self-running economy against faction archetype and civilization character (R-159-18, R-159-21).

## Things I could not judge

- Whether the damage and rebuild half of the Q-159-07 sentence ("Damage persists on short jumps; rebuild speed follows civ economy") is withdrawn together with the economy-persists half. Westley's reply addresses only the economy half, and the register says "the 2026-09-22 answer (the economy persists through time jumps) is withdrawn as fixed". Rows 3 and 23 depend on the answer.
- Whether the region's climate, landform and trouble are meant to be stored in the C sim's region record or derived from the seed by the map generator. R-166-03 says "the simulation's region ... decides climate and landform", which does not say which. This decides whether rows 1, 6 and 7 are real contract and determinism changes or only pointers.
- Whether the mission-level economy is meant to read the faction's tier, economy multiplier or budget at all (rows 12, 14, 15, 16). No decision says either way.
- Whether "events" in R-166-03 are the strategic events of E-04 or in-mission events (row 10).
- Whether the five-layer land maps and the "space maps" of the layer lab both come from regions, and if so what in the region says which (Set L `README.md` section 4 measures space maps only). E-17 and the era packs may answer this; it is outside E-02 to E-08.
- Q-159-10's unpicked options (trading posts at neutral settlements, trade caravans, demands from the civilization, a shared market) would meet E-06's trade edges, E-03 R-023 edge types and "Nobody negotiates" (E-06 Purpose L18) if picked. Not picked, so no finding.
- Noticed in passing, not a Set L or Set E finding: E-03's scale requirement (L131) still times "a 110-region world" while R-021-01 now draws 250 to 400 regions.
