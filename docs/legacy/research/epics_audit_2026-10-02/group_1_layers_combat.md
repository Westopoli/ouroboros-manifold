## 2. Decisions with no home in these epics

From Set L:

- A guard stance or hold-fire order. Westley, 2026-10-01: "Yes a guard stance is fine". Only a register flag (Q-096-01) exists; E-13 has no rule, and the flag does not name holding fire (the S4 ambush play).
- The leash-before-damage switch, and the target-choice switch (nearest, or most damaged for the lab). Neither is a game rule yet; the first has no register row.
- Push-out of a unit found inside solid terrain, and the clamp-and-slide at the map edge (A125, parity P3).
- Occlusion of a cross-layer line of sight or fire by cover or ground.
- Whether a ping sees behind cover.
- Refusing a fight as a counter. Q-008-06 is open; no rule says how a side declines a fight.
- The reactive formation (README line 102: "Ideas recorded, not decided"); no story and none needed yet. Travel lanes have a deferred story (E-17 S-165) but nothing in E-13.

From Set E (units and combat):

- One population cap for workers and soldiers, up to thousands: no population weight per unit in E-11, and no statement in E-11 or E-13 of how workers count against the unit-count budgets.
- Literal workers as units: no type, no behaviour, no movement rule.
- Unit roles needing specific resources: no cost or resource field in the roster.
- Counters between unit families: the category triangle exists (S-081); "family" is undefined.
- Production buildings and defenses (walls, gates, towers) as things on the map: no combatant, blocker or sight rule in E-11, E-12 or E-13; the route grid is static.
- Strongpoints at slopes between height bands: the slope rules exist (E-01 R-001-06, R-004-06); there is no walking unit type, so no slope holds anything in the launch eras.
- Height bands fitted per map as the five layers: E-01 still says the elevation mapping is fixed globally.
- Ground filling the layers below it: no rule connecting terrain height to the open or blocked mask and the ground flag.
- Some ground too steep to cross: no steepness in the slope rule or the ground flag.

## 3. Things I could not judge

- E-01 R-163-01, "units on every layer see it from further away": does "every layer" include the emitter's own layer? The cascade brief says same-layer sight is unchanged. Only Westley or the architect can say which was meant.
- Whether the per-map stretching of the height bands (R-166-06) should change any layer number that is a fixed world distance today: transition times (R-004-01), slope length (R-004-07), the cross-layer vision factors (R-005-02 to R-005-05) and the reach table. The rulings do not say, and the epics assume a layer step has a standard height.
