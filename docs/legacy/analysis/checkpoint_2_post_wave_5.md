## Gate-vs-Next-Fest sequencing conflict (NEW)

Surfaced this checkpoint. Load-bearing for pre-launch planning.

### Verbatim citations

**Pre-Zip Beta Readiness Gate** (per `docs/priority_queue.md` top-of-file Pre-Zip Gate section + CLAUDE.md project workflow): friends-ZIP → $100 Steam Direct → 30-day Steam page → launch. The 30-day Steam page directive implies Steam page goes live 30 days before launch.

**T-D2 Next Fest timing requirement** (verbatim, `docs/legacy/topics/steam_next_fest_timing.md`): "60-120 day Steam-page-pre-fest lead time" — title needs Steam page existing + accumulating wishlist for 60-120 days BEFORE Next Fest slot to enter on momentum. Next Fest cadence Feb / Jun / Oct. October > February > June for niche-strategy per T-D2 audience-availability ranking. 8-16 week gap between Next Fest end + launch is optimal (drift outside this window degrades momentum tail).

### The conflict

If Steam page goes live only 30 days before launch (Pre-Zip Gate), Next Fest entry is impossible — no 60-120 day pre-fest momentum window available. Either:

- Steam page is live longer than Pre-Zip Gate implies (so Next Fest pre-fest window exists),
- Next Fest is skipped (forfeits 5-30× baseline velocity multiplier),
- Launch is timed so Next Fest is after launch (waste of slot — Next Fest is pre-launch event),
- Pre-Zip Gate sequencing is reinterpreted.

Funnel-math impact: skipping Next Fest drops floor scenario from "closes" → "marginal." Floor 270k T-0 pool assumes 5-15k Next Fest contribution at minimum. Without Next Fest, sprint-shape pool collapses to ~200-230k → 80k floor target slips. Midpoint + stretch fail entirely without Next Fest. REPORTED.

### Resolution options

Four options. Bible authority + Westley pick.

**Option A: Reinterpret Pre-Zip Gate as "30-day public-marketing-active page" not "30-day page-existence."**

- Pros: Page can exist + accumulate wishlist 6-18 months pre-launch w/ low-key presence; "marketing-active" phase (heavy trailer + festival + press) starts T-30. Reconciles Next Fest cadence cleanly.
- Cons: Page existence + early wishlist accrual still draws scrutiny — Steam page going live triggers algorithmic + community attention regardless of "marketing-active" framing.
- Implication: Steam page goes live 6-18 months pre-launch, Next Fest slot at T-90 to T-120, Pre-Zip Gate 30-day mark becomes "final marketing sprint" milestone not page-debut.

**Option B: Skip Next Fest entirely, lean on themed festivals + trailers + creator coverage.**

- Pros: Pre-Zip Gate intact. No replanning Pre-Zip sequencing.
- Cons: Forfeits 5-30× baseline weekly velocity multiplier. Funnel-math floor collapses from 270k → ~200-230k T-0. Floor target slips from "closes" → "marginal." Midpoint + stretch fail without Next Fest amplifier.
- Implication: Bible target effectively narrows to lower-bound 80k floor at best, w/ degraded probability.

**Option C: Time launch so Steam page goes live ~120 days pre-launch w/ deliberately quiet first 90 days.**

- Pros: Threads needle — Steam page exists for Next Fest pre-fest momentum window AND Pre-Zip Gate 30-day directive becomes "30-day active launch sprint" (final 30 days).
- Cons: Steam page "quiet first 90 days" is half-fiction; algorithmic + community discovery happens regardless of dev marketing intensity. Requires deliberate suppression of trailer-drop + press during first 90 days, then sudden uptick at T-90 — unusual cadence.
- Implication: Steam page live T-120, Next Fest entry at T-90, active marketing T-30 onward. 90-day quiet-presence window.

**Option D: Reinterpret bible target as launch-month and skip Next Fest, lean on post-launch demo + first-sale uplift.**

- Pros: Post-launch demo (released day-of or T+30) can still hit later Next Fest cycle as post-launch promotional vehicle. Pre-Zip Gate intact.
- Cons: Pre-launch wishlist pool capped at ~120-180k (sprint-shape w/o Next Fest amplifier). First-month unit target collapses to floor at best. Bible 80-200k effectively becomes 60-100k realized. Funnel-math target re-baseline required.
- Implication: First-month target downgraded; "first 6 months" or "year-1" replaces "first month" as monetization milestone.

**Research Lane does not pick.** Bible authority + Westley resolve. Options A and C are sequencing-reframes; B and D are scope-reductions. Surfacing for Design Lane resolution before W6 launch-calendar capture.
