## Vanguard — "The Manta"

### T2
**Prompt:**
Wide flat manta ray futuristic spaceship, blunt front edge, two rear tail prongs with thrusters. Flat angular faces, sharp clean edges, simple geometric shapes. No rockets, no landing gear, no exhaust, minimal detail.

**Look for:** Wide kite from above, thin from side, twin rear prongs.

### T1
**Prompt:**
Wide flat manta ray futuristic spaceship, blocky faceted hull panels, blunt sharp front, crude boxy thrusters on two rear prongs. Flat angular faces, sharp clean edges, simple geometric shapes. No rockets, no landing gear, no exhaust, minimal detail.

**Look for:** Same manta as T2 but rougher, faceted, industrial.

### T3
**Prompt:**
Wide flat manta ray futuristic spaceship, paper-thin edges, smooth continuous form, rear prongs merge into hull. Flat angular faces, sharp clean edges, simple geometric shapes. No rockets, no landing gear, no exhaust, minimal detail.

**Look for:** Same manta but abstracted, thinner, smoother, almost featureless.
