## Phase 1 — Tier 2 (Establishment)

### 1. Vanguard T2
**Prompt:**
Flat angular faces, sharp clean edges, simple geometric shapes. Wide flat manta ray vessel, blunt front edge, two rear tail prongs with thrusters. No rockets, no landing gear, no wings, no exhaust, minimal detail.

**Look for:** Wide kite from above, thin from side, twin rear prongs.

---

### 2. Bombard T2
**Prompt:**
Flat angular faces, sharp clean edges, simple geometric shapes. Long sleek artillery vessel built around one central weapon running its full length. No rockets, no landing gear, no wings, no exhaust, minimal detail.

**Look for:** Longest silhouette in roster, weapon axis dominates.

---

### 3. Bastion T2
**Prompt:**
Flat angular faces, sharp clean edges, simple geometric shapes. Compact heavy armored vessel, wider than long, dense and squat. No rockets, no landing gear, no wings, no exhaust, minimal detail.

**Look for:** Widest and most compact, reads as dense and immovable.

---

### 4. Raider T2
**Prompt:**
Flat angular faces, sharp clean edges, simple geometric shapes. Small narrow dart-shaped fighter, swept back, sleek. No rockets, no landing gear, no wings, no exhaust, minimal detail.

**Look for:** Smallest model, narrow, fast-reading.

---

### 5. Interceptor T2
**Prompt:**
Flat angular faces, sharp clean edges, simple geometric shapes. Small broad flat patrol craft, wider than the dart fighter, stable and watchful. No rockets, no landing gear, no wings, no exhaust, minimal detail.

**Look for:** Wider than Raider, reads as coverage not attack.

---

### 6. Warden T2
**Prompt:**
Flat angular faces, sharp clean edges, simple geometric shapes. Floating support vessel, radial hull with a central dome on top, vertical profile taller than wide. No rockets, no landing gear, no wings, no exhaust, minimal detail.

**Look for:** Radial/vertical, reads as projecting influence outward.
