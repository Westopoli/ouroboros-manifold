2. R-091-02: "the elevation bonus is retired for ground eras" and "no spawn path sets a tier yet". R-091-03: "Given the Settled Reach ... elevation ... affects sensors only"; its test sets only the era name and asserts a 1040 radius, while fog.c:462 returns no bonus when era_kind is 1. Whether the Settled Reach is a ground era decides which text stands.
3. Epic header: "the 9 flags still open are listed at the foot"; the foot lists Q-091-01 and Q-159-09, and Q-091-01 is "closed 2026-10-21" in S-091. R-089-04 and R-090-02 still carry `[Q-...]` tags beside preambles that say the flag is closed.
4. Sight is answered by three paths: `om_unit_acquire_target` (fog.c:687, observer-only; what the E12 R-093-02 test exercises), `om_tac_faction_sees` (unit.c:1170, side union plus pings plus stealth; what the tick uses), and the lab's `seen_by_sight` (union of om_unit_can_see, no pings, no stealth).
