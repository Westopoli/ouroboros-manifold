# Lab fights that end in a draw, and the stage 4 look (2026-11-18)

Westley, 2026-11-18: a lab fight that ends with both sides alive is a bug in the simulation lab to dig into; a draw is either unit behaviour not following a requirement or the placement of the attack-move not following one (DEC-draws-are-defects-2026-11-18). Three Sonnet tracers classified the 43 drawn fights of the Round 5 sweep, each with a re-runnable line of evidence: `2026-11-18-lab-draws/draws_sight_on.md`, `draws_sight_off.md`, `draws_control.md`. This page is the summary and what follows from it.

## Causes found across the 43

Unit behaviour against a requirement:
1. **An attack-move dropped after a lock ends.** After its target dies a unit sometimes loses its destination within 1 to 4 ticks and never gets another, far from its point, with no arrival and no give-up. E-13 R-096-02 says an attack-move is not finished until the unit reaches its point. About 3 percent of such lock ends; 51 of 115 when the unit had fired within 2 ticks, 15 of 268 otherwise. Neighbours resume on the same tick. Seen in at least seven fights. Not root-caused; the comment above `om_tac_chase` in `csrc/unit.c` about a move refused during the pause after a shot fits.
2. **Units that never arrive and never give up.** A unit walking alone stands 16 to 19 wu from its point with the destination set for 10,000 ticks (R-099-04 gives an arrival radius of 22.5 wu; the Round 5 fix applied it to group members only). The give-up of R-099-05 skips a unit with no group. Units pacing 104 to 300 wu from a slot they cannot reach are outside the rule's 75 wu and have no rule.

Placement of the attack-move against a requirement:
3. **Arrived, and beyond the notice distance.** In every `both_same_point` fight the two sides' slot centroids coincide, so the order point is right; but units arrive in their formation (R-095-02), and since Round 5 they notice only within weapon range plus a tenth, so survivors of the first clash stand in slots 276 to 1,410 wu apart. This is the stage 1 stand-off and the csim scene of two 16-unit groups (3 against 3, each unit 20 to 22 wu from its slot, the nearest enemies 465 wu apart). Checked tick by tick later on 2026-11-18 (`~/Projects/om-wt/engine-rules-baseline/r6-eg/`): in stage 1 every lock end was followed by a new target or a resumed walk; each unit's order ends at its formation slot, 6 by arrival and 49 by the give-up of R-099-05 next to the slot, which fired only on units with no target jammed behind their own line; with the give-up switched off the survivors all arrive and still stand 345 wu apart. The cause is where a group attack-mover resumes to after its fight (its slot, as built, or the clicked point): Q-096-03.
4. **An attacker placed on the centre of a split defender** (L-0077: three clusters; the attackers end 345 to 678 wu from each).
5. **An order point inside an obstacle** (L-0043: the middle of its impassable ridge; the layout's flank route is never ordered).

Neither, the rules as written:
6. **The hold after a move away from fire that cannot be answered** (R-087-01): ten sight-on sides, all on the denied stacked-plane layouts, where the low side can never see up.
7. **The sight-off setting never sets weapon reach across layers**, so in 17 of the 20 sight-off draws the sides stand on different layers 1 to 9 wu apart and cannot fire. Older than Round 5 (17 of the 20 also drew in Round 4); not a setting any stage uses for those layouts.
8. Two fights still firing at the cap (L-0043 and L-0054 with sight on).

Cleared: the unit "inside a blocked cell" at the map corner (L-0005 control) walked there by repeated moves away from fire and was shot dead later; the corner is not blocked; the checker misread the canvas edge.

## What Round 6 does about it

`docs/northstar/plans/engine-rules-2026-11-08/round6.md`: an order is no longer lost in the pause after a shot (the cause of cause 1, traced: the route planner answers "no route" while the unit's speed is zero and the destination is cleared); a lone walker arrives and gives up as a group member does; where a group attack-mover resumes to after its fight is asked of Westley (Q-096-03; piece E5, the stage 1 stand-off, is built from his answer); the approved fights become the standing set (DEC-regression-set-2026-11-18), each under its one order per side (DEC-one-click-2026-11-18); the three stage 2 situations convert again; the checker reports an order that ended short.

## The stage 4 look

At his request, once (DEC-look-once-2026-11-18). About twenty frames across the four fights, then seed 10 of each fight recorded at every tick and measured by `2026-11-18-lab-draws/stage4_look.py` and `stage4_look_pushes.py`, written from the rule text and independent of the lab checker. 6,762 shots and 1,014 target picks judged.

Nothing found, in any of the four: a unit inside a box or wall; a shot whose line crosses one; a shot at a target the side does not see; a shot beyond range (the farthest is at 100 percent of range); a shot uphill; a pick beyond the notice distance or past a nearer seen enemy; a unit moving under its own power on the tick it fired or in the nine ticks after. Guards in the vantage fight stay within 18 wu of their posts.

Found, in both trap fights, inside the dense red crowd:
- A unit that had just fired is pushed 4 to 7 wu in one tick by a unit touching it, 109 times (30 in L-0080, 79 in L-0083). By R-097-10 as written this is a push, not a walk.
- Six red pairs on one layer stand overlapped for 5 to 7 s (closest 19.7 and 20.1 wu, the rest 25 to 28). This is against the Invariant that no two same-layer units stand within 30 wu after the collision pass. The checker judges overlap on slopes only. The cause first given here (two halted units pushed apart only when one was displaced earlier in the tick) was wrong: the engine has no such condition. Traced later the same day (`~/Projects/om-wt/engine-rules-baseline/r6-eo/`): each pair is a walker wedged between stationary units that give no ground; it steps into the gap every tick, its neighbours push it opposite ways and the pushes cancel. Open as Q-097-04, with a trial of "a walker that cannot pass waits" running.

One still misled: in the cover fight a blue Bombard's shot looks as if it crosses a box; by the positions it passes through the 100 wu gap between two boxes, 15 wu from the nearer edge.

Not rule breaks, noted for him: both traps go to red against his guess of blue 75 percent (the valley group meets the whole red force alone and is dead by tick 900; the hill groups fire from the hills and come down a few at a time); in the vantage fight red bunches on the ramp for the whole fight; in the cover fight three blue Interceptors stand 360 wu from the fight until something hits them.
