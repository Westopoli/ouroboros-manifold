End rule (scripts/skilllab/arena/run.py lines 10, 46-51, 275-283): the runner stops with "silence" when, tested once a sim second, no unit lost hp for 200 ticks (`last_shot <= tick - SILENCE_TICKS`, 10 s) and no living unit's position differs by more than MOVED_WU = 5 wu from its position 200 ticks earlier. It is a net-displacement test: a unit still holding a destination but jittering inside 5 wu counts as silent; a unit jostling more than 5 wu defeats it (L-0031). Otherwise the fight runs to the cap (CAP_SECONDS, 12000 ticks).
