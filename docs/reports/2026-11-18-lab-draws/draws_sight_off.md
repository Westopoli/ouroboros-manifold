## What `--sight off` sets (run.py `fight`, sight == "off")
1. Targeting: `OM_TP_TARGET_BY_SIGHT` 0 (any enemy within the notice distance, attack range x 1.1, is acquirable, seen or not). No era call, no guard: `hold:A` means A gets no order and stands idle; groups with `hold` get no guard either. Hills are not built (a "vantage" is a label; units take the layer from the layout's column). Slopes and planar blocks are built only when the layout has them.
2. Movement: built-in types fly (movement kind 0) unless the layout has features or `movement = "walk"` (then walkers). Orders: `both_same_point` sends both sides to the midpoint x,y of the tick-0 centroids (no layer in the point); `hold:X` sends the other side to X's centroid; `groups` per layout; `orders_point` overrides.
3. Reach: `set_reach` is called only under stage 4 (0 up, 1 down). Under sight off every type keeps (0, 0): measured with `profile_reach` on all 10 roles in all 20 fights, always `[0, 0]`. By `om_tac_in_reach` (csrc/unit.c:605) a unit then neither acquires nor fires at a unit on another layer. `can_shoot` (tactical_move.c:2937) tests terrain/line only, never layers, so it answers True for every cross-layer pair here.

End rule (run.py:213 and `SILENCE_TICKS`/`MOVED_WU`): "silence (DRAFT-silence-seconds 10: over the last 10 s of sim time no shot by either side and no living unit moved more than MOVED_WU [5 wu], clock from tick 0)", tested once a sim second; both alive = winner "stall". Cap = 600 s = 12,000 ticks. So silence cannot fire while units still close: C7 does not occur in these 20.
