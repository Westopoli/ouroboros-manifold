## The runner's end rule (quoted)

`scripts/skilllab/arena/run.py` docstring and loop: "Fight end: wipe; silence (DRAFT-silence-seconds 10: over the last 10 s of sim time no shot by either side and no living unit moved more than MOVED_WU, clock from tick 0); cap." `MOVED_WU = 5.0`, tested once a sim second (`CHECK_EVERY = 20`); `SILENCE_TICKS = 200`; cap `CAP_SECONDS = 600` = 12,000 ticks. The arena runner has no re-engage (README line 112's re-engage is `scripts/skilllab/fight.py`; `grep -i reengage scripts/skilllab/arena/*.py` is empty). Written requirement on how a lab fight ends: README line 71 and register Q-LAB-03 (Westley 2026-10-13): "have both sets of units attack moved on the same location so they either move and stop or attack each other, unless there's a bug it's not possible for a tie (eventually). If there's one side that's required to hold a choke or a position, we could just make sure that the other side's attack move is close enough within vision/firing range that there will always be a victor, no units will be left standing around." A cap "is a lab defect to trace, never a result".

New class used below: **C9** = the attack-move order was dropped when a lock ended (target died) while the unit was far from its point: no arrival, no give-up (not within 75 wu of the slot), no new order, so it stands for good (R-096-02: "an attack-move is not finished until the unit reaches its point, except that a unit that gives up under R-099-05 ends where it stands").

Stage 4 reach here is 0 up, 1 down for every role (a unit hits its own layer and the one below, never above). Notice distance: range x 1.1 (Vanguard 330, Raider 220, Warden 385, Interceptor 275, Bastion 880, Bombard 1320). Layer-dz is 0 in these worlds, so distances are planar.

### The same-layer / reach facts behind "neither"

Vantage layouts L-0004, L-0005, L-0006, L-0025, L-0028 put one side above the other by the `layer` column. Under stage 4 reach (0 up, 1 down) the low side cannot hit anything above it; "arrived, idle, beyond notice" is then the only outcome available once the low side has walked away from fire it cannot answer. R-087-01 (Westley 2026-11-17: moved away, "it holds where it stops"; a repeat hit moves it again) and R-087-04 (an idle unit does not chase past its notice distance) both read as built. No requirement written on placement for these (README line 103: the meeting point is "the midpoint of the two anchors"; Q-LAB-03 above); the layout files carry only `predict_why` ("position outweighs numbers"). So: no placement requirement is written for the vantage layouts, and the unit behaviour follows the rules; the draw is the product of the pair of rules, not one defect. (Round 4 ended them as wipes because the idle B units chased at sensor range; the Round 5 notice distance removed that.)

- Premise partly off: u38 does reach (4000.0, 4000.0) at tick 7566 (the brief's tick) but it does not stay to the cap. - How it got there: u38 (Interceptor, L0, B) arrived at its slot (2080,1900) at tick 413, then was hit (first hit 490) by A units on L1 that it cannot answer (reach up 0) and walked away from them: R-087-01 move away, 200 wu directly away from the damage, hold where it ends, move again on a repeat hit. Path: (2088,1918) at 413, (2400,2163) at 1000, (3311,2936) by 2000, (3358,3131) by 5000, hit by u13 at 6526 (3587,3459), 6586 (3706,3621), 6646 (3822,3784), then (3937,3947), (3982,4000) at 7339, (3987,4000) at 7399, (3991,4000) 7459, (3996,4000) 7519, (4000,4000) 7566: after the wall each hit moves it only 4 to 5 wu along the wall because the step away from the damage is clamped to the canvas [0, 4000] (`om_move_clamp_axis`, tactical_move.c line 695, an inclusive clamp: (4000,4000) is a legal position).
- Why it does not leave: it holds no order and no destination (dest None since 413), it cannot shoot the A units (layer 1) and so never takes a target; the only thing that ever moves it is a hit, and a hit pushes it away from the shooter, i.e. into the corner.
- Is the corner blocked? No. The layout has no features (`features` key absent), so the lab hands the engine no blocked rectangle; the checker's own `cell_blocked(4000,4000)` computes cell (40,40) of a 40 x 40 grid and returns False for anything outside the grid (inv_check.py line 212). The only "blocked" reading I can reproduce is the engine's navmesh query, which reports every cell blocked in a world with no terrain (`om_navmesh_is_cell_blocked` gives 1 for (39,39), (40,40) and (38,39) on all three layers; `terrain_walkable_at` is False at (4000,4000), (3500,3500), (0,0), (4000,0) alike; `corner.py`). I could not find the checker line that says "inside a blocked cell" for u38; the sweep row for this fight lists no such key (its violations are I13, I13s, I14, I27, I9, I9d, and the I13 rows name u38 as an overlap partner of u28, first at 7686). So: not a blocked cell; the checker is not reading the canvas edge as blocked in `inv_check.py`; the "blocked" reading, if it exists, is the unbuilt-terrain navmesh stub.
- Verdict: the rule working as written (R-087-01 plus the canvas clamp); the draw it belongs to is C7 (the cap fires while A is still hunting B's last three). No engine defect in the corner; an engine defect candidate exists elsewhere (C9 below).

## Engine-defect candidates (not fixed, not root-caused)

1. **No resume after a lock ends (C9).** After the target dies, a unit that held an attack-move sometimes has its destination dropped within 1 to 4 ticks and never gets another one, while far from its slot, outside R-099-05's 75 wu and not arrived. Across the nine fights: 383 lock ends where the target died and the unit still held a destination more than 100 wu from its slot, 10 end with no destination (L-0019 u16 3083, u3 1711, u17/u21 1376; L-0028 u21, u29 856; L-0043 u53 2156; L-0044 u40 2083; L-0005 u13 1727; L-0006 u46 6218), and the survivors named in the table (L-0019 u16 and u24, L-0028 B group, L-0044 u6). Association: of lock ends where the unit fired within 2 ticks of the lock end, 51 of 115 have no destination 8 ticks later; where it did not fire, 15 of 268 (`prem4.py`). That fits the comment in `csrc/unit.c` above `om_tac_chase` ("the route planner refuses a move while the unit cannot move (the pause after its own shot, R-084-04) and drops the destination"), but I did not prove it. Rule: R-096-02.
2. **A unit that left its group never gives up (L-0028 u6, u9, u10).** gid 0, destination held, 16 to 19 wu from it, ~10,000 ticks, pushing. The give-up pass skips units with `group_id == 0` (`om_move_giveup_pass` in tactical_move.c skips a unit whose `group_id` is 0); R-099-05 says "a unit never paces with no end". Because these three move more than 5 wu in every 10-second window, the runner's silence rule cannot fire and the cap ends L-0028.
3. Not a defect: u38 at the corner (a).
