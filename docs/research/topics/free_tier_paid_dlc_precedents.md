## Pure-F2P + cosmetic-only — Path of Exile

PoE (Grinding Gear, 2013 full launch) cleanest cosmetic-only F2P precedent. Base game, skill trees, acts, endgame maps, leagues, story all free. Revenue: cosmetic MTX + premium stash tabs ($2-6 / bundles). Stash tabs = soft-functional-upgrade exception, community-accepted as soft-necessity for endgame. VERIFIED.

Conversion: not disclosed. F2P broad benchmark 2-5%. PoE engaged likely above; ESTIMATED 2-10% of registered convert (per `free_anchor_precedents.md` L94-95). Player base 20M+ registered ~2020 (REPORTED). Annual revenue ESTIMATED $50-100M+ peak (50+ devs, Tencent minority stake 2018, AAA cadence).

QoL pay-gating norm: stash-tab = seed for selling convenience (storage, organization, loadouts) not power. Power-locked stays free; convenience-locked sells. Norm extended into F2P live-service 2014-2024. REPORTED.

Cadence: new league every 3-4 months, paired themed cosmetic-pack drop. Continuous cosmetic revenue, not one-shot. VERIFIED. PoE2 (EA late 2024) separate client, shared cosmetic transactions via linked account. REPORTED.

Niche relevance: cosmetic-only sustains at tens-of-millions scale. At Ouroboros 80-200k, 2-10% × ~$30 lifetime insufficient floor vs paid-DLC $19.99-$24.99/era. Substrate. REPORTED.

## Pure-F2P + hybrid currency — Warframe

Warframe (Digital Extremes, 2013 EA) cosmetic+convenience F2P with player-to-player Platinum trade economy. VERIFIED.

Model: base + Warframes + primary weapons + quests + open worlds + story expansions ("New War," "Whispers in the Walls," "Duviri Paradox") free or earnable. Revenue: Platinum (premium currency purchase or earned via trade), cosmetics (Tennogen + deluxe skins), Prime Access bundles ($39.99-$79.99). Prime Access = time-limited prestige/convenience, not content gate. VERIFIED.

Platinum trade complication: items trade for Platinum → paid content accessible via gameplay + trade. Real-money Platinum optional. Conversion measurement complicated — trade-paying ≠ direct-money. AMBIGUOUS conversion quantification.

TennoCon ecosystem: annual community convention anchors live-service narrative (story reveals, dev panels, Tennocon Relay Twitch reward, TennoLive). Marketing + retention surface, cements tribe identity. REPORTED.

Revenue + base: 50M+ registered across platforms (REPORTED). Annual revenue ESTIMATED $50-150M peak (100+ devs, 10+ year cadence).

Live-service framing: closer to "Prime-Access-as-paid-cadence" than cosmetic-only. Prime Access every ~3 months = nearest analog to "DLC-per-era cadence" inside free-anchor, but Prime Access also earnable in-game (no permanent gate); Ouroboros' paid era = permanent gate. AMBIGUOUS analog strength.

## Soft-mandatory subscription — Albion Online

Albion (Sandbox Interactive, B2P July 2017 → F2P April 2019) niche-tribe subscription precedent. Closer to Ouroboros niche-strategy audience than PoE / Warframe (both action-genre). REPORTED.

Model post-2019: base free. Revenue: Premium subscription ($9.99/mo, multi-month discount), Gold (premium currency, real-money or silver→gold conversion), cosmetics. VERIFIED.

Soft-mandatory framing: Premium yield bonuses (silver, fame, learning points) significant — competitive players compelled to maintain. Harder paywall than PoE/Warframe. VERIFIED.

Niche-tribe conversion: ESTIMATED 20-35% of active subscribe given competitive necessity (above broad F2P 2-10%). Niche receptivity effect: hardcore sandbox-MMO (player-driven economy, full-loot PvP, territorial control) subscribes more reliably than broad casual (EVE pattern). Most actionable signal for Ouroboros niche-tribe scale.

B2P → F2P transition April 2019: Sandbox discussed qualitatively (growth, discovery), no before/after revenue. One-way precedent: established hardcore absorbed without churn collapse. Reverse (F2P→B2P) harder, not surveyed. AMBIGUOUS revenue.

Player base 4-6M registered (REPORTED). Annual revenue ESTIMATED $10-30M.

Substrate: Albion convert-rate (20-35%) most actionable benchmark if Ouroboros considers subscription layer over Roman-free. If pure DLC-attach (no sub), less directly relevant.

## Free-update cadence inside paid model — Stellaris + CK3

Two Paradox precedents for free content inside otherwise-paid model.

Stellaris flavor-pack tier ($7.99-$9.99) as low-friction add-on: species packs $7.99, story packs $9.99 sit at deliberately low-friction price. Per `dlc_pricing_ladders.md` L88-92, lower tier is "supporting the devs" gesture territory. Estimated attach 15-25% per pack with decay (per `ck3_dlc_ladder.md` L48). Substrate: low-friction tier inside paid-DLC ladder = precedent for cosmetic-tier-equivalent inside Ouroboros' 7-era ladder. Not free, but price low enough = effectively impulse. REPORTED.

CK3 free updates (ruler designer, portrait packs, Chapter free updates) = most-developed free-content-inside-paid-base pattern surveyed. REPORTED.

- Ruler designer free update: cosmetic character customization shipped free, not paid DLC. Reduced new-player friction; identity-anchor feature.
- Portrait packs paired with paid expansions: each major paid expansion ships free portrait-pack update alongside — "you got something even if you didn't buy DLC" gesture.
- Chapter Pass free updates: mechanics tweaks, UI, bug fixes bundled with cosmetic touch-ups ship alongside paid drops. Sustains free-player engagement between paid drops.

Substrate: CK3 pattern shows paid-base sustains free-update cadence without cannibalizing paid-DLC attach. Free updates are NOT a free tier — they are free improvements to paid base. Ouroboros Roman-free is structurally different (permanent free tier of base content, not free updates to paid-purchased base). CK3 model = precedent for *Roman-paid-base + free updates*, NOT for *Roman-free permanent tier + paid expansion eras*. Distinction matters for L274 conflict. REPORTED.

## Pure-F2P vs paid-base contrast — PoE vs Diablo IV

Cleanest in-genre (ARPG) contrast pair for "F2P + cosmetic" vs "paid-base + season pass."

PoE model: F2P + cosmetic-only (above). 20M+ registered. "Real ARPG" perception inside endgame-grinder niche. VERIFIED.

Diablo IV model: $69.99 paid-base launch 2023. Vessel of Hatred expansion 2024 $39.99. Season pass (free + premium track) for cosmetics and battle-pass progression. Activision-Blizzard explicitly rejected Diablo Immortal F2P-mobile monetization for D4 console/PC, framing D4 as premium AAA. REPORTED.

Audience-acquisition multiplier (free-tier signal):
- PoE: low discovery friction; F2P-tag in Steam search attracts trial. ESTIMATED 5-10× audience-acquisition vs equivalent paid-base ARPG (REPORTED via F2P storefront positioning signal).
- D4: high price = high paid-base trust-signal. AAA Blizzard weight + $69.99 sticker = quality assertion. Serious-buyer self-selection. REPORTED.

Friction conversion (free → paid attach %):
- PoE: cosmetic conversion ESTIMATED 2-10% of registered ever spend. Lifetime per-paying spend ESTIMATED $20-80.
- D4: ~100% of owners paid (paid-base required). Season-pass attach ESTIMATED 30-60% per season among active. AMBIGUOUS retention-adjusted.

Tribe-tolerance for F2P-feel:
- ARPG endgame tribe: high F2P-tolerance. PoE = tribe hero; F2P framing reinforces "real game" (Blizzard-haters valorize no-paywall ethos).
- AAA-buyer tribe: low F2P-tolerance. D4 audience reads paid-base as quality signal; F2P framing for premium ARPG = Diablo Immortal mobile-feel (negative).

Implication for Ouroboros niche-strategy audience: strategy niche (CK3, Stellaris, RimWorld, Wildermyth) closer to AAA-buyer perception structure than ARPG-endgame tribe. Paid-base = trust-signal norm. F2P framing in strategy reads as either (a) phone-game-feel (negative) or (b) competitive-PvP-MMO (Albion/EVE, niche-tribe-acceptable but different tribe). Niche-strategy F2P-tolerance LOW absent explicit competitive-PvP-MMO framing. AMBIGUOUS whether Roman-free reads as "free demo" (acceptable) vs "F2P phone-game" (negative) absent additional framing. REPORTED.

## Digital TCG hybrid — MTG Arena + Hearthstone

Two TCG precedents for free-anchor + paid-card hybrid.

**MTG Arena** (WotC, 2018-2019). F2P base. Revenue: booster packs (gem currency, real-money), draft entry fees (gems), cosmetics (styles, avatars, sleeves, pets). Wildcards = scarcity-rare currency via pack opening, crafts specific cards. VERIFIED. Acquisition: free entry attracted lapsed-paper-MTG + new digital-TCG (REPORTED). Conversion not disclosed (Hasbro/WotC aggregate only; per-player ARPU AMBIGUOUS). Tribe-tolerance: paper MTG players high WTP for digital cards; F2P accepted because comparison-anchor = paper-card prices, worse. Cadence: new sets every 3 months (paper-MTG schedule); each set = paid drop + optional bundle pre-purchase.

**Hearthstone** (Blizzard, 2014). F2P base. Revenue: booster packs (gold earned or gems purchased), pre-order bundles for expansions, Tavern Pass battle-pass, Adventures single-player. VERIFIED. Acquisition: F2P + Blizzard brand → massive discovery. Peak ESTIMATED 70-100M registered. Conversion not disclosed; industry estimates 5-15% of active (ESTIMATED). Tribe-tolerance: TCG tribe accepts F2P because card-game economy intrinsically grind-or-pay. Pre-order bundle norm: each expansion ships $49.99-$79.99 bundle (cosmetic + booster bulk) = primary revenue spike per cycle.

Substrate: TCG hybrid maps poorly to strategy DLC-per-era. TCG paid content = consumable (random cards); Ouroboros paid content = permanent (era unlock). TCG-tribe F2P-tolerance does NOT transfer to strategy-tribe. Lifted signal: F2P-feel-acceptance is genre-specific. REPORTED.

## Counter-precedent — RimWorld + CK3 paid-base + paid-DLC norm

Per kickoff directive: RimWorld + CK3 NOT free-base. Paid-base + paid-DLC = niche-strategy norm. Free-anchor precedents above sit paradigmatically outside the niche-strategy paid-base culture Ouroboros' bible-locked $25-35 targets.

- **RimWorld:** $34.99 paid base, 4 expansions @ $24.99 (Royalty, Ideology, Biotech, Anomaly). No free tier, no subscription, no Game Pass. Direct-purchase only. 5-10M owners, ~$300M+ lifetime. Per `rimworld_dlc_ladder.md`. VERIFIED model, ESTIMATED revenue.
- **CK3:** $49.99 paid base, multi-tier DLC ($6.99 / $14.99 / $29.99 per `dlc_pricing_ladders.md` L44). Game Pass exposure but direct-purchase remains primary. 4M+ units. Per `ck3_dlc_ladder.md`. REPORTED.
- **Wildermyth:** $24.99 paid base, Omenroad DLC $9.99. No free tier. 500k-1M owners ESTIMATED. Per `wildermyth_omenroad.md`. REPORTED.

Niche-strategy paid-base culture: $25-40 = trust-signal norm. Paid base communicates (a) quality investment, (b) dev commitment, (c) self-selecting engaged buyer, (d) curated content (no grind-or-pay). Free-base inside niche-strategy tribe absent explicit framing (Albion-style competitive PvP MMO) risks reading low-effort / phone-game / grind-or-pay (negative). Pattern source: Steam review themes + niche-strategy tribe identity markers per `primary_tribe_psychographic_profile.md`. REPORTED.

Implication: Roman-free + paid-DLC-per-era structurally novel within niche-strategy precedent set. No surveyed precedent instantiates "free permanent base (Roman) + paid expansion per era (6 eras) inside niche-strategy tribe." Closest analogs:

- **CK3 + Game Pass:** paid base, Game Pass subscribers get base + some DLC included. CK3 retains direct-purchase revenue; Game Pass did not cannibalize. Conversion gap not disclosed; ESTIMATED Game Pass → DLC at 50-70% of direct-purchase attach rate.
- **Albion B2P→F2P:** established hardcore audience absorbed transition. One-way precedent. REPORTED.
- **PoE/Warframe pure F2P:** different audience tribe (action-genre). VERIFIED.

Roman-free permanent-base = novel shape. Free-tier conversion math has no direct precedent; must triangulate from adjacent patterns. AMBIGUOUS direct match.

## Cross-axes — substrate

**Axis 1: Free-tier audience-acquisition multiplier vs paid-base trust-signal.** Free-tier acquisition multiplier ESTIMATED 5-10× discovery vs equivalent paid-base in same genre (PoE vs paid-base ARPGs, Hearthstone vs paid TCGs); strategy-genre multiplier not surveyed, AMBIGUOUS generalization to strategy tribe. Paid-base trust-signal: niche-strategy tribe weights paid base as quality assertion; paid buyer self-selects for engagement; conversion to additional DLC higher per-buyer than free-tier discovery-to-DLC. REPORTED. Trade-off: free-tier = discovery volume × low conversion %; paid-base = conversion % × lower volume. Total revenue = volume × conversion × ARPU.

**Axis 2: Friction conversion (free → paid attach %).** Broad F2P 2-5% of registered (PoE/Hearthstone tier, ESTIMATED). Engaged niche F2P 5-15% of active (Hearthstone-active tier, ESTIMATED). Niche-tribe subscription 20-35% of active (Albion-style, ESTIMATED). Game Pass → DLC ESTIMATED 50-70% of direct-purchase attach rate (closest paid-base-adjacent free-tier analog). Roman-free → paid era DLC: no direct precedent; triangulation 10-30% of active Roman-free → first paid era, steep decay across subsequent eras. AMBIGUOUS.

**Axis 3: Tribe-tolerance for F2P-feel in strategy.** ARPG tribe HIGH (PoE valorized, VERIFIED). TCG tribe HIGH (Hearthstone/MTG Arena culturally accepted, VERIFIED). MMO sandbox-PvP tribe HIGH with subscription overlay (Albion/EVE pattern, VERIFIED). Niche-strategy tribe (CK3/Stellaris/RimWorld/Wildermyth audience) LOW — paid-base $25-40 = trust-signal norm; F2P framing absent niche-MMO competitive overlay risks phone-game/grind-or-pay perception. REPORTED. Source: tribe Steam review themes + identity markers per `primary_tribe_psychographic_profile.md` + `pricing_elasticity_niche.md`.

**Axis 4: What "Roman-free baseline + 7-era paid DLC" implies for free-tier audience expectations vs niche-strategy paid-base norms.**

Free-tier expectations (precedent-set):
- Free base ≈ complete game (PoE). Paid = cosmetic / convenience.
- Free base ≈ trial / demo (Game Pass, B2P→F2P transition). Paid = main product.
- Free base ≈ grind-or-pay treadmill (Hearthstone). Paid = accelerates.

Niche-strategy paid-base norms (precedent-set):
- Paid base ≈ self-contained game, replayable indefinitely (RimWorld, CK3).
- Paid DLC ≈ optional depth accreting over years; no DLC required to keep playing.
- Paid base + paid DLC ≈ stable trust contract; $25-40 buyer expects no further mandatory spend.

Substrate enumeration (not picking):
- Roman-free as "complete game" (PoE pattern): audience expects cosmetic-only paid. Conflicts with 7-era paid expansion (power-locked content gate violates cosmetic-only F2P norm).
- Roman-free as "trial / demo" (Game Pass pattern): audience expects paid = main product. Aligns with 7-era paid expansion. But "demo" framing conflicts L274 "Free to play" tagline — demos ≠ F2P in marketing tag norm.
- Roman-free as "grind-or-pay treadmill" (Hearthstone pattern): audience expects paid accelerates. Misaligns niche-strategy stable-trust-contract expectation. Tribe reads grind-or-pay negatively.
- Roman-free as novel "free permanent base + paid permanent expansions": no precedent governs audience expectations. Reading depends on framing (trial-demo vs F2P vs free-base-with-paid-expansions). Framing choice = Design Lane's.

Confidence: REPORTED per pattern; AMBIGUOUS which pattern audience maps to absent framing tests.
