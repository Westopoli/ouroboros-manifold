## Deadlock ruling (Architect, 2026-10-30, DEC-sweep-drift-warn-2026-10-30)

The first hand-run sweep could not run: the gate's status stage rejects on `STATUS sweep DRIFT` (a sweep is due), and the sweep itself runs through the gate, so nothing could ever clear the drift. Rule: in `scripts/specfactory/merge_gate.sh` the status stage treats `STATUS sweep DRIFT` as a WARN under every tier and rejects on every other DRIFT as before; what blocks a landing after a bad sweep is the last `sweeps.md` row being REJECT (gate.md: "a landing after a red sweep waits for the bisect"), which the status stage checks as `GATE status REJECT sweep red <n> at <sha>`. This story owns that change (`merge_gate.sh` lines 427 to 433 on main) and `tests/specfactory/test_sweep_timer.py` gains two tests for it: a fixture whose `status_check.py` stub prints `STATUS sweep DRIFT ...` gates to `GATE status WARN`; a fixture whose last `sweeps.md` row is REJECT gates to `GATE status REJECT sweep red`.
