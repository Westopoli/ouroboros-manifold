# H-091: lab-stage5-facing-arcs-bands

"Units have fronts; fire cones matter; a height band is a slower climb and a longer sight." (lab staircase, "Stage 5: facing, arcs and height bands")

Flag client; LAB stage 5 (plan `docs/northstar/story_reviews/westley/lab-staircase.md`; overnight plan `docs/northstar/plans/lab-stage5-2026-11-26/plan.md`). Channel: lab. Built on `origin/main` caa79b7ff on branch `s/lab-stage5`; not landed. Lab stage 4 is not Seen; the assumptions taken are in the plan, section 5.

## What lab stage 5 adds (and nothing else)

1. The firing arc in the sim (E-13 R-100-04), read from `OM_TP_FIRING_ARC_DEG`.
2. A lab switch `--facing on` beside `--sight`: facing applied at spawn, the arc set for the fight, three new things in the fight file (the arc, `climbing`, `hits`).
3. Lab measures: hits by front, side and rear; the delay before a flanked unit answers; the climb's slowdown; a check that no shot leaves outside the arc.
4. Probe scenes and a drafted objective ring.
5. Replay drawing: arc wedges, hit marks, a climb bar, a band mark per unit.

With `--facing off` (the default) every fight file of lab stages 1 to 4 is byte-identical to main.

## Drafts (Westley rules)

DRAFT-stage5-arc-deg 90 (one width for every type tonight; his per-category widths are not built). Sweep values 360, 180, 120, 90, 60. Hit classes by the angle between the victim's facing and the direction to the attacker: front up to DRAFT-stage5-front-deg 45, rear from DRAFT-stage5-rear-deg 135, side between. DRAFT-stage5-climb-compare 2.0 (the second climb comparison; the standard run keeps the engine's 1.1). Hit mark colours: front white, side orange, rear red. Probe block: 8 Vanguards, 2 ranks of 4, DRAFT 40 wu apart. DRAFT-stage5-pinned-delay 100 ticks; DRAFT-stage5-pinned-rear-ticks 50. Replay drawing drafts, named in `scenes/proto/fight_replay/replay_arcs.gd`: the wedge's alpha and segment count, the hit ring's size and width, the climb bar's size, the two band colours.

## Rules and tests

### E1 the firing arc (csim, `tests/csim/test_E13_R100_04_firing_arc.py`; engine `csrc/unit.c` fire pass)

- R-091-01 (E-13 R-100-04) Given `OM_TP_FIRING_ARC_DEG` 90 and a unit holding a living target in range whose direction is more than 45 degrees from the unit's facing, When the tick runs, Then the unit does not fire that tick, keeps the same target, and its facing moves toward the target by no more than its category's turn rate (R-100-02: Line 4.0 rad/s, 0.2 rad a tick at 20 ticks a second); on the first tick the target is within 45 degrees it may fire. Checked every tick.
- R-091-02 Given the arc at 360 (the default), Then every existing golden, hash and fight file is unchanged: the csim goldens, `tests/skilllab/golden_hashes.json` and the stage 3 and 4 pinned fight files pass untouched.
- R-091-03 Given a unit with its target dead ahead (0 degrees) and the arc at 90, Then its first shot comes on the same tick as with the arc at 360; given the target at 180 degrees (directly behind), its first shot comes later by at least the ticks its turn rate needs to bring the target inside the arc (135 degrees at 0.2 rad a tick: 12 ticks) and by no more than that plus 2.
- R-091-04 The arc changes nothing but the shot: with the arc at 90, acquisition (who targets whom and when), the lock, return-fire targeting and every unit's orders and destinations are the same, tick for tick, as with 360 until the first tick a shot is withheld.
- R-091-05 A save taken mid-fight with the arc at 90 and loaded gives the same later frames as the run that was not saved; two runs with the same seed give byte-identical traces.
- R-091-06 The arc is the full angle centred on the facing: a target at exactly half the arc is inside; values are taken as the parameter is set (10 to 360).

### Added 2026-11-27 after the first measurements (`tests/skilllab/arena/test_stage5_pinned.py`)

The first runs showed that a lone attack from the side or rear barely registers: the holders turn to their target while it approaches, so only the first 4 ticks of hits land on a side or rear. A flank that lasts needs the holders already locked on an enemy to their front.

- R-091-23 A seventh probe scene `pinned_flank` exists beside the others, marked DRAFT and "Architect-made probe, not a catalogue layout": the same holding block of 8 Vanguards facing east; a front group of 8 Vanguards to the east and a rear group of 8 Vanguards to the west, farther away, so that the rear group's first hit comes at least DRAFT-stage5-pinned-delay 100 ticks after the front group's first hit; order rule R2 (the holder holds, every attacker attack-moves onto the holder's centre of mass). It passes the preflight with `--facing on` and runs to a wipe at seed 1. With `--arc-deg 90` the holders receive rear-class hits on at least DRAFT-stage5-pinned-rear-ticks 50 distinct ticks (the lock keeps them facing their front target, R-087-03 as written).
  Proof: tests/skilllab/arena/test_stage5_pinned.py::test_R_091_23_the_rear_groups_first_hit_comes_at_least_100_ticks_after_the_front_groups | scenario | verified 33470ba37 2026-11-27
- R-091-24 With `--facing on` the runner takes `--slope-up-div V`: it sets `OM_TP_SLOPE_UP_DIV` to V for that fight and writes V in `stage5.slope_up_div`; without the flag the engine's own value (1.1) is used and written. With facing off the flag is refused with a usage error. A unit climbing with V 2.0 has a slope-to-flat speed ratio within 0.05 of 0.5; with the default, within 0.05 of 0.909.
