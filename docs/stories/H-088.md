## What stage 4 adds (and nothing else)

- **Sight is on.** `run.py --sight on` is the stage 4 rule set (DEC-stage4-sight-bundle-2026-11-05): targeting by sight (R-093-02: a unit acquires, keeps and fires only at what its side sees), a ground era with walking units, reach 0 up 1 down (DEC-stage4-vantage-reach-2026-11-05, DRAFT-stage4-reach-down 1: high ground sees down, low ground never sees up, R-005-09), and the holder of `hold:A` or `hold:B` on guard (R-096-08, DEC-stage4-guard-holder-2026-11-05). `--sight off` is stages 1 to 3 unchanged.
- **Cover blocks sight, never hits.** R-007-01 and R-007-04: no cover call is made; the stage 3 boxes, walls and buildings are blocked route cells and already hide units once sight is on (DEC-cover-blocks-sight-not-hits-2026-11-05). Cover reduces nothing about damage.
- **The fight file records sight.** Per unit `seen` (the sorted living enemy ids it sees), `sights` (sensor radius per role), `result.first_seen` (the first sampled sim second each side sees any enemy), `orders.guard`; optional additive keys on `om-fight-2`.
- **The replay draws sight.** A faint disc per role per side (per unit on V), enemies no unit of the other side sees drawn hollow, the HUD counts what each side sees and prints who saw first.
- **The page shows the fog control.** The control strip is the same fight with sight off (DEC-stage4-control-sight-off-2026-11-05); every row prints `first seen A <tick|never>  B <tick|never>`.
- **Layouts (DEC-stage4-layouts-2026-11-05, his 2026-11-01 words):** vantage L-0061, traps L-0080 and L-0083, cover L-0039 as drawn. Four fights, twenty seeds each (DRAFT-seeds-per-layout), the percentage a heuristic target and unscored. Staging is out.
