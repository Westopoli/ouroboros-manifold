# H-057: engage-on-sight

"Whenever a unit sees an enemy unit and it is under an attack move or standing still, it attacks the unit it sees in its vision."

Flag sim; tool Workflow; scene: the stage 1 fight replay (L-0054). Ready 2026-11-01 (DEC-engage-on-sight-2026-11-01). Westley's words, 2026-11-01, on the L-0054 stall; he has decided this before and A199 (2026-10-03, "hold formation until shooting") is replaced by it.

## Why

Stage 1 fight L-0054 (128 vs 128 Vanguards, both sides attack-moved to the same point, seed 1) ends as a STALL at tick 8640 with 38 survivors a side standing 305 to 453 wu apart. Reproduced on the engine 2026-11-01: every survivor still holds its attack-move, has arrived at its formation slot (R-095-02 offsets, never updated), and `unit.c:2182` acquires only within `attack_range` (300); the A199 comment above it says "no pursuit on sight". Nobody shoots, nobody walks the last five wu. Dead bodies do not block (the collision grid files only `hp > 0` units; units sent through piles of bodies moved as far as units on clear ground). A fresh attack-move to the survivors restarted the fight at once. Rule gap, not a bug: the rules said where to stand and when to shoot, nothing said what an arrived attack-mover does with an enemy in sight just outside range.

## Rule (E-11 R-087-04, rewritten in this story's records)

An idle unit (no order, or an attack-move that has arrived) or an attack-moving unit with no target, when an enemy its side can see (E-12 R-093-02; reach across layers R-008-04) is within its sensor radius, acquires the nearest such enemy at its cohort turn (R-087-08), anchors where it stands (R-087-05), and walks toward it until the enemy is within attack range, then halts and fires (R-096-02). An attack-mover leaves its slot and route to do so and resumes toward its point or slot after the engagement (R-096-02, the existing `om_tac_release` path). An idle unit holds where the engagement ends (R-087-06). The 200 wu leash (R-087-05, R-096-03) bounds the walk: past it the target is dropped and the unit resumes or holds. A plain-moving unit never acquires (R-096-01). Attack-target (R-096-04), guard (R-096-08) and non-combat units (R-087-07) are unchanged. Stage 1 runs with sight off (`OM_TP_TARGET_BY_SIGHT` 0): there, "sees" means within sensor radius.

Known consequence, for Westley to veto by a word: an enemy force under the `defend` doctrine (E-14 R-120-02, "auto-engages without pursuit") now pursues within the 200 wu leash like every other standing unit. Not changed in this story; the E-14 text gains that sentence in this story's records.

## Change (`csrc/unit.c` only; no header, ABI, save or pipe change)

- At the cohort acquisition (`unit.c:2176-2190`): the search radius for an idle or attack-moving unit becomes the type's `sensor_radius` (profile; never below `attack_range`), with the sight filter `om_tac_may_act_on` as today; the A199 comment is replaced by this story's. The switch-0 baseline arm (`OM_TP_PURSUE_BY_STEERING` 0) is untouched.
- A unit with a target beyond attack range steps toward it, the way the return-fire pursuit (`unit.c` last_attacker branch, `om_command_move` to the attacker) and attack-target (R-096-04) already walk: the builder reuses that path, with the anchor set at acquisition so the leash applies. An attack-move group member that acquires leaves its group for the engagement exactly as the return-fire redirect does (`am_gid` cleared, `order_x/y` kept as the point to resume to), so `om_tac_release` brings it back.
- An idle unit (order NONE) that acquires beyond range walks under the same leash and, when the target dies or the leash breaks, drops to idle where it stands (`om_tac_drop_orders` path; R-087-06 wording).
- Determinism: no new source of order; the cohort stagger and nearest-with-tie-break already exist.

## Tests (`tests/csim/test_E11_S087_engage_on_sight.py`, new; a different Sonnet writes them first, red on main)

1. Two idle Vanguards, hostile, 400 wu apart (sensor 700, range 300), sight off: within 200 ticks both hold a target; within 600 ticks one has taken damage. Control: 900 wu apart, no target and no damage after 600 ticks.
2. A lone attack-mover marching to a point 1500 wu away passes an enemy standing 400 wu off its path: it acquires within 200 ticks, the enemy dies (give it low hp), and the unit then arrives at its point (R-096-02 resume) within the ticks the plain march would take plus 400.
3. A group of 8 attack-movers (the slot-and-shape detector of `tests/csim/test_PP_attack_move_no_sight_pursuit.py` reused as a helper, not edited): the members that see a lone enemy 400 wu off the line engage it; after it dies every member reaches its slot.
4. Leash: an idle Vanguard sees an enemy at 400 wu that walks away at the same speed; the pursuer drops the target once it is 200 wu from its anchor and stops (holds). Control: the enemy standing still is reached and shot.
5. Plain-move control: the same unit on a plain move past the 400 wu enemy acquires nothing and arrives (R-096-01).
6. The L-0054 shape in miniature: two 16-unit blobs attack-moved to the same point with the front rows removed after first contact (kill them by script at tick 900): the fight ends by elimination, not by a standoff, within 6000 ticks.
