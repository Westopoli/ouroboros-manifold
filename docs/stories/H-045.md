## Why

H-002 (trailer-schema `status_check.py`, 7812e5bd) rewrote the status check to four trailer checks (`land_trailer`, `tested_without_land`, `seen_without_verdict`, `seen_without_commit`) and dropped the sweep-due check without saying so. `docs/method/gate.md:21` and `docs/method/clerk.md:15` (step 8) still state the rule, and the clerk's step 8 reads `STATUS sweep WARN|DRIFT` to start a sweep; today that line is never printed, so no landing clerk has started a sweep since 2026-10-30 and only the 23:30 timer sweeps. The eighteen tests `tests/specfactory/test_gate_tiers.py::test_4_*` are the rule's spec and have been red since; they stay the spec (Westley 2026-11-01: "repair now").

## Change

`scripts/specfactory/status_check.py` gains the sweep check back, in a new function `check_sweep_due(repo, base)` called from the existing main after the four trailer checks, printing exactly one line `STATUS sweep OK|WARN|DRIFT <reason>`:
- Anchor: the last `PASS` row of `docs/northstar/plans/push/sweeps.md`, or the `Base` row when no PASS row exists; a `REJECT` row is never the anchor and never a DRIFT (test_4_a_red_sweep_is_never_a_drift, test_4_the_last_pass_or_base_row_wins...).
- Landings since the anchor: first-parent `Land ` merge commits on main after the anchor's sha (test_4_only_first_parent_land_merges_count_as_landings).
- `WARN` when landings exceed DRAFT-slow-tier-batch 3 or the anchor is older than DRAFT-slow-tier-hours 2 h with at least one landing since; `DRIFT` at DRAFT-slow-tier-drift-mult 2 times either; `OK` otherwise; a pass row with no landings since it is quiet at any age (test_4_a_pass_row_with_no_landings_since_it_is_quiet_at_any_age). Constants are the three drafts the tests derive from (`test_gate_tiers.py:75`).
- `merge_gate.sh` is not changed: its red-row reject (lines 433 to 446) stays the hard stop; the clerk card's step 8 is the reader of the new line.
