## What stage 2 adds (and nothing else)

- **All six roles** (Raider, Interceptor, Vanguard, Warden, Bombard, Bastion) in one layout, per-unit `range` from the engine's roster (the layout's `range` 0 means the role's own; a non-zero value is still rejected until Westley rules on range overrides).
- **Arc blocks** (DRAFT-arc-shape, from his 2026-11-01 rule "lined up flush ... in an arc, that maximise the number of units concurrently firing"): a side's block may be `shape = "arc"` with `centre`, `radius`, `start_deg`, `end_deg`, units spaced at the block pitch along the arc, each facing the arc's centre. Rectangular blocks stay. The situations template gains the arc columns; the converter and `check_layout` (R1 to R5) accept both.
- **Win rate over seeds.** The runner gains `--seeds N` (DRAFT-seeds-per-layout 20, Westley's rule: predictions are probabilities); one `fight.json` per seed, plus `<id>.rate.json`: `{wins_a, wins_b, stalls, seeds, rate_a, predict_rate, agree}`. The layout's `predict` becomes a probability for side A in `predict_rate` (0 to 1; his numbers 0.98, 0.95, 0.80, 0.75, 0.60); `predict` A/B/draw stays for v1 layouts. Agree when `|rate_a - predict_rate| <= DRAFT-rate-band 0.10` (audit item 1, heuristics.md). Any stall is listed by seed and brought to him (DEC-lab-amove-rule-2026-11-01).
- **Page:** one row per layout with the rate, his predicted rate, agree, the stall count, a per-role survivors table (mean over seeds), and the frame strip of the median-length seed; a click on a seed number opens that seed's strip.
- **Replay:** range rings per unit (already drawn), a `target` line per unit per frame, a "first shot" marker on the frame where the first hp drop happens, HUD line with the seed and the rate.
- Sight stays off (`OM_TP_TARGET_BY_SIGHT` 0). Field stays `small_flat`, no features. Orders per the two rules (H-052).

## Audit against the rules on file (2026-11-01, Westley: "Have you audited the stage 2 spec for more gaps like the one I pointed out")

Gaps found and how the story closes them; the two marked **his** need his word before the story is Ready.
1. **What a seed varies (ruled 2026-11-01: no jitter).** Westley: variance comes from the variables as they are added; the percentages are heuristics of where the lines cross and a configuration off by more than 5 to 10 points is the thing flagged (`docs/northstar/research/layer_dynamics/heuristics.md`). DRAFT-rate-band becomes 10 points. Original question: Positions come from the layout; only the stochastic rolls change between seeds, so twenty seeds of one layout may give rates of exactly 0 or 1 and never meet a 0.75 or 0.60 prediction. Options: (a) each seed also jitters every unit's position inside its block by DRAFT-seed-jitter up to half the pitch, deterministic from the seed; (b) no jitter, and a rate is read as near 0, near 1, or in between, with the band rule only for in-between predictions. No `--jitter`: not built, not tested (ruled).
2. **Both sides wiped on the same tick.** His rule: one force wins. The spec no longer calls it a draw: `result.winner` is `"none"`, `end` is `"wipe"`, the row is flagged like a stall and brought to him (rare by construction).
3. **Abilities.** The roster carries abilities (`engine.disable_abilities`); stage 2 runs with abilities off (DRAFT-abilities-off-until-stage 6) so the role comparison is weapons, speed and range only; the page says so.
4. **Facing is drawn, not simulated** until stage 5 binds `om_unit_set_facing`; an arc "facing the centre" is a picture. The page and the README rows say "facing not simulated" until stage 5.
5. **"The same location" (ruled 2026-11-01).** Midpoint is fine as the default as long as both sides go to the exact same spot; he describes the spot per layout when authoring (`Orders point:` in the template). Original question: DRAFT-same-point is the midpoint of the two sides' tick-0 centroids; a layout may name the point instead (`orders_point` in the layout and an `Orders point:` line in the template). Whether the midpoint is ever acceptable is his.
6. **Does the arc do its job?** The page shows, per side, the number of units whose range reaches the enemy's first-contact point (`firing_capable` in the rate file, computed from the first frame with an hp drop); that is "maximise the number of units concurrently firing" as a number he can check against the picture.
7. **Pre-scale label** on every stage 2 page and strip while the tuning session (H-023) is owed.
8. **Silence rule carried over** unchanged from H-052 (10 s, stalls flagged); a stall at seed k lists k.
9. **Headcounts** 8 to 256 per side independently, his rule, unchanged; the arc shape must fit its count at the pitch or the converter rejects the layout with the needed radius in the message.

## Interfaces (ruled 2026-11-01 night for the test writer; every name here is the name in the code)

1. **`rate.py`** is a library, no CLI: `rate(fights, predict_rate, band=0.10) -> dict` where `fights` is the list of fight dicts of one layout (one per seed) and the result is the rate file's content; `run.py --seeds N` calls it and writes `<id>.rate.json` beside the fight files. Keys: `layout_id`, `seeds` (the list of seed numbers run), `wins_a`, `wins_b`, `stalls`, `stall_seeds` (list of seed numbers whose `result.winner` is `"stall"`), `none_seeds` (same for `"none"`, the same-tick wipe), `rate_a` = `wins_a / len(seeds)` (stalls and none count as no win for either side), `predict_rate`, `band`, `agree` (`abs(rate_a - predict_rate) <= band` and no stall and no none seed), `firing_capable` (`{"A": mean, "B": mean}`, item 5), `median_seed` (the seed whose fight has the median tick count; lower seed on ties).
2. **Arc columns** in the Sides table, after the existing ones: `shape` (`block`, the default when blank, or `arc`), `centre_x`, `centre_y`, `radius`, `start_deg`, `end_deg`; the block columns are ignored for an arc. Angles in degrees in the converter's own convention (`COMPASS`: atan2 with y growing south, so 0 is east, 90 south, 180 west, 270 or -90 north), the arc running from `start_deg` to `end_deg` clockwise on screen. One ring. "Spaced at the pitch" means the chord between neighbours is `PITCH` (40 wu): angular step `2*asin(PITCH/(2*radius))`; the n units sit at `start_deg + k*step` for k in 0..n-1; the first and last units are on the ends when the span is exactly used. Each unit faces the arc's centre (`atan2(centre_y - y, centre_x - x)`). An arc that cannot hold its count at the pitch within its span is rejected with exactly: `arc too small: <n> units at pitch 40 on <span>° need radius <r>` where `<r>` is `ceil(PITCH / (2*sin(span_rad / (2*(n-1)))))` and `<span>` is the span in whole degrees.
3. **Arc in the layout:** the converter emits per-unit rows (`x`, `y`, `facing`, `role`) as for a block, and records the arc under the side as `side_a.arc = {centre: [x, y], radius, start_deg, end_deg}` (absent for blocks). `check_layout` gains a new rule with the next free id in `rules.toml` (R6 is taken by the wall standoff; the Dispatcher names the id, R<n> below), "arc placement": when a side carries `arc`, every unit of that side lies within 1 wu of `radius` from `centre`, else the layout is rejected with `R<n> arc placement: unit <id> is <d> wu from the centre, radius <r>`.
4. **Replay `qa_state`** gains `targets` (list of `[unit_id, target_id]` for the living units of the current frame that have a target), `first_shot_frame` (frame index, not tick, of the first frame in which any unit's hp is lower than in the previous frame; -1 when none), `seed` (the fight's `seed`) and `rate_a` (from `<id>.rate.json` beside the fight file when it exists, else `null`). HUD line appends `seed <n>` and, when a rate file exists, `rate <0.00>`. The first-shot marker is a ring on the damaged unit on that frame.
5. **`firing_capable`** per seed and side: the first-contact point is the position, at the first-shot frame, of the unit whose hp dropped first (lowest unit id on ties); a side's `firing_capable` is the count of its units alive at frame 0 whose role range (the roster's range for that role, read from the engine profile, the same number the range rings draw) reaches that point from the unit's frame-0 position (`distance <= range`); the rate file carries the mean over seeds per side, rounded to one decimal. -1 for a seed with no shot.
6. **Band** 0.10, item 1 wins over the earlier 0.15 in this file (fixed above).
7. **Layout keys and template lines.** Template head lines (`TEMPLATE.md`): `Expected win rate: <0 to 1, the probability of the side named in Predict; the converter stores side A's as predict_rate: the number when Predict is A, one minus it when B, 0.50 for draw>` (required for `Stage: 2` and above, Reject `missing expected win rate`; absent for stage 1 files), `Orders: both_same_point | hold:A | hold:B` (default `both_same_point`), `Orders point: midpoint | <x> <y>` (default `midpoint`). Layout keys: `predict_rate` (float), `orders` (the H-052 string), `orders_point` (`[x, y]`, absent for midpoint). `predict` (A/B/draw) stays required and is what v1 pages show.
8. **No jitter.** Nothing built or tested for it.

## Replay readability (ruled 2026-11-02 after the look reviewer failed the first stage 2 pictures; part of this story, one landing)

The first stage 2 strips were rejected by the fresh Sonnet reviewer: units about 10 px (4 px after the strip downscale), 1200 wu Bombard rings leaving the frame, rings told apart by size only, Raider and Interceptor glyphs alike, "end: wipe" for both a win and a mutual wipe, facing a 5 px tick. Every number below is DRAFT-stage2-look, the Architect's, for Westley's eye to change.

1. **Camera fit.** The replay camera fits the bounding box of every unit position over all frames of the fight, 10% margin, fixed for the whole fight, zoomed so a unit is at least 14 px across on the 1920x1080 window (when the fight needs a wider field than that allows, the units keep 14 px and the camera follows the fight's centroid). `qa_state()` gains `camera: {fit: [x0, y0, x1, y1], unit_px}`.
2. **Rings, one per role per side.** Per-unit rings stay behind key R (off by default). By default the replay draws one ring per role per side, centred on that side's unit of that role nearest the opposing centroid at frame 0, in the side's colour, with a distinct line style per role (DRAFT: Raider solid, Interceptor dotted, Vanguard dashed, Warden dash-dot, Bombard long dash, Bastion double line). The legend lists each ring: `<side> <role> <range> wu <style>`. A ring may leave the frame (a 1200 wu ring on a 4000 wu field does); an off-frame ring is still listed and its visible arc is drawn. `qa_state()` gains `rings: [{side, role, range, style, centre: [x, y]}]`.
3. **Role glyphs.** Six distinct shapes (DRAFT: Raider triangle, Interceptor diamond, Vanguard square, Warden pentagon, Bombard circle, Bastion hexagon), side by colour; the legend shows each. `qa_state()` gains `glyphs: {role: shape}`.
4. **Facing** tick length equals the glyph size (never under 8 px).
5. **End line.** `end: A wins (wipe)`, `end: B wins (wipe)`, `end: mutual wipe, no winner`, `end: stall (silence)`, `end: cap`; and the HUD rate line reads `rate A <measured> vs predicted <predicted>: AGREE|DISAGREE (band 0.10)`. `qa_state()` gains `end_label` and `rate_label` (strings, compared exactly in tests).
6. **No per-seed strip pages.** `page.py` writes one strip per layout and no `strip.html` per seed and no links to them.

Interface names and four replaced tests (ruled 2026-11-02 for the builder): ring styles `solid, dotted, dashed, dash_dot, long_dash, double` in the role order Raider, Interceptor, Vanguard, Warden, Bombard, Bastion; glyph names `triangle, diamond, square, pentagon, circle, hexagon` in the same order; camera margin 10% of each dimension on every side; on a wide field the fit changes per frame and `unit_px` stays at least 14; `facing_px` in `qa_state()` equals the glyph size, minimum 8; `rings[].side` is `A` or `B`; `end_label` is set on the last frame only and `""` before; `rate_label` is set on every frame when a rate file exists and `""` otherwise; rates print with two decimals; ring-centre ties are decided by the lowest unit id. The old `end_line` key is superseded. Four existing tests are replaced by this section and are edited with `Test-defect: replaced by H-053 Replay readability item <n> (DEC-stage2-replay-readability-2026-11-02)`: the end-word test, the rate-verdict test, the per-seed 480 px strip test (retargeted to the per-layout strip) and the per-unit ring-count test (key R on before it counts; the default count is the per-role rings).

Tests (the writer adds them to `tests/qa/test_fight_replay.py` and `tests/skilllab/arena/test_page.py`, red first): camera fit contains every unit of the fixture and `unit_px >= 14`; the fixture's rings list has one entry per role per side with a distinct style per role; glyph shapes are six distinct names; end_label and rate_label strings for a win, a mutual wipe and a stall fixture (positive control: the mutual-wipe label never reads "wins"); `page.py` on a three-seed fixture writes no per-seed strip file. The pictures are re-run and re-reviewed after the build; the records push waits for LOOK OK on all eight, or the reviewer's remaining fails are named in the README head.
