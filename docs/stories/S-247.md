# S-247: layout-kit-assault

"I see a layout the engine made for an Assault, round after round, until I say approve."

Flag sim; tool Workflow (`/manager-mode` cascade); no scene. Ready 2026-11-05 (rules R-112-01 to R-112-08 and R-113-01 to R-113-05 in `docs/bible/epics/E-17-map-generation.md`; no open `Q-` row). First of four stories of the layout engine (S-247, S-248, the contract story S-249, then the B2 drawing H-061).

## Why

Beta stage B2 must draw the game's layout flat and no engine produces one (`docs/reports/2026-11-05-layout-engine-triage.md`). Westley took the triage's recommendation: one definition of a layout (the kit of puzzle pieces with numbers, ruled 2026-10-04), one C function producing it from seed and mission kind, drawn flat for B2, terrain-obeys brought over for B3. This story builds the engine for one mission kind, Assault, and iterates on his pictures until he approves; the full engine (S-248) then repeats the loop.

## Change

New files only, behind a frozen header: `csrc/layout_kit.h` (contract), `csrc/layout_drafts.h`, `csrc/layout_kit.c`, `csrc/layout_kit_pieces.c`, `csrc/layout_kit_graph.c`, `csrc/tools/layout_dump.c`, `scripts/layout/render.py`, tests under `tests/csim/` and `tests/layout/`; Makefile targets; the bible rewrite of S-112 and S-113 (done in the records commit). The game is not touched. Full spec: `docs/northstar/plans/cascades/layout-kit-assault/spec.md`.

## Tests

`tests/csim/test_layout_kit.py` (umbrella, red on the branch's records commit), `tests/csim/test_layout_kit_{connected,fairness,bypass,approaches,chokes,bounds,determinism,header,places}.py`, `tests/layout/test_render.py` (written by the cascade's shard-test-writer from the spec's Tests section).

## Done when

He opens `docs/northstar/story_reviews/westley/layout_engine/round-A1/` and says approve: three maps (seed 1; mirror, rotate, separate) and three 12-seed sheets of an Assault layout, each reading as a map he would fight on. A deny becomes the next round (plan: a number change edits `csrc/layout_drafts.h` and re-renders; a rule change amends the spec and re-runs the cascade). Three rounds without approval: an options page with two or three directions.
