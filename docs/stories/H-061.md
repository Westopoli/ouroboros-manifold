# H-061: beta-b2-layout-no-units

"Start simple just like the simulation lab, with just a layout and no units, and show several versions."

Flag client; tool Workflow; beta stage B2. Spec'd; Ready when the layout engine exists. Rewritten 2026-11-05 (DEC-b2-game-layout-2026-11-05): the 2026-11-02 version of this story read "layout" as a simulation-lab catalogue layout (`docs/northstar/research/layer_dynamics/catalogue/`, drawn by `scenes/proto/layout_shot/layout_shot.gd`). That is a different thing: a lab layout presets units on one situation to measure it. "Just like the simulation lab" is the staircase method (one approved step at a time, several versions to switch between), not the lab's layouts.

## What it is

After DEPLOY FORCES in the beta, the mission's layout, the game's layout (E-17: the objective, each side's base, cities and resource sites, chokes with their width, high ground, lanes of travel, the map edge), drawn flat on the ground as labelled shapes: the layout engine's output and nothing else. The camera rig is live (pan and zoom, on the Linux box and on the Mac), no relief, no units. The `variant` picker on mission preparation (H-060) switches between the versions.

What the layout engine produces, how many versions B2 shows and whether places (objective, bases, cities, resource sites) are drawn here or wait for B4 come out of Westley's layout-engine work (separate chat from 2026-11-05; E-17 S-112 to S-119 are "rewrite pending", the eight files in `simulation/layout_library/` failed the 2026-10-18 audit, and no C layout generator runs in a mission on main). This story's Change, Tests and Scene lines are written after that, in the B2 cascade plan.
