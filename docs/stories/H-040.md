# H-040: arena-stage1-flat-field

Stage 1 of the simulation-lab staircase (DEC-lab-staircase-2026-10-31). Depends on H-039 (the snapshot contract) landed, and on H-025 round two for the shot scene's geometry and range-ring code (merge `s/H-025-*` into this branch if H-025 has not landed; this story's landing waits for H-025's).

"I place two groups of one unit type on an open field, both attack-move, and I watch the fight happen in the game and see who won against who I said would win."

Flag client; tool Workflow; Prototype gate (`docs/method/requirements.md`): judged by eye, Westley approves. Eyes gate (Westley 2026-10-30): this story does not land until he reports a scene on his display. Every number is an Architect draft dated 2026-10-31; Westley rules.

## Stage 1 floor (DEC-lab-stage1-floor-2026-10-31)

- Field: preset `small_flat` (`scripts/skilllab/catalogue/presets.toml`, 4000 wide, one layer), no features, no slope, no cover.
- Units: one role per side, DRAFT-stage1-role Vanguard both sides; headcounts from the layout (8 to 256 a side); every unit `order_attack_move` to the enemy centroid at tick 0; no defender, no guard.
- Sight off: `engine.set_param(w, OM_TP_TARGET_BY_SIGHT, 0)` before tick 0 (`csrc/unit.c:1175`: at switch 0 a target is usable whether or not the side sees it). Written as `sight: "off"` in the file.
- Cap: DRAFT-fight-cap 600 s of sim time (12,000 ticks at 20).

## Five parts, five builders, no shared file

Each part is its own branch `s/H-040-<part>`, its own worktree, its own test writer and clerk; parts 2, 3, 4 and 5 build in parallel once H-039 is on main. Part 3 is the only Godot part.

### Part 1: six layouts (`s/H-040-layouts`, Architect or Westley; no builder)

`docs/northstar/research/layer_dynamics/catalogue/L-0049.toml` to `L-0054.toml` plus `.json`: `situation = "open_field"`, `landscape = "small_flat"`, Vanguard both sides, pairs 16:16, 16:32, 32:16, 8:64, 64:8, 128:128, side A at x 800 facing east and side B at x 3200 facing west in 40 wu blocks, `predict` and `predict_why` per file (Westley's words when he writes them; the Architect's draft otherwise, marked `DRAFT`). Each passes `check_layout` (R1 to R5). If Westley writes `SIT-` documents first, part 5 converts them and these drafts are replaced.

### Part 2: arena runner (`s/H-040-run`)

`scripts/skilllab/arena/run.py`, CLI `python3 -m scripts.skilllab.arena.run --layout <toml> --seed <int> --out <dir> [--sight off|on] [--cap-seconds 600]`.
- Reads the layout (`tomllib`), refuses one failing `check_layout` (prints `REJECT <id> <rule>`, exit 2).
- Builds the world through the existing helpers only: `scripts/skilllab/engine.py` `Engine`, `fight.py` `_register_types` for the roster stats, `spawn` per layout row at the row's x, y, layer and facing (positions come from the layout, never from `formations.place`), `set_param` for sight, `order_attack_move` to the enemy centroid.
- Steps the engine, reading `unit_pos`, `unit_hp`, `unit_facing`, `unit_target`, `unit_alive`, `unit_layer` at each tick in `snapshot.frame_ticks(...)` and at the end; stops when one side has no survivors or at the cap.
- Writes `<out>/<id>-s<seed>.fight.json` through `snapshot.write`, and appends one row to `<out>/results.md` (`| id | a:b | predict | winner | survivors | ticks | agree |`).
- Prints `FIGHT <id> seed <n> winner <A|B|draw> ticks <t> agree <yes|no>` and exits 0.
- Imports only `scripts.skilllab.engine`, `scripts.skilllab.fight` (the helpers named), `scripts.skilllab.catalogue.produce.check_layout`, `scripts.skilllab.arena.snapshot`. Nothing from the "not used before stage 6" list (`docs/northstar/research/layer_dynamics/trim_report_2026-10-31.md`); the test greps the module's imports.

Tests `tests/skilllab/arena/test_run.py`: a 2:2 Vanguard layout fixture runs to a result under the cap, the file validates, `alive` in the last frame matches `survivors`; the same layout and seed twice give byte-identical files; a 1:1 fixture with the cap at 1 s writes `capped: true` and `winner: "draw"`; `--plant`-style control: a layout fixture breaking R2 gives `REJECT` and no file; the import grep.

### Part 3: replay scene (`s/H-040-replay`)

`scenes/proto/fight_replay/fight_replay.tscn` and `fight_replay.gd` (plus `.uid`), new files. It `preload`s and reuses the layout shot's drawing (`scenes/proto/layout_shot/layout_shot.gd` helpers for terrain, units, glyphs, legend, camera framing) and adds a frame cursor; it edits no existing file (`scripts/specfactory/gd_reach_stubs.txt` gains its scripts, DEC-proto-scenes-reach-2026-10-30).
- Reads `OM_FIGHT` (a `fight.json` path, parsed with `JSON.parse_string`; unknown keys ignored). Draws frame 0 exactly as the layout shot draws a layout: sides A blue and B red, facing arrows, glyph at least 14 px, legend, camera framed on all units with margin 0.15.
- Space or Right steps one frame; Left steps back; `OM_AUTOPLAY=1` advances at DRAFT-replay-fps 1 frame per second. A dead unit stays drawn as a flat grey marker at its last position (he sees where it fell). A unit's `target` is a thin line to the target while it has one and has fired (replay-firing-lines, 2026-11-07: Westley asked for lines only while a unit fires; a row with no `fired` key, an older file, draws the line as before).
- HUD line top-left: `tick <t>  A <alive_a>  B <alive_b>  frame <i>/<n>`; after the last frame: `winner <w>  predicted <p>  <agree|disagree>`.
- `OM_SHOT_OUT=<dir>` saves `frame-<i>.png` for every frame (30 render frames settle before the first, 2 between) and quits.
- `qa_state()`: `scene`, `fight_id`, `frame`, `frame_count`, `tick`, `alive_a`, `alive_b`, `result` (the result table), `autoplay` (bool).

Tests `tests/qa/test_fight_replay.py` (launches Godot; H-035 import rule applies): headless run with `OM_FIGHT` on `snapshot.example(frames=3)` written to a temp file; `frame_count` equals 3; stepping (qa daemon key press or `OM_AUTOPLAY=1` after 2.5 s) reaches `frame` 2 with `alive_a`, `alive_b` from the file; `OM_SHOT_OUT` yields 3 PNGs none blank (reuse the look metric, uniform fraction below 1); positive control: the same file with one frame removed gives `frame_count` 2.

### Part 4: stage page (`s/H-040-page`)

`scripts/skilllab/arena/page.py`, CLI `python3 -m scripts.skilllab.arena.page --dir <out dir> --out <html>`: one static page in the shape of `scripts/skilllab/catalogue/review.py build` (same CSS, no new framework). One row per `*.fight.json`: id, pair, predict and predict_why (from the layout), winner, survivors, ticks, agree in green or red, then the frame strip (every `frame-<i>.png` in order, 240 px high, captions `tick <t> A <a> B <b>`), then the fall map (the last frame larger). Reads `fight.json` and the layout; imports `snapshot.read` and `tomllib` only. The page's words are Westley's verdict cell per fight as in `review.py` (`verdicts.md` shape, one row per fight id).

Tests `tests/skilllab/arena/test_page.py`: with two example fights and stub PNGs the page lists both ids, every PNG once in order, the agree cell carries the right class; a `fight.json` that fails `validate` is listed as `INVALID <id>` and the page still builds (positive control).

### Part 5: situations converter (`s/H-040-situations`)

`scripts/skilllab/arena/situations.py`, CLI `python3 -m scripts.skilllab.arena.situations --dir docs/northstar/research/layer_dynamics/situations --out docs/northstar/research/layer_dynamics/catalogue --next-id <L-NNNN>`: reads every `SIT-*.md` written from `TEMPLATE.md` (its tables; the format is in the template), writes `L-NNNN.toml` and `.json` in the H-025 schema with `features` from the template's geometry table, runs `check_layout`, prints `CONVERTED <sit> <layout>` or `REJECT <sit> <rule or missing field>` and exits 2 on any REJECT. Units are placed in 40 wu blocks (DRAFT-block-pitch, the H-025 round-two spacing) from the block's anchor, count and facing in the table; the author writes no per-unit rows. A situation whose `stage` is above the current one is still converted (it waits in the catalogue).

Tests `tests/skilllab/arena/test_situations.py`: the template's own example converts to a layout that passes `check_layout` with the stated headcounts and predict; a copy missing `predict` gives `REJECT ... missing predict`; two files get consecutive ids.

## Confirmed builder choices (2026-11-01)

- Layout rule R4 (8 to 256 a side) stands; the story's 2:2 and 1:1 fixtures were wrong, the tests use 8:12 and 8:8.
- `Engine.spawn` carries no facing and `engine.py` does not bind `om_unit_set_facing`: stage 1 spawns without facing (both sides attack-move at tick 0, so facing follows at once). Binding the facing is stage 5's work (DRAFT-facing-binding-stage5); the frame's `facing` is what the engine reports.
- Page: classes `agree`/`disagree`, the last frame both in the strip and as the fall map, `FALL_HEIGHT` 480 px DRAFT.
- Converter: anchor is the block centre, north is -pi/2, a non-zero `range` is rejected until stage 2 (ranges come from the engine).
- Replay: HUD frame index from 0, camera framed on all frames, `small_flat` drawn as the landscape, look constants DRAFT.
- Layouts: `seed` equals the layout number; the layout carries no sight field (sight is the runner's flag).
- H-039 `read()` raises `ValueError` on an invalid file.

## Orders and fight end (ruled 2026-11-01, DEC-lab-amove-rule-2026-11-01; contract H-052)

Westley's rule, verbatim: "either one or both teams need to have all their units attack-moved onto the same location. (exceptions to this rule are chokepoints, traps, and I'll mention it for each individual layout and document it before we finalize all the layouts) ... Specific sides like the forces in the trap are not supposed to A-move, but the opposing force has to A-move on the center mass of the force being trapped. That leads to no possible situation where a draw happens, one force will win no matter what. The only other situation is both forces A-moved on the same spot, and every single layout will follow one of those two rules."

Runner (part 2) changes before this story lands:
- Orders from the layout's `orders` key (H-052): `both_same_point` sends every unit of both sides to one point, DRAFT-same-point the midpoint of the two sides' tick-0 centroids; `hold:<side>` gives the held side no order and sends every attacker to the held side's centre of mass at tick 0. The stage 1 layouts L-0049 to L-0054 are `both_same_point`. (Stages 1 to 3; from stage 4 `--sight on` puts the holder on `order_guard`, H-088.)
- Fight end: `wipe`, or `silence` after DRAFT-silence-seconds 10 s of sim time with no shot by either side (the engine's shot events, as `fight.py` `_Reengage` counts them, or hp deltas if no shot event is exposed), or the 600 s cap as a backstop. `silence` with both sides alive is a `stall`: the runner prints `FIGHT <id> seed <n> STALL ticks <t>` and exits 0; the page shows the row in a flagged colour with "stall: bring to Westley"; the clerk lists every stall in its report and in the README rows it sends him. Westley: "when it does I'd like to have a hand in seeing what's going on until I'm sure the rules are being properly enforced."
- The replay HUD prints `end: wipe|silence|cap` and `STALL` on the last frame.
- Tests (part 2): a 8:8 fixture ends by `wipe` well under the cap; a fixture with the two sides out of range and `hold:A` orders where the attacker's point is unreachable (a planted blocker) ends by `silence` at about 10 s with `stall` true (positive control); the same fixture with `both_same_point` ends by `wipe`.

The six stage 1 fights are re-run after this change and the page is re-sent to him before the landing's look review.
