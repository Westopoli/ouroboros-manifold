# H-008: the simulation lab

"Players with better understanding of the landscape, unit formation, layer awareness/intentionality, and the other players' current position can defeat a much larger force." (Westley, 2026-10-01.) "The whole point of the simulation lab is to find multiple points where the lines cross" (2026-10-15). "Where the lines cross should be documented quite extensively, we know the lab is finished when that happens" (2026-10-27). "The lab is more important than the beta" (2026-10-26).

This is the umbrella story for the lab under `docs/method/`. It states what the lab is, what it measures and when it is done, so that every lab build below it is a story with a rule to prove. It builds nothing itself. Westley reads and approves this page before any lab story builds (`docs/method/westley.md`: a story that changes a rule needs his go).

## What the lab is

An instrument on the real C sim (never a model of it) that runs fights between a force of 16 played with one skill and a larger force played naively, and finds the enemy headcount at which the skilled force stops winning: the crossing, in whole units ("16 beats 17 and loses to 18"). Five skills: landscape, formation, layers, enemy position, unit-type matchup. Knobs are swept (vision, range, damage, emitting, arc, cone, reach) so the crossing is found as a function of the game's numbers, which is how those numbers get tuned. Output is a page Westley opens: findings, a top-down diagram per crossing, and the conditions verdicts.

## New rule this story writes (Westley to approve)

R-LAB-01 Positioning decides. Given two forces of equal units and numbers, When one holds the better position (higher layer with reach, cover, flank, or sight without being seen), Then that force wins the fight by design; the lab predicts the winner from the setup before the fight and reports every fight where the prediction fails as a finding. (Westley 2026-10-19: "a better-positioned force WILL succeed by design; predict from the setup; never say the setup says nothing.") Home: E-01 acceptance bar and the lab README section 2.

## Done

The lab is finished when the crossings document exists: for each of the five skills, the crossing headcount per layout class and per knob setting, with a diagram, and the regions where all conditions C0 to C9 hold, found two ways (all settings at once; one knob at a time). Each lab story below moves one capability toward it; this story is Seen when Westley has read that document.
