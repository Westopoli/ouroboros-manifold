# H-052: arena-snapshot-contract-v2 (orders and fight end)

"The fight file says how each side was ordered and why the fight ended: a wipe, ten seconds of silence, or the cap; a silent end with both sides alive is flagged for me."

Flag harness; tool Workflow; no scene. Ready 2026-11-01. Amends H-039's `fight.json`; lands with H-040 (not yet landed) in H-040's landing, as the contract part of its runner change.

## Why

Westley, 2026-11-01, on the stage 1 page: "with this number of units and the distance between them, the fight was essentially over at 3200 ticks, we don't need to run 12k ticks." and "depending on the layer, either one or both teams need to have all their units attack-moved onto the same location ... Specific sides like the forces in the trap are not supposed to A-move, but the opposing force has to A-move on the center mass of the force being trapped. That leads to no possible situation where a draw happens ... we should have a flag that if neither side has fired for 10 seconds, the simulation ends, and if both teams still have living units then that specific simulation needs to be flagged and brought up to me". He notes he asked for this before; the space lab's runner has the re-engage and silence logic (`scripts/skilllab/fight.py` `_Reengage`, `REENGAGE_SILENCE_TICKS`), which the stage 1 runner did not use.

## Schema change (`scripts/skilllab/arena/snapshot.py`, `SNAPSHOT.md`)

`schema` becomes `om-fight-2`; `om-fight-1` files still validate (the checker accepts both and the page shows the version).
- New top-level `orders`: `{"mode": "both_same_point" | "hold_and_attack", "point": [x, y], "attacker": "A" | "B" | null}`. `both_same_point`: every unit of both sides attack-moves to `point`. `hold_and_attack`: the holder (the other side) receives no order and fires from where it stands; the attacker attack-moves every unit to `point`, which is the holder's centre of mass at tick 0. No other mode exists (Westley: "every single layout will follow one of those two rules").
- New `result.end`: `"wipe"` (one side has no survivors), `"silence"` (no shot by either side for DRAFT-silence-seconds 10 of sim time), `"cap"` (DRAFT-fight-cap 600 s, a backstop that should not be reached).
- New `result.stall`: true when `end` is `silence` and both sides have survivors. `winner` is then `"stall"`, not `"draw"`; `agree` is false and the row is flagged.
- The checker rejects an `om-fight-2` file missing `orders` or `result.end`, and a `stall` without `end == "silence"`.

The layout schema (H-025) gains `orders = "both_same_point"` or `orders = "hold:A"` / `orders = "hold:B"` (the held side); a layout without the key means `both_same_point` (stage 1 default). The situations template gains the line `Orders:`.
