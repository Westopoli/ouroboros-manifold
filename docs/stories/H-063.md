## What stage 3 adds (and nothing else)

- **The layout's features become obstacles for the sim.** Chokepoint walls (a gap between), city buildings, cover boxes as blocks, a slope band with `OM_TP_SLOPE_UP_DIV`, plateau and ridge edges as tier steps on one travel layer (R-001-06 and R-001-07: terrain tiers, not planes). Feature kinds follow the H-025 round-two schema (`walls`, `plateau`, `boxes`, `ring`, `buildings`, `band`, `ridge`, `disc`, `pocket`). Hazard discs and the vantage's second visual band stay drawn only (stage 7; a second visual band without a second travel band is not expressible, finding H-058 vantage-height).
- **The arena asks the engine for blocking** (the ground lab's preflight route check in `scripts/skilllab/scenes_ground.py` is the pattern) and REJECTs a layout whose attackers cannot reach the defenders, with a line naming the first unreachable unit (a new rule id, the next free in `rules.toml`; test with a positive control).
- **Replay draws the same geometry** as the layout shot (shared drawing code from `scenes/proto/layout_shot/layout_shot.gd`, no second drawing) under the units; the stage 2 readability rules (camera fit, role rings and glyphs, labelled end line) carry over.
- **Layouts:** the catalogue's chokepoint, city, cover and slope layouts (round three's LOOK OK pictures: chokepoint L-0055 to L-0060, city L-0073 to L-0078, plus the cover and slope variants as they exist), each with a winner guess and his percentage as the heuristic target (unscored). Which of them he picks first is his; the Architect proposes chokepoint L-0055, city L-0073, slope and cover once each, four fights.
- **Sight stays off** (`OM_TP_TARGET_BY_SIGHT` 0), abilities off, engine rules of H-057 (engage on sight, per-type leash). Orders per the two rules (H-052). Scoring as ruled 2026-11-02 (winner guess; target and gap printed, unscored).
- **What the pictures must show him:** where the geometry slows or stops a unit (a marker on units blocked for more than 2 s: the first-contact queue at a gap is the thing he wants to see), and the same fight without the geometry beside it as a control strip (the layout with `features` emptied, same units), so the effect of the ground is visible, not argued.
