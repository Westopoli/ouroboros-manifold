- *Hosting (rev 3: the D-VIEW pre-flight's M1 to M9, ruled 2026-10-13).* OPT-IN for the first look: `tactical_view.gd` hosts the `SpaceView` only when the environment has `OM_SPACE_VIEW=1` (read once through `OS.get_environment`; the D play sets it); the default space era stays 2D until Westley approves the shot; flipping the default is a later piece `VG-D-DEFAULT` that pays the test and play ripple under `TD-VG-D-1`. The host is assigned to the `_ground3d` member, duck-typed (`set_frame`, `qa_state`, `camera_rig`, `to_screen`, `to_world`, `add_layer`), and is created AFTER `set_terrain` in `_load_live` (right after :276) and after the NetMatch branch's `set_terrain`, because `set_terrain` with the space era's empty terrain frees `_ground3d` (:290-300): `if _space_view_opted_in() and _ground3d == null and not _ground_era(): _host_space_view()`; `set_terrain` is not edited. `qa_state` gains one line forwarding the host's `space3d` key; `SpaceView.qa_state()` returns `ground3d: {present: false}` plus `space3d: {present: true, ...}`, so `play_support.py:177` and every present-false test are untouched. The hosting code hides only the 2D unit bodies (`unit_squads` and the shape draw); `UnitVisuals` hp bars and the selection ring stay and project through the host's `to_screen` with the unit's layer. Planes: the view's `to_screen(wx, wy)` and `to_world(screen)` use the FOCUS sheet's plane (markers, fog, shots, clicks, drag-select, the daemon `project` verb); `units_px` uses each unit's own sheet; `space3d.focus_layer` reports which. Cells follow the sim: a fill square is centred at `((cx + 0.5)·100, (cy + 0.5)·100)`, stride 240, mask index `cy·240 + cx` (`csrc/space_raster.c`), not the mockup's placer offsets.
- *Field.* `space_view.gd` calls `SimSession.bridge().space_field()` (`autoload/c_bridge.gd:646-672`, fetched at :768) once after `mission_begin`: 5 masks of 240 by 240 cells and the polys `[layer, kind, points]`. Sheets: engine layer L (0-based) sits at `y = L * LAYER_GAP_WU`, `LAYER_GAP_WU := 1000.0` pinned in `space_view.gd` with a parity test that reads `scenes/mockups/landscape/field_map_space.gd:22` (`DRAFT-space-layer-gap`, Q-246-01). Asteroids are the pass 6 look: a slab per poly as `placer.gd _place_sheets` (:238-336) builds them (plate thickness 60, rubble 30 from `kits/space.json`, `DRAFT-sheet-thickness`), plus one MultiMesh square per mask-blocked cell not covered by a poly (the client rasterises polys to find them); no walls (the sim does not send them); meteors spanning layers (R-246-02) are the follow-up piece `VG-D-METEORS` after the verdict. Darkening of non-focus sheets 55 percent as the kit-mode spec section 4 (`DRAFT-sheet-dim`).
- *Units.* `Units3D` reused as flyers with floors at the sheet tops: a unit's `y = sheet top + 150` where the sheet top is `L * gap + thickness` (plate 60, rubble 30) and 150 is `FLYER_CLEARANCE_WU` (`ground_units.gd:11`); its shadow disc sits on its own sheet top (+0.5), never under sheet 0; in transit (state bit 4) `y` interpolates linearly from its layer to `tl` between the current tick and `ld`. Every type is one body for now (R-088-01's raider squad drawing waits for `DRAFT-raider-radius` with the roster). Own units always; enemies only when visible, as `Ground3D.set_frame` filters.
- *Layer indicators (R-246-04, the condition).* `space_rings.gd`: a flat ring under every unit at its sheet top + 2, coloured by the unit's layer with the strip's five colours (`scenes/tactical_c/layer_view.gd:13 LAYER_COLOURS`); a unit in transit shows a second, dashed ring on its destination sheet in the destination colour (`DRAFT-ring-radius` 1.2 × body radius; ring and rim widths scale with camera height so they read at least `DRAFT-ring-px` 3 px; cell squares `DRAFT-cell-thickness` 30 wu). Rings draw through the tube like their units.
- *Vision tube (Q-S3-01 (a), the condition).* Focus layer = the strip's `focus_layer` (the selection's majority layer when the strip is unset). Tube centre = the cursor's position projected onto the focus sheet; radius `DRAFT-tube-radius` 1,500 wu. Every sheet above the focus layer fades its slabs and cell squares inside the tube to alpha `DRAFT-tube-alpha` 0.15; the "small effect that says so" is a rim line at the tube's edge on each faded sheet in that sheet's layer colour (`DRAFT-tube-rim` 10 wu wide), pulsing alpha 0.6 to 1.0 over 1.5 s. Done in `sheet_tube.gdshader` (uniforms `tube_center`, `tube_radius`, `focus_y`; a world-space cylinder test per fragment), one material for every slab and square, so the cost is one uniform update per frame. Picking inside the tube resolves on the focus sheet (the ray hits the focus sheet's plane first because faded geometry is `no_depth` for picking: the view's own ray test, not physics).
- *Camera (gap g2).* The rig as is (`camera_rig_3d.gd`, VG-C's; no setter for height or pitch, pitch 50 below 6,000): the play pans to the player's spawn anchor and zooms out through the rig API until the rig reports a height of at least `DRAFT-space-cam-height` 5,400 (the rig's pitch is 50 below 6,000 and fov 75; sheet 4 at y 4,000 enters the frame near 5,100 by the rig math; 300 wu zoom steps from 3,000 reach 5,400), so sheets 0 to 4 and a layer-4 unit are in view; the 3D fit of A18 (FU-A18-3d) applies later.
