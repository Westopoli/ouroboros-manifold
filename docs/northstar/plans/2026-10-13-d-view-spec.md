- *Hosting (Dispatcher gaps g1 and g3, ruled 2026-10-13).* The `SpaceView` is assigned to the `_ground3d` member, duck-typed: it exposes the surface `Ground3D` exposes (`set_frame`, `qa_state` returning the `ground3d` dict plus a `space3d` key, `camera_rig`, `to_screen`, `to_world`, `add_layer`), so the ten `_ground3d != null` sites need no branch. The hosting code hides the 2D unit nodes (`unit_visuals`, `unit_squads` parents `visible = false` while `_ground3d != null`), one line; ground draws both today (finding `FU-ground-2d-overdraw`, lane C, not this piece). Nothing else in `tactical_view.gd` changes.
- *Field.* `space_view.gd` calls `SimSession.bridge().space_field()` (`autoload/c_bridge.gd:646-672`, fetched at :768) once after `mission_begin`: 5 masks of 240 by 240 cells and the polys `[layer, kind, points]`. Sheets: engine layer L (0-based) sits at `y = L * LAYER_GAP_WU`, `LAYER_GAP_WU := 1000.0` pinned in `space_view.gd` with a parity test that reads `scenes/mockups/landscape/field_map_space.gd:22` (`DRAFT-space-layer-gap`, Q-246-01). Asteroids are the pass 6 look: a slab per poly as `placer.gd _place_sheets` (:238-336) builds them (plate thickness 60, rubble 30 from `kits/space.json`, `DRAFT-sheet-thickness`), plus one MultiMesh square per mask-blocked cell not covered by a poly (the client rasterises polys to find them); no walls (the sim does not send them); meteors spanning layers (R-246-02) are the follow-up piece `VG-D-METEORS` after the verdict. Darkening of non-focus sheets 55 percent as the kit-mode spec section 4 (`DRAFT-sheet-dim`).
- *Units.* `Units3D` reused as flyers with floors at the sheet tops: a unit's `y = L * gap + the Units3D flyer clearance (150, ground_units.gd:20)`; in transit (state bit 4) `y` interpolates linearly from its layer to `tl` between the current tick and `ld`. Every type is one body for now (R-088-01's raider squad drawing waits for `DRAFT-raider-radius` with the roster). Own units always; enemies only when visible, as `Ground3D.set_frame` filters.
- *Layer indicators (R-246-04, the condition).* `space_rings.gd`: a flat ring under every unit on its sheet top, coloured by the unit's layer with the strip's five colours (`scenes/tactical_c/layer_view.gd:13 LAYER_COLOURS`); a unit in transit shows a second, dashed ring on its destination sheet in the destination colour (`DRAFT-ring-radius` 1.2 × body radius; ring and rim widths scale with camera height so they read at least `DRAFT-ring-px` 3 px; cell squares `DRAFT-cell-thickness` 30 wu). Rings draw through the tube like their units.
- *Vision tube (Q-S3-01 (a), the condition).* Focus layer = the strip's `focus_layer` (the selection's majority layer when the strip is unset). Tube centre = the cursor's position projected onto the focus sheet; radius `DRAFT-tube-radius` 1,500 wu. Every sheet above the focus layer fades its slabs and cell squares inside the tube to alpha `DRAFT-tube-alpha` 0.15; the "small effect that says so" is a rim line at the tube's edge on each faded sheet in that sheet's layer colour (`DRAFT-tube-rim` 10 wu wide), pulsing alpha 0.6 to 1.0 over 1.5 s. Done in `sheet_tube.gdshader` (uniforms `tube_center`, `tube_radius`, `focus_y`; a world-space cylinder test per fragment), one material for every slab and square, so the cost is one uniform update per frame. Picking inside the tube resolves on the focus sheet (the ray hits the focus sheet's plane first because faded geometry is `no_depth` for picking: the view's own ray test, not physics).
- *Camera (gap g2).* The rig as is (`camera_rig_3d.gd`, VG-C's; no setter for height or pitch, pitch 50 below 6,000): the play pans to the player's spawn anchor and zooms out through the rig API until the rig reports a height above `DRAFT-space-cam-height` 4,200, so sheets 0 to 4 are in view; the 3D fit of A18 (FU-A18-3d) applies later.
- *qa_state.* `space3d: {present: true, gap_wu, sheets: [{layer, y, blocked_cells, slabs}], units: {id: {layer, y, ring_layer, dest_ring_layer or -1}}, tube: {active, cx, cy, radius_wu, faded_layers: [...]}, camera: {x, y, height, pitch}}`; `units_px` from the `to_screen` analogue. Deterministic element inventory: 5 sheets, `blocked_cells` per sheet equal to the mask's popcount, `slabs` equal to the poly count per layer, one ring per drawn unit.
