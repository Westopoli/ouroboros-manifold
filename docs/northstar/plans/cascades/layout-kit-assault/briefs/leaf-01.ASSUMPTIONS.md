- **Further drafts (reason each)**: DRAFT_TURN_Q16 (one turn of the Q16 tables, needed for the k*turn/steps integer angle); DRAFT_MIRROR_AXIS_STEP 6 (mirror only works with the axis along x or y, a quarter turn); DRAFT_PATTERN_COUNT 3 and DRAFT_PATTERN_WIDE 1.4 (band/ring/patches weight on the district grid); DRAFT_EPS, DRAFT_INF (guards, unreachable sentinel); DRAFT_SOLVE_STEPS 40 and DRAFT_BULGE_PTS 7 (bisection that fits a meandering lane to a length ratio, and its polyline points); DRAFT_BASE_U/_JIT/_V, DRAFT_GATE_U/_JIT/_V, DRAFT_EXP_DU/_JIT, DRAFT_EXP_DV/_JIT (where each side's base, gate and expansion lie); DRAFT_OBJ_DU/_DV (objective beside the defender's base); DRAFT_CROSS_T_MIN/_MAX/_V/_LEN/_LEN_JIT/_TURN_MAX (where the crossing starts on the road and its lane length, lean); DRAFT_CHOKE_FRAC_MIN/_MAX, DRAFT_CHOKE_LEN, DRAFT_WALL_SPAN (the choke on its lane); DRAFT_WAYROUND_MARGIN 0.05 (keeps the way-round ratio inside 1.5..2.4 after the JSON rounds to hundredths); DRAFT_VANT_OFFSET, DRAFT_VANT_R (vantage); DRAFT_PRIZE_SPUR, DRAFT_M_FRAC_MIN/_MAX (prize spurs and the fair point's clamp); DRAFT_PAD_BASE_A/_B/_OBJ/_RES/_EXP (pad radii); DRAFT_LANE_W_ROUTE/_WAY/_CLIMB/_OBJ (lane widths, not scaled with the canvas) — source: spec "Named drafts" last sentence, no source for the values (pure guess, drafts for Westley).
- **Graph shape**: road A - gateA - J1 -[choke lane]- J2 - M(fair point, prizes) - gateB - B, objective hung on gateB and B by two objective_route lanes (its two approaches), way_round J1-J2, climb lanes vantage-J1 and vantage-gateA, expansion lanes off each gate — source: spec Design step 3 and checks 1 to 4, shape is my choice.
- **Gate piece**: no place or choke carries `OM_LK_PIECE_GATE`; the gates are junction nodes (place -1) at each home ground's edge, so no `piece` field shows them — source: header gives `piece` only to places, chokes and vantages; pure guess.
- **Expansion piece**: expansions carry `OM_LK_PIECE_HOME_GROUND`; resources `prize`; objective `objective_ground` (test pins the last two).
- **Fair point**: the two prizes hang off node M on the road after the crossing; M's position is solved in closed form so route distances from the two bases to M are equal (exactly, unless clamped by DRAFT_M_FRAC_*) — source: spec Design step 3 "equal route distance"; the closed form is mine.
- **Sides modes**: mirror needs the axis along x or y (axis steps 0, 6, 12, 18), reflects u to -u; rotate negates u and v; separate draws side B's ground from its own stream and bisects (DRAFT_MEANDER_STEPS) a meander ratio of the lane M to gate B between DRAFT_MEANDER_MIN and _MAX until the fair point is equal-distance — source: spec Design step 4. Only the ground (base, gate, expansion) is transformed, the road, crossing, prizes and heights are built once between the gates.
- **Division pattern**: band/ring/two patches only change the weights of the nearest-anchor fill of the 8 by 8 district grid (each anchor first claims its nearest free cell, so all six roles always appear) — source: spec Design step 3, reference layout_gen.gd not available to me; pure guess.
- **Choke axis_k**: the 15-degree step (0..11) closest to the lane's direction (along the passage), found by dot product against the Q16 table directions — source: header says "the gap's axis in 15-degree steps"; direction vs perpendicular is a guess.
- **Landform requests**: one per choke, per vantage, and one per base for the home-ground landform (basin, plateau or bend); routes carry no name; vantage `side` +1 when nearer base A by straight line — source: spec step 5.
- **Return -1 when no candidate fits the caps**: build() fails only on a cap overflow, which cannot happen with this fixed structure; kept as the header says.
- **ASSAULT_CROSSINGS/HEIGHTS/PRIZES**: the builder builds exactly one of each; a draft other than 1 makes the candidate fail (generate returns -1) instead of building more — source: spec Design step 3; the pieces are single-instance.
- **Objective offset**: the objective's distance from the defender's base is a fixed draft (no jitter), so dist_b_objective is the same for every seed — source: Q-191-11 "beside the defender"; no source for jitter.
