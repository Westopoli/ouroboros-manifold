# Sim lane scout B: the source of the chronicle (ROADMAP 2b step 1, part 2)

Scout report, 2026-10-13, branch `push/simlane-b`, tree `origin/main` at c7961d75 plus these files. Findings only: nothing in `csrc/`, `tests/`, `docs/bible/` or the ambiguity register was changed; every fix below is a proposed piece for Westley to read first. Thresholds are his.

## Method

A time jump is one pipe verb and streams no deltas, so every jump is replayed in a second pipe process: `save` just before the jump, a fresh `pipe/ouroboros_pipe_dbg` does `set_seed` (`new_campaign:false`), `load`, then `step n`. The deltas it streams (events, attacks, collisions, revolts, owner changes) are the independent record of what the jump saw. The replay is accepted only if its ownership timeline equals the `ownership.snapshots` of the jump's `chronicle` frame (every other check is skipped for a jump that fails this; none did). The entries of the `chronicle` frame are then compared with that record, and with the mission log (`mission_start` region, `debrief.band`) for the same ticks.

- Seeds 1 to 10, `n_regions` 80 (see Limitations: 60 is refused by generation), features [0..7], faction 0, `era_kind` 0.
- Per seed 10 base jumps: step 30, jump 50 (no mission); mission 1 (`objective_complete` 1.0, `mission_end`, band 4), jump 50; jump 20 (no mission); mission 2 (completion 0.4, band 2), jump 100; mission 3 (`mission_refuse`, band 5), jump 50; step 15, jump 50 (no mission); four more missions at completion 0.0 / 0.2 / 0.6 / 0.85 (bands 0, 1, 3 reached), jump 20 each. Then 14 further mission+jump(50) cycles per seed to watch chapter, lineage and entry count over a long campaign.
- 240 jumps, 24 per seed, all replayed and verified.
- Scripts: `scripts/simlane/chronicle_source.py` (driver, oracle, checks; `--seeds 1-10 --cycles 14 --out DIR`), `scripts/simlane/chronicle_probe.py` (ctypes probe of the categorisers). Repro for any row: `cd ~/Projects/om-push/wt/simlane-b && ~/Projects/ouroboros-manifold/.venv/bin/python -m scripts.simlane.chronicle_source --seeds <seed> --cycles 14 --out <dir>`, then read `<dir>/result.json` (`rows`: one record per occurrence with seed, jump label and tick) and `<dir>/seed<N>_jumps.json` (frames and events).

## What passed (counts over the 240 jumps)

- Replay fidelity: 240 of 240 ownership timelines equal.
- R-055-06 chapter counter: advances by exactly 1 after each of the 210 mission jumps and by 0 after each of the 30 no-mission jumps, 240 of 240.
- R-065-02 consequence mapping and placement, all six bands: band 4 x160 and band 3 x10 give `consequence_success`, bands 2 x17 and 1 x10 give `consequence_mixed`, band 0 x3 and refusal (band 5) x10 give `consequence_failure`; exactly one consequence entry after every mission jump, it is entry 1, in the mission region (210 of 210), none in any of the 30 no-mission jumps among the base set. Refusal maps to failure as R-065-02 says.
- Ripple placement: the set of ripple regions equals "regions adjacent to the mission region with a fired event" in 210 of 210 jumps.
- R-065-05 category cap: no category above 2 among chain and regional entries, 240 of 240. R-065-06 minimum: 240 of 240 jumps carry 5 or more entries.
- Ranking audit (R-065-05): I rebuilt the regional candidates from the deltas the way `gen_time_jump` feeds them and checked every one that did not reach the frame: 4368 skips justified by the 2-per-category cap or by a higher score, 0 ranking violations; 1147 of 1147 selected regional entries have a region with an event, ownership change or revolt behind them (no padding entry with no source was seen).
- Significance formula: every regional entry's `significance` equals (tier + min(sum|delta|/50, 3)) times 1.0 or 1.8 for one of its sources (1147 of 1147; the launch tier table in `generational.c` equals `om_event_template_significance_tier` for ids 0 to 25).
- Faction refs: `refs.factions` equals the region owner at the jump end for every entry that has text (2174 of 2174).
- No digit in any entry text or expanded text (2174 entries with text). No entry tick range outside the jump window; every entry's range contains a keyframe whenever the jump has any (see SL-B-11 for the converse).
- Chain entries have a source: all 264 chain entries sit on a region where a chain began in the window.

## Chapters and Q-057-01

Chapter counter: advances once per mission jump, never otherwise (240 of 240, SL-B passed list). No hard cap on chapters in a lineage is in force: the first lineage stayed through the whole base campaign (8 missions) plus 5 to 8 further post-mission jumps in the 14-cycle runs (seeds 1 and 2: 5; seed 10: 7; seeds 3 to 7 and 9: 8), i.e. past chapter 16 against the removed cap of 6 (A-050). That is consistent with Q-057-01's answer (no cap, implementer's curve). The decay curve itself cannot be separated from R-057-03's base probability by this data, so no curve is claimed. What the long runs do show is SL-B-03: from about tick 850 the selector rotates on every jump with a frozen commander, which the stay rule for a fresh lineage (R-057-02) does not allow.

Q-067-01 (category count): the frame carries 21 categories as ruled (3 outcome + ripple); ripple has no fragment pool (placeholder line), so the 399-fragment math holds only for the other 20.

## Limitations (what is unverified)

- `n_regions` 60, as the brief asked, makes `set_seed` fail (generation below the feasible minimum, as the viewer spec's pre-flight 8 says; the error shows on the next verb, not on `set_seed`). All runs use 80.
- Collisions: 0 in the whole stream (113235 attacks), so no collision-sourced entry could be compared. Mission-internal events (Q-068-03) are not in the strategic stream and could not be compared.
- The opening-jump scroll (R-065-07) is built silently inside `set_seed` and no frame carries it; not checked.
- The lineage term of the significance (+0.8) and the lineage cluster's region are not on the wire: significance is checked as base x {1.0, 1.8} per source (events score at the event tick, the ownership term after the descendants are generated, so a single region can legitimately carry two factors); the lineage region and `lineage_quiet` placement (R-065-04) are not verifiable from outside. The lineage category (R-067-02) is only checked through the case-sensitivity probe, not per entry.
- Text content beyond "no digit": anaphora (R-068-05), synonyms (R-068-06), first-mention qualifier (R-068-04), mention offsets (R-069-07) and the 90/140 word limits were not exercised; the 40 percent overlap rule is only seen through its effect (SL-B-04).
- SL-B-03's cause is a hypothesis (character exhaustion); the selector's rotation record is not on the wire.
- `scripts/specfactory/pre_gate.sh` was not run: the new files have no owner row (the brief forbade editing `pieces.toml`). They are tooling, never in the game loop.
- Build used `make` and `make pipe/ouroboros_pipe pipe/ouroboros_pipe_dbg` from the worktree; `pipe/` and `*.so` outputs are untracked and not committed.
