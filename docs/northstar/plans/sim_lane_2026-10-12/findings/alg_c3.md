## Proposed Q- rows

- **Q-C3-01 (SL-C3-01), when an ability cooldown starts.** ALG-63 gives Surge 1500, Intercept 600, Barrage 1800, Fortify 1400 without a start point; Brace alone says "from activation". Candidates: (a) every cooldown counts from activation (Surge ready 1500 after the cast, not 1560); (b) every cooldown counts from the end of the effect; (c) keep the built mix (Brace, Fortify, Rally from activation; Surge, Barrage, Intercept from the end).
- **Q-C3-02 (SL-C3-06), the fairness numbers.** ALG-72 has no formulas or thresholds. Candidates: (a) port the Python `evaluate_fairness` formulas and thresholds into the algorithms row verbatim; (b) drop ALG-72 until the terrain-first generator defines its own balance measure; (c) name the four balances and their [0, 1] scales in the bible and leave thresholds to the design phase (Q-113-01 (b)).
- **Q-C3-03 (SL-C3-10), what ALG-77 decays.** Candidates: (a) the history weight of each previous lineage in the ALG-47 previous pool is multiplied by 0.75 per generation since that lineage last supplied a commander (an added input to the score); (b) ALG-77 is the rotation-chance decay already built (0.75 per chapter past the minimum), and the register wording is corrected; (c) both.
