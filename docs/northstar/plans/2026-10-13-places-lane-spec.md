The objective position and both base positions are mission.c's (inputs), never the engine's; the engine moves no spawn [R 4, 5]. Two sides only; four to six players (R-195-01) are not in this lane. `seed` is the mission seed (`mission.c:2718`: `world_seed ^ region·0x9E3779B1 ^ archetype`). Salts are **added** to it, as the terrain adds its own (`terrain.c:545` `seed + TG_SALT_WOBBLE`; its salts stop at 401, :358-368): places `seed + 410`, lot threshold `seed + 420` (P2), lane warp `seed + 430` and `seed + 440` (P2), and for P1 also age `seed + 450` and richness `seed + 460`. `om_tn_fbm2` reads `seed + i` for octave i (`terrain_noise.c:104`), so every stream is `salt + 0..2` and no two streams, ours or the terrain's, share a seed for any mission seed. (The ruling [A2 10] kept `seed ^ salt`; an exhaustive check over the low 14 bits of the seed found XOR collisions, e.g. 410 and 420 on octaves 0 and 2 for 128 of 4,096 low-bit patterns, and 420 octave 1 with the terrain's 101; addition has none. The P1 tests also run seeds 1 to 64.)

Algorithm; the canvas is read as a grid of side/100 cells, `h[cell]` = `height_at` at the cell centre, `water[cell]` = `h < 0`, `slope[cell]` as `om_layout_slope` on that grid (an edge cell takes its inner neighbour, as `om_layout_slope` steps inward); the heights are read once (57,600 calls at most), then every score reads the cached grid.
1. **Objective**: kind 0, `side` -1, at (objective_x, objective_y).
2. **Bases**: player then enemy, at the given positions, `side` = base_side[i]. The kind is the E-22 R-177-02 table, verbatim, a rule not a draft (archetype ids `mission.c:161-170`; "home" rows are BASE_HOME, camps BASE_FORWARD): 0 Defensive Stand: player HOME, enemy FORWARD; 1 Siege Breaking: FORWARD (relief camp), FORWARD (siege camp); 2 Reconquest: FORWARD, HOME (the city being taken); 3 Assault: FORWARD, HOME; 4 Suppression: HOME (garrison in a city), FORWARD (hidden cells); 5 Multi-Front Defense: HOME, FORWARD; 6 Interception: FORWARD (field camp), FORWARD (staging camp); 7 Evacuation: HOME (the place being left), FORWARD (pursuing base); 8 Containment: FORWARD, FORWARD; 9 Counterinsurgency: HOME (garrison in a city), FORWARD (hidden cells). HOME bases get `age_steps` = `DRAFT-home-age` 40; FORWARD bases 0.
3. **The score** `s` of a candidate (cells whose indices are 1 mod 4, so a 400 wu grid, `DRAFT-places-grid`; a candidate is valid when its cell is not water and `walkable_at` is true at its centre): `s = 0.45·water_within + 0.30·flatness + 0.25·noise01` (`DRAFT-places-weights`). `water_within = min(1, n/12)`, n = water cells whose centre is within 600 wu (`DRAFT-places-water-radius`; 12 = `DRAFT-places-water-sat`). `flatness` = share of cells within 300 wu (`DRAFT-places-flat-radius`) whose slope is under 0.06 (`DRAFT-places-flat-slope`). `noise01 = (om_tn_fbm2(seed + 410, x_q16, y_q16, 3, 32768) + 65536) / 131072` with `x_q16 = (int32)((int64)x_wu · 65536 / 1600)` (one lattice unit = 1,600 wu, `DRAFT-places-noise-lattice`; octaves 3, gain 0.5: `DRAFT-places-noise-octaves`). R-193-01's rivers, coasts and flats drive it; there is no river mask on main, so water within 600 wu stands in for a river or a coast.
4. **Resource-hosting towns first** (R-191-04, Q-191-03, R-177-06; "opposite the objective", Westley 2026-09-23, means one each side of the objective along the line level between the bases). Let `B0`, `B1` be the two bases, `d` the unit vector perpendicular to `B1-B0`, `O` the objective, `t(p) = (p-O)·d`, `D(p) = |p-B0| - |p-B1|` (the equidistance error). For each side σ in (-1, +1): the candidate set is every valid candidate with `σ·t(p) >= 2400` (`DRAFT-resource-clearance`) and `|D(p)| <= 600` (`DRAFT-resource-fairness`), at least 4000 wu (`DRAFT-places-spacing`) from both bases, and whose satellite point `q = p + σ·300·d` (`DRAFT-satellite-offset`) is inside the canvas, not water, walkable, with `|D(q)| <= 600`. Take the candidate with the greatest `s` (no floor: the player always has the means, R-177-06). It becomes a NEUTRAL_SETTLEMENT at p, and a RESOURCE_SITE at q. If `|B1-B0| < 1` or the set is empty for a side, that side gets none (the P1 fixture asserts both found; `DRAFT-resource-sites-min` 2).
5. **Other neutral settlements by score** ("nothing hard coded"): repeatedly take the valid candidate with the greatest `s` that is at least 4000 wu (`DRAFT-places-spacing`) from every base, the objective and every town chosen, while `s > 0.35` (`DRAFT-places-floor`), the settlements and HOME bases together number fewer than `DRAFT-towns-max` 6 [A2 8] and the place list has room (`n + 2 <= 64`). The land and the floor decide the count up to the maximum; the maximum keeps the lot quota and the pre-growth cost bounded.
6. **Ages**: a NEUTRAL_SETTLEMENT's `age_steps = 10 + (30·u_q16 + 32768) / 65536` (integer division), `u_q16 = (om_tn_fbm2(seed + 450, x_q16, y_q16, 3, 32768) + 65536) / 2` at the town's position: 10 to 40 (`DRAFT-town-age-min` 10, `DRAFT-town-age-max` 40: the front radius at pre-growth is 1,000 to 4,000 wu, and the lot quota bounds a town long before the front dies, about hop 195), so towns run from hamlet to town [A2 1].
7. **Satellites**: every settlement that hosts no resource site gets an EXPANSION_SITE at 300 wu, on the first of the four axis steps (E, S, W, N, starting at index `(u_q16 >> 14) & 3` of its age noise) that lands inside the canvas on walkable, non-water ground; none if none does. Satellites are exempt from the spacing rule.
8. **Richness** of a RESOURCE_SITE: `round(100·clamp(0.2 + 0.8·region_economy·(0.5 + n), 0, 1))`, n = `noise01` with `seed + 460` at the site (`DRAFT-richness-formula`; sometimes limited, sometimes abundant, scaled by the region's economy, R-177-06). Defender slant: when one base is BASE_HOME, the resource site nearer that base gets +10 (`DRAFT-defender-slant`), capped at 100. 0 for every other kind.
9. **Order** of `out->places`: objective, player base, enemy base, towns in the order chosen (resource-hosting towns first), then satellites in the order of their towns (a resource site directly follows its town's turn). Cap `OM_PLACE_CAP` 64; no sort, no `qsort`.

Tests (`test_VG_P_p1_layout.py`; the stub `layout_stub_terrain.c` exports `void om_stub_terrain(int32_t kind, om_layout_terrain *out)` with kinds: 0 flat, 1 lake and cliff, 2 rolling (heights from `om_tn_fbm2`, water where below 0), 3 all water; a Python `ctypes.Structure` mirrors `om_layout_terrain` and `om_place`; no Python callbacks): same inputs twice give byte-equal place lists; two seeds differ (flat stub, so only the noise differs); the R-177-02 table for all ten archetypes, both sides; no two settlement or base places within 4,000 wu (satellites exempt, [R 8]); at most 6 settlements and HOME bases in all; the determinism, spacing, no-water and resource-site checks run for seeds 1 to 64; no place on water or unwalkable ground on the lake-and-cliff stub, satellites included; each RESOURCE_SITE has `|D| <= 600`, they sit on opposite sides of the objective (`σ·t` of opposite signs, each at least 2,400 wu from it), each is 300 wu (+-1) from a NEUTRAL_SETTLEMENT, and the site nearer the HOME base has +10 over the same seed run with an archetype with no HOME base (economy 0.2 so nothing clamps); ages: HOME 40, FORWARD 0, neutral in [10, 40], other kinds 0; richness in [0, 100] and the mean at economy 1.0 greater than at economy 0.2 over 20 seeds; `n <= 64`; runtime under `DRAFT-places-budget` 20 ms on the 24,000 wu flat stub (stub table through the adapter struct [R 12]; the real-terrain time is measured and printed by P3's test, not asserted here); on the rolling 16,000 wu fixture the count of NEUTRAL_SETTLEMENTs is printed and asserted at least 2 [R 11].

### P2 lot-threshold noise, lanes and the growth probe: `csrc/economy_lot_noise.c`, `csrc/lot_noise.h`, `csrc/growth_probe.c`, `tests/csim/test_VG_P_p2_lot_noise_lanes.py`, `tests/csim/test_VG_P_p2_probe_shape.py` (new; owner VG-P2; depends on P1, serially: it includes `layout_internal.h` and uses `om_layout_terrain`)

New code in new files; P3 wires it. There is **no noise on the growth value**: the field keeps today's metric and decay (P3 only replaces `DIAG_DECAY`), so the front is exact and always empties; the ragged outline comes from a per-cell noisy threshold on when a lot may appear. `lot_noise.h` includes `layout_internal.h`.
```c
/* csrc/lot_noise.h */
/* the lot threshold multiplier g of cell (gx, gy), Q16: 45875 .. 91750 (0.7 .. 1.4); a lot may appear when density >= 0.10 * g (R-193-01: a ragged outline at any age) */
uint32_t om_lot_threshold_q16(uint64_t seed, int32_t gx, int32_t gy);
/* the pinned fbm range used for the rescale: lo <= every om_tn_fbm2 result the threshold takes, hi >= every one (Q16, measured) */
void     om_lot_threshold_range(int32_t *lo_q16, int32_t *hi_q16);
/* lane cells. core_cells: n_cores cell indices (side*side grid). level_out[n_cores] 0..3. lane[side*side] 1 = lane. */
void     om_lane_build(const om_layout_terrain *t, uint64_t seed, int32_t side, const uint32_t *core_cells, uint32_t n_cores,
                       uint8_t *lane, uint8_t *level_out);
/* csrc/growth_probe.c (exported for tests, called from no production path, no header): the pre-growth of P3 on a flat, dry, laneless side*side grid with no world.
   core_cells[n_cores], ages[n_cores] (growth steps, 0..400); n_cores 1..6; side 1..240. Economy fixed at 1.0 (DRAFT-probe-economy).
   Outputs, each NULL-able: out_P and out_density (double, side*side, Q16 values as the sim stores them), out_lot_core (uint8, side*side, 255 = no lot, else the creating core),
   out_death_step (the pre-growth step at which the active list emptied, or -1 if it was still active after the last). Returns the number of lots, or -1 on a bad argument. */
int32_t  om_growth_probe_run(uint64_t seed, int32_t side, uint32_t n_cores, const uint32_t *core_cells, const int32_t *ages,
                             double *out_P, double *out_density, uint8_t *out_lot_core, int32_t *out_death_step);
```
- `om_lot_threshold_q16`: `f = om_tn_fbm2(seed + 420, gx·8192, gy·8192, 3, 32768)` (octaves 3, gain 32768 = 0.5, `DRAFT-lot-threshold-octaves`; lattice coordinate = cell index × 8192, so one lattice unit is 8 cells = 800 wu, `DRAFT-lot-threshold-lattice`); `v_q16 = clamp(((int64)(f - lo) * 65536) / (hi - lo), 0, 65536)`; result `45875 + ((v_q16 * 45875) >> 16)` (`DRAFT-lot-threshold-noise` 0.7..1.4). At today's decay a 40 percent swing of the threshold moves the outline by about 35 hops on a mature field and warps a young one by the same scale. `lo`, `hi` (`DRAFT-lot-threshold-range`) are the min and max of `om_tn_fbm2` over 10,000 cells that the P2 test measures; the source pins them with slack `DRAFT-noise-range-slack` 0.05 of the span, and the test asserts `lo <= measured_min`, `measured_max <= hi` and that each pin is within the slack of the measured value.
- `om_lane_build` replaces `gx%4==3 || gy%4==3` (`economy_density.c:106`; R-193-09, streets along contour lines and rivers). Per cell, at lane level L (0..3): **contour** lane when `floor(h/(150·2^L))` (`DRAFT-lane-contour` 150 wu, from the cell-centre height) differs between the cell and its east neighbour (gx+1) or its neighbour at gy+1; **water** lane when any of its eight neighbours is water (`om_layout_is_water`; no river mask exists, so water adjacency stands in); **fallback** lane (`DRAFT-lane-fallback`) when no contour or water lane cell lies within 4 cells (Chebyshev): `floor_mod(gx + wx, 4·2^L) == 4·2^L - 1` or `floor_mod(gy + wy, 4·2^L) == 4·2^L - 1`, with the warp `wx = floor_div(3·fx + 32768, 65536)`, `fx = om_tn_fbm2(seed + 430, gx·8192, gy·8192, 2, 32768)`, `wy` the same with `seed + 440` (a smooth warp of up to +-3 cells, so the grid wanders with the ground). Level per cell = the level of its nearest core (Chebyshev; ties to the lower index). **Cap**: a core's level is the lowest L in 0..3 for which lane cells are at most 30 percent (`DRAFT-lane-share`) of the non-water cells in the 41 x 41 window around its core cell (`DRAFT-lane-window` 20 cells); each doubling doubles the contour interval and the fallback period together. The result depends on terrain, seed and core cells only, never on growth, so a save and a load rebuild the same lanes. Heights come through the adapter at cell centres.
- `om_growth_probe_run` is a copy of the hop loop, the per-core maturity and economy, the pre-growth schedule and the lot rule of P3 (below), on a grid where every cell is dry flat ground: `factor = 0.99 · 0.99 (NONWATER_FAR, economy_density.c:34) · [0.986 on a diagonal hop]`, the accept test `v > P[j] + 0.0005 and v > 0.02`, the Q16 truncation, and the lot rule `density >= 0.10 · g` in Euclidean ring order with the quota. It is the prototype of the shape: the shape test runs on the real C noise before any held file is touched, and P3's `economy_density.c` must match it byte for byte on flat ground (a P3 test compares them).

Tests.
