# A7: pipe cost of one tactical_step at 1,000 and 5,000 units

Measurement only. No contract change, no sim or pipe edit. Base: origin/main 0b49e09, `-O2` build, 16-core box, 2026-10-01.

## How measured

- Script: `scripts/bench_tactical_pipe.py N [steps] [spread|clash]` (needs `make pipe/ouroboros_pipe`; run from repo root).
- No pipe verb spawns units (`place_structure` makes 64 max, `rng_force` absent). So script builds throwaway LD_PRELOAD shim: wraps `om_mission_begin` to spawn N extra units, wraps `om_tactical_step` and `om_player_can_see_unit` to time them. Pipe binary untouched, so frame code is the real one.
- Mission (seed 1, 80 regions, faction 0) already holds 572 units. Spawned 428 and 4428 extra: frames hold exactly 1,000 and 5,000 units. Units alternate faction 0/1, types 0..5.
- Two layouts. `spread`: uniform over 16000 square, little contact. `clash`: two opposing 2500-wide blobs, heavy combat. Clash is the realistic worst case.
- 60 steps per run, one `tactical_step n=1` per request. Roundtrip = request written to full frame read (python, pipe). Serialise+io = roundtrip minus shim-timed `om_tactical_step` (includes pipe write/read, small).
- Sim-only = ctypes call of `om_tactical_step` on same world, same spawn, no pipe (same script).
- Client parse: Godot 4.6.3 headless `JSON.new().parse` of dumped frame, 30 runs.

## Results (ms, 60 steps)

| units | layout | frame bytes | roundtrip med / p95 / max | sim step (shim) med / p95 / max | serialise+io med / p95 | serialise share | sim only (ctypes) med / p95 / max |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1,000 | spread | 139,241 | 8.9 / 11.8 / 23.9 | 1.8 / 2.8 / 16.8 | 7.1 / 8.2 | 80% | 1.4 / 2.3 / 15.2 |
| 1,000 | clash | 140,499 | 8.9 / 10.7 / 24.1 | 1.9 / 2.7 / 16.7 | 6.9 / 8.1 | 78% | 1.6 / 2.7 / 16.5 |
| 5,000 | spread | 769,097 | 35.8 / 56.5 / 111.4 | 3.9 / 9.8 / 73.0 | 32.1 / 47.8 | 90% | 3.0 / 5.9 / 57.5 |
| 5,000 | clash | 753,897 | 82.2 / 113.5 / 123.2 | 25.9 / 34.7 / 65.9 | 56.6 / 74.0 | 69% | 28.2 / 35.0 / 93.5 |

Noise: run-to-run medians moved about 10% (first 5,000 spread run: 39 ms; second: 36 ms). Max values are one-off spikes (first steps, page faults), p95 is the stable tail.

Frame is about 154 bytes per unit. Serialise+io at 5,000 is about 6 to 7 us per unit in spread.

Where serialise goes at 5,000 (shim timing of `om_player_can_see_unit`, called once per unit per faction key for `visible{}`):

| layout | serialise+io med | in `visible{}` | rest (JSON build, per-unit accessors, snprintf `%.17g`, fwrite) |
|---|---|---|---|
| spread | 32 ms | 4.6 ms | about 27 ms |
| clash | 57 ms | 29.8 ms | about 27 ms |

So the frame body costs about 27 ms flat at 5,000 units. `visible{}` adds 5 ms to 30 ms depending on how much fog work the contact needs (`om_fog_faction_sees`). That cost is real in real fights and is not a JSON cost.

## Budget check (plan: step under 50 ms at 5,000 units)

- Spread: roundtrip median 36 ms, p95 56 ms. Passes on median, fails p95.
- Clash: roundtrip median 82 ms, p95 113 ms. Fails. Even sim-only is 28 ms median, 35 ms p95: sim alone eats 56 to 70% of 50 ms.
- 1,000 units: 9 ms. Passes with room.
- The 50 ms assumes 20 calls/s. The client already runs 10 calls/s above 1,000 live units (R-099-03, `scenes/tactical_c/tactical_view.gd` `TACTICAL_HIGH_LOAD_*`; each call spans 2 base ticks). Budget at that rate is 100 ms per call. Clash 5,000 median 82 ms fits under 100 ms but p95 113 ms does not, and that is before client work below. Westley should say which budget he means: 50 ms (20 calls/s, would need period dropped) or 100 ms (what the client does now).

## Client side

- Frame 750 KB wire (820 KB re-dumped with spaces) at 5,000 units, 140 KB at 1,000. Reader cap is 10 MB (`autoload/c_bridge.gd` `_read_live_frame`), no risk.
- Godot `JSON.parse`: 7 ms median at 1,000 units, 34 ms median (p95 38) at 5,000 units. Runs on the reader thread (`_reader_loop`), so it overlaps with the next sim step but adds latency: pipe 82 + parse 34 = about 116 ms per call in a clash at 5,000, unpipelined.
- Client parses EVERY frame. `tactical_step n=k` makes the pipe write k full frames (`handle_tactical_step` loops `write_tactical`); client parses all k, keeps only the last (`tactical_step_n`). `qa_step(k)` in `_process` asks k up to 10 per render frame after a stall, so a catch-up burst wastes (k-1) full frames of build, write, and parse. In steady state k is about 1 per render frame at 60 fps (10 to 20 calls/s), so this is mostly latent.
- Not measured: main-thread cost of `load_frame` and `_live_unit_count` (scans `units[]` every `_process`), and rendering. Needs a desktop run.

## Conclusion

Serialisation dominates the step in calm scenes (80% at 1,000, 90% at 5,000 spread: 32 ms of 36). It does NOT dominate in a real fight at 5,000: sim step is 28 ms and serialise+io 57 ms, and half of that serialise time is `visible{}` fog evaluation, not JSON. In a clash both halves are too big for 50 ms, and the sim step alone is over half of it.

So a delta frame (`units_changed[]` plus periodic full frame) is justified but is not sufficient for the 50 ms target:
- Delta cuts the 27 ms body build, the write, and client parse (34 ms) in scenes where few units change. In a clash nearly every unit moves each tick, so delta saves little exactly when load is worst.
- The biggest single non-sim cost in a clash is `visible{}` (30 ms): per-unit per-faction fog test every frame. It is separate from the units list.
- Sim step (28 ms at 5,000 in combat) needs its own look; not profiled here (perf not permitted on this box).

## Recommended next pieces (not implemented)

1. Contract piece first (docs/architecture.md): delta tactical frame. Make it cheap on the wire as well: send `visible` only as changes (or only the player's faction key, since the client reads one), drop never-changing fields (`type`, `tier`, `state` are constant 0), full frame every N steps. Then `T1-N`, `C5-N` per plan.
2. Cheap win independent of delta: compute `visible` for the player faction only (client reads `visible[player]`); halves the fog calls and removes the enemy-faction key. Needs the contract to say the other keys are not guaranteed. Saves up to about 15 ms in a clash.
3. Pipe: when `tactical_step n>1`, write only the last frame (or every kth), since `tactical_step_n` discards the rest. Contract change, small.
4. Profile the sim step at 5,000 in combat (gprof build or a dbg timer) before deciding a sim fix; `om_tac_refresh_positions`, `om_tactical_ai_tick` and `tactical_move.c` are the first suspects. Needs a box where perf is allowed.
5. Westley to confirm the budget (50 ms at 20 calls/s, or 100 ms at the 10 calls/s the client already uses above 1,000 units) before anything is built.
6. A desktop measurement of main-thread `load_frame` at 5,000 units (not covered here).
