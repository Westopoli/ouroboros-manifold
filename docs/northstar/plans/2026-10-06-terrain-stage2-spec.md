Written 2026-10-06 from the two stage-2 pre-flight scouts (routing and roster seams; vision and era-kind seams). Rests on: the walkers mockup approved final ("approved, no need for any more iteration, can be integrated as is"), the landscape engine approved, terrain stage 1 on `push/terrain-land` (the field, bands, walkable cells, slopes, ground as blocked bits, pipe verbs), and Westley's answers of 2026-10-06: slope speed 1.1x slower up and 1.1x faster down (R-004-07), a unit on a slope counts as the band on its side of the midline (Q-005-05), eye height and the flyer's seen-from distance are lab drafts (the latter longer than its own sight), era kind is a pack field (Q-146-04 a). ## Scope

Two pieces behind one contract piece. **2a Walkers:** a movement kind per unit type; walkers stand only on the ground's band, route by least time over walkable cells and slopes, change layer at a slope's midline by position, never take a timed transfer, and spawn on the band under them. **2b Ground vision and era kind:** an era kind (space 0, ground 1) stored in the world; in a ground era a lower unit never sees a higher one, a higher unit sees down within its sensor radius unless ground stands in the line, a flyer sees down and is seen from farther than it sees, vantage nodes and the elevation bonus do nothing, and the ping is refused. Not in stage 2: the pipe and client (stage 3), missions calling the generator (stage 3), seer and scout units (E-11 S-201, their own piece), swimming (declared, not routed), slopes spanning more than two layers (one boundary per slope, as stage 1 detects them; Q-STAGE2-01 below), walker layer orders (refused, R-001-07's "paths to the slope" half is Q-STAGE2-02).

The space model is the default: with era kind 0 and every type flying, nothing in play changes, and no recorded hash may move.

## Contract piece (lands first; one agent; `push/terrain2-base` from `push/terrain-land` after it lands on main)

`signatures.toml` then `sig_table.py --emit`; every new public symbol listed here and no other:

- Enum `om_movement_kind` in the header in the form of the existing enums: `OM_MOVE_FLYING = 0, OM_MOVE_WALKING = 1, OM_MOVE_SWIMMING = 2`.
- `int om_roster_set_movement_kind(om_world *w, int type, int kind)` (0 ok; nonzero for an unknown type or kind), `int om_unit_profile_movement_kind(const om_world *w, int type)` (module `unit.c`). Stored in the roster, not in move state: a per-type array for the six built-in types (default flying) and a field in the custom-type row; hashed in the units section and saved with the roster (reach is neither saved nor hashed today; a kind must be, or a save loses walkers).
- `int om_set_era_kind(om_world *w, int kind)`, `int om_era_kind(const om_world *w)` (module `fog.c`; stored in fog state beside the era flags; hashed and saved with them; default 0).
- `uint32_t om_terrain_walk_mask(const om_world *w, int layer, uint8_t *out, uint32_t cap)` (module `terrain.c`): one byte per cell, 1 where a walker may stand on that layer (R-200-02: the band's own layer, and both layers of a slope cell). `int om_terrain_slope_at(const om_world *w, double x, double y, OMSlope *out)` (the slope whose run covers the position, else -1). `int om_terrain_sight_blocks(const om_world *w, double x0, double y0, double x1, double y1)` (ground between two eye points at `OM_TP_SIGHT_EYE_HEIGHT` above the terrain blocks the line: sample every quarter cell; 0 when no terrain is present).
- Tactical parameters appended last, in this order, after the current last member: `OM_TP_SLOPE_UP_DIV`, `OM_TP_SLOPE_DOWN_MULT`, `OM_TP_SIGHT_EYE_HEIGHT`, `OM_TP_GROUND_FLYER_SEEN_MULT`; `pp_contract.md` rows, `conftest.py` members, the parameter-count tests (ledger row).
- A ping refusal reason for a ground era (next value after the four in `fog.c`).
- `csrc/om_internal.h`: `om_move_walker_tick(om_world *w)` (the walker pass), `om_walk_route(const om_world *w, uint32_t unit, double gx, double gy, double *pts, uint32_t cap, double *seconds)` (the planner), the per-layer walk mask arrays and the per-type kind array on the structs that hold them; `csrc/walk_route.c` created with a stub.
- Era pack field: `assets/eras/*/manifest.json` gains `"kind": "space"` (settled_reach, test_era) or `"ground"`; `autoload/era_config.gd` reads it (client side only; the pipe does not send it in stage 2). Document the field in `docs/architecture.md` where the manifest is described.
- Checks as stage 1: build with `-Werror`, `sig_table --check`, lints, csim ids equal to main's apart from the count tests, transport, net, the two traces.

## Open questions this spec leaves (register rows on approval)

- Q-STAGE2-01: slopes spanning more than two layers (R-001-06 cross-overs) are not detected by stage 1 and not routed here; candidates: (a) stage 1 detects multi-boundary runs later; (b) a long slope is several adjacent two-layer slopes.
- Q-STAGE2-02: a walker ordered to another layer: (a) refused (this spec); (b) R-001-07's "paths to the slope and walks it" (a later piece on top of the planner).
- Q-STAGE2-03: which wins when the sim's 8-neighbour Dijkstra and the mockup's A* differ in detail: (a) the sim is the reference once Westley has seen walkers on the ported ground (stage 3 look); (b) match the mockup cell for cell.
- Q-STAGE2-04: the swimming kind: declared in the enum, routed as walking on "water" cells once water exists in the field (stage 1 clamps water to height 0).

## Limits, stop and kill rules

As stage 1. Stop on a recorded hash that moves, a symbol not listed here, a test that looks wrong, a file outside the lane, a number nobody gave. The run stops if the contract fails its gate or two lanes are dropped. Builders never edit tests.
