## Patterns

1. **Ruling to spec: the spec resolves the rule narrower than you said.** Row 28 (growth noise), row 6 (objective placement) and row 3 (vantage). One sentence: the spec author should quote the ruling beside each interface and say what was dropped.
2. **Register rows that contradict themselves.** Rows 25, 27, 29 and 8. One sentence: a closed row needs a single final answer, and an "open, default x" row must name the build that waits.
3. **Ruling to epic.** The home-base framing (row 14) and the lab penalty (row 33) live only in specs or READMEs. One sentence: a ruling is folded into the rule text in the same commit.
4. **Brief to shot: the shot was not made to be judged.** Rows 4, 11, 12 and 20 (no legend, no unit, taken before the fixes, too zoomed out). One sentence: every batch row gives a legend, the unit and the date of the run.
5. **Status copies.** Rows 17, 21, 26 and 32. One sentence: a verdict is folded into the queue cell, the README, `stories.md` and the lane table together.

Drift clusters in lanes P (places and the terrain prototype), A and B, and in the status copies. Lanes C, F and V show shot-batch problems, not decision problems.
