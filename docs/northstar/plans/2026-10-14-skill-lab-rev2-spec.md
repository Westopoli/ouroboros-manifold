## Context

The skill lab (`scripts/skilllab/`, record `docs/northstar/research/layer_dynamics/README.md`) exists to find the rule settings at which a skilled player beats a much larger force, above all for cross-layer vision and fire. On 2026-10-13 an audit and three independent proposals found that its "able to fire" count ignores other layers and vision, that the firing count was never compared with the result, that the three-knob sweep Westley ordered on 2026-10-01 ran on same-layer scenes and produced identical fights, that run 1 scored fights by invented end rules, and that two attack-moved groups stall out of range because the engine ends a group's attack-move after its first engagement. Westley ruled on every open point the same day (README section 4 "Rulings of 2026-10-13"; register rows Q-LAB-03, Q-096-01, Q-100-01, Q-082-06; E-13 R-096-02; E-01 S-164). This workflow builds the lab those rulings describe, with tests that enforce the rules and the scale the lab must run at, and ends with the first coarse knob batch run and its analysis artifact in Westley's hands.

## Rulings in force (Westley, 2026-10-13; binding; an agent never re-decides one)

1. A fight ends by elimination only. A tick cap stays as a bug detector; a capped fight is a lab defect to trace, never a result. Hold timer, hold radius, blind window, draw band, silence window, quiet-from-first-damage, mop-up, re-issue and the approach opening are retired (register Q-LAB-03).
2. Same units, same values, both sides, always. A skilled play differs only in arrangement, orders and which roster types stand where. The only asymmetry is a labelled positive control (the planted 20 percent damage scan).
3. Naive baseline for a defending play reported both ways: against a naive attacker and against a naive defender at the same post. DRAFT (Architect): for a layer play the naive defender runs twice, on the attacker's layer and on the play's layer.
4. Scan range: enemy headcount 8 to 48 against 16. A play that beats 48 is a ceiling hit, never "no gain".
5. The measure: the result is who was left standing; the firing count predicts who should have won; two predictions per fight, count-only and type-weighted; the weight is the category multiplier of the attacker's category against its target's category and nothing else (never damage, interval, health or a cross-layer damage multiplier); the target of record, per unit per sample, is the engine's target if `can_engage` against it is 0, else the nearest living hostile enemy with `can_engage` 0, else the nearest living hostile enemy (so the `states` code carries the first failing reason; -1 only when no living hostile exists); the count-only prediction counts units with code 0; the weight is summed over units with code 0 only, an unable unit contributes 0 (ruled 2026-10-13 for the L1 tests). A mismatch is a finding. The opening window after first contact is the primary prediction form (DRAFT-opening-window-ticks 40); whole-fight totals stay as a chart.
6. Attack-move, group or lone, is not finished until the unit reaches its point; it resumes after every engagement (R-096-02 rewritten; the group-end clause was never Westley's). Contact architecture (approved): a meeting is both sides attack-moved to one point; an assault is the attacker attack-moved onto the holder's centre of mass (the mean of the holder's placed positions, on the holder's layer) while the holder guards; each side keeps its own layer; the pre-flight refuses a cell whose units do not fit, whose path is not routable, or whose types cannot fire at the holder's offset. With the resume rule a blind cross-layer cell still meets at the point, so the vision family runs its whole band.
7. Guard is a radius guard (R-096-08, `GUARD_RADIUS` 200 wu DRAFT): engages what its side sees inside the radius of its post, returns, never chases past it. The built guard holds and never steps; piece E4 builds the step-and-return; lab tests on the guard are written against the built order and re-run after E4.
8. Firing arc (Q-100-01): a unit fires only at targets inside an arc about its facing; width is one knob for every type (DRAFT-firing-arc-deg 30, Westley 2026-10-19 "#5 yes 30 degrees": a unit fires only within 15 degrees either side of its facing; 360 is today's behaviour and the control). The arc gates the shot only, never acquisition or the hold: a flanked unit acquires, stops, turns at the R-100-02 rate (the existing face pass), and fires once the target is inside. DRAFT (Architect, replaces draft (a); Westley to change): this is what "turns at the R-100-02 rate" means.
9. Cone area damage (Q-082-06): a circular sector from the attacker about its facing, attachable to any type; range, half-angle and damage share are knobs (DRAFT-aoe-cone-range 300, DRAFT-aoe-cone-deg 45, DRAFT-aoe-cone-share 1.0); off when range is 0. The Bombard's disc (R-084-03) stays until a cone is set on it.
10. Cross-layer vision: symmetric in space (R-005-05), asymmetric on ground (R-005-09). Cross-layer fire applies in ground eras too (S-164 widened). Emitting is a swept knob (Westley's start: sight x2.0, seen x3.0; seen always above sight, R-163-01). Firing up is a penalty: multipliers sweep from 1.0 down to 0.25; one point above 1.0 only as a labelled sign-flip control.
11. No absurd outliers: every sweep starts at the bible's value (or Westley's start), steps by a named factor inside 0.25x to 4x (clipped where the engine refuses), three to five points, coarse first, refined only around a crossing the coarse grid showed.
12. Output rule (new; L7 writes it into README section 5): the lab's output is data (CSV, JSON, run metadata). Claude analyses the data. The artifact Westley sees shows the notable layouts, matches and configurations as top-down diagrams with arrows marking the evidence for each assertion, explains what the data means, and above all where the lines cross. A number without its diagram is not evidence.
13. Scale marks (DRAFT, Westley's): one 16 v 48 fight under 1.5 s on one worker; one play scan 8 to 48 at 3 replicates under 90 s at 12 workers; the first coarse knob batch under 4 h wall at 12 workers; rows byte-identical at 1 and 12 workers.
14. Runs: the smoke run at landing and the first coarse knob batch are automatic; Westley reads the analysis artifact.
15. Unit size (Q-LAB-02 (a), Westley 2026-10-06): the runtime collision radius is the body size the sim uses. Architect 2026-10-13 under that ruling: it reaches the collision pass, the steering probe, the navmesh margins and the arrival tolerances (every place the bible ties to the radius); spawn spacing and the broad-phase grid layout stay compile-time. This widens A133 (Q-C6, "collision pass only") and `pp_contract.md` line 78 is rewritten by E3.

- `int om_roster_set_cone(om_world *w, int unit_type, double range, double half_angle_deg, double share);` module `tactical_move.c` beside `om_roster_set_reach`; returns 0 ok, -1 unknown type, -2 refused value (range < 0, half-angle outside [1, 180], share outside [0, 10]); range 0 clears the cone. Stores per type: range, half-angle, share and `cos(half-angle)` computed once with the engine's own trig (`om_cos` where the module has it, else `om_sin(x + pi/2)`; never libm trig). Read by `unit.c` through an `om_internal.h` accessor.
- `int om_combat_can_engage(const om_world *w, uint32_t attacker, uint32_t target);` module `unit.c`. Returns -1 for an unknown id, a dead attacker or target, the same or a neutral faction, attacker equal to target, or a non-combat attacker (Supply, Evacuation); else the first failing reason in this diagnostic order (the engine's gates are a conjunction; the order is for reading, not the engine's): 1 out of reach (type reach at the layer offset), 2 not seen by the side (target stealthed, or `OM_TP_TARGET_BY_SIGHT` on and the side's set, which includes live ping reveals, does not hold it), 3 out of range at this offset (attack range times the offset multiplier), 4 outside the arc (only when the arc is below 360), 5 line blocked (`om_combat_can_shoot`); 0 when every gate passes. It ignores cooldown, backswing, the cohort slot and the order kind: it answers "could fire this tick if ready", and that is what "able to fire" means. Parity is one-way: after tick t, every fire event of tick t has `can_engage` 0, or -1 when either unit of the pair is dead after the tick; 0 never implies a shot.
- ABI and save format: the parameter is hashed into the net section hash and written in the save slot (`tactical_move.c` around 3425, 3528, 3665), so E0 is both an ABI bump and a save-format bump. E0 lands after `VG-CONTRACT-E7` (ABI 7 to 8) and `VG-W2` (landed, save 11) and takes the next free number of each at its landing (settled with Architect fc 2026-10-13: numbers are taken at landing in gate order, never pre-claimed; the branch and its pins say "next free at landing" and the clerk writes the actual numbers; the E0 branch name goes to the visible-game Dispatcher so it queues right behind E7); it rewrites every pin: `tests/csim/conftest.py` (`EXPECTED_ABI_VERSION`), `tests/csim/test_E02_S020_abi.py`, `tests/csim/test_VG_E_contract.py`, `tests/csim/test_VG_E_ke5.py`, the save-format pins the W2 landing leaves, `docs/architecture.md` (the hello frame's `abi`), `docs/northstar/spec_factory/signatures.toml` (`abi_version`, two new symbol rows with `module`, the symbol count).
