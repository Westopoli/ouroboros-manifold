# Skill lab rev 2: close the measure's blind spots and add the 2026-10-13 rules

Spec for a Sonnet 5.5 overseer. Method: tests first by a different agent than the builder, one shard per disjoint file set, builders in worktrees, a fresh auditor per shard before any build, pre-gate before every push, one landing clerk, STOP rules in every brief, one report per agent (manager-mode shape, repository method rev 2). Westley answers open questions through the Architect chat; the overseer never guesses a rule or a number.

Approved by Westley on 2026-10-13 (plan file `lovely-purring-wozniak.md`; the plan and this spec are handed to the overseer together). Owner row `LAB-REV2` holds this file; the records step of the workflow adds the shard rows before any build. Audit status is recorded in the "Audit and pre-flight" section at the end.

## Context

The skill lab (`scripts/skilllab/`, record `docs/northstar/research/layer_dynamics/README.md`) exists to find the rule settings at which a skilled player beats a much larger force, above all for cross-layer vision and fire. On 2026-10-13 an audit and three independent proposals (`docs/northstar/research/layer_dynamics/proposals_2026-10-13/`, read `synthesis.md` first) found that its "able to fire" count ignores other layers and ignores vision, that the firing count was never compared with the result, that the three-knob sweep Westley ordered on 2026-10-01 ran on same-layer scenes and produced identical fights, that run 1 scored fights by invented end rules, and that two attack-moved groups stall out of range because the engine ends a group's attack-move after its first engagement. Westley ruled on every open point the same day; the rulings are on main (README section 4 "Rulings of 2026-10-13", register rows Q-LAB-03, Q-096-01, Q-100-01, Q-082-06, E-13 R-096-02, E-01 S-164). This workflow builds the lab that those rulings describe, with tests that enforce the rules and the scale the lab must run at, and ends with the first coarse knob batch running and its analysis artifact in Westley's hands.

## Rulings in force (Westley, 2026-10-13; binding, never re-decided by an agent)

1. A fight ends by elimination only. A tick cap stays as a bug detector; a capped fight is a lab defect to trace, never a result. Hold timer, hold radius, blind window, draw band, silence window, mop-up: retired.
2. Same units, same values, both sides, always. A skilled play differs only in arrangement, orders and which roster types stand where. The only asymmetry is a labelled positive control (the planted 20 percent damage scan).
3. Naive baseline for a defending play reported both ways: against a naive attacker and against a naive defender at the same post. For a layer play the naive defender runs twice, on the attacker's layer and on the play's layer.
4. Scan range: enemy headcount 8 to 48 against 16. A play that beats 48 is a ceiling hit, never "no gain".
5. The measure: the result is who was left standing; the firing count predicts who should have won; two predictions per fight, count-only and type-weighted; the weight is the category multiplier of the attacker's category against its target's category and nothing else; the target of record is the engine's target when it is eligible, else the nearest eligible enemy. A mismatch is a finding. The opening window after first contact is the primary prediction form (40 ticks, DRAFT-opening-window-ticks); whole-fight totals stay as a chart.
6. Attack-move, group or lone, is not finished until the unit reaches its point; it resumes after every engagement (R-096-02 rewritten). Contact architecture (approved): a meeting is both sides attack-moved to one point; an assault is the attacker attack-moved onto the holder's centre of mass while the holder guards; the point is on the enemy's layer and each side keeps its own layer; pre-flight refuses a cell whose units do not fit, whose path is not routable, or whose types cannot fire at the holder's offset.
7. Guard is a radius guard (R-096-08, `GUARD_RADIUS` 200 wu draft): engages what its side sees inside the radius of its post, returns, never chases past it.
8. Firing arc (Q-100-01): a unit fires only at targets inside an arc about its facing; width is a knob (DRAFT-firing-arc-deg 120; 360 is today's behaviour and the control); the flanked unit turns at the R-100-02 rate and counts as able once the target is inside (draft (a), Westley to change).
9. Cone area damage (Q-082-06): a wedge from the attacker about its facing with a rounded wide end (a circular sector), attachable to any type; range, half-angle and damage share are knobs (DRAFT-aoe-cone-range 300, DRAFT-aoe-cone-deg 45, DRAFT-aoe-cone-share 1.0); off when range is 0. The Bombard's disc (R-084-03) stays until a cone is set on it.
10. Cross-layer vision: symmetric in space (R-005-05), asymmetric on ground (R-005-09). Cross-layer fire applies in ground eras too (S-164 widened). Emitting is a swept knob (sight x2.0, seen x3.0 start; seen above sight). Firing up is a penalty: multipliers sweep from 1.0 down to 0.25; one point above 1.0 only as a labelled sign-flip control.
11. No absurd outliers: every sweep starts at the bible's value, steps by a named factor inside 0.25x to 4x (clipped where the engine refuses), three to five points, coarse first, refined only around a crossing the coarse grid showed.
12. Output rule (new, goes into README section 5): the lab's output is data (CSV, JSON, run metadata). Claude analyses the data. The artifact Westley sees shows the notable layouts, matches and configurations as top-down diagrams with arrows marking the evidence for each assertion, explains what the data means, and above all where the lines cross. A number without its diagram is not evidence.
13. Scale marks (drafts, Westley's): one 16 v 48 fight under 1.5 s on one worker; one play scan 8 to 48 at 3 replicates under 90 s at 12 workers; the first coarse knob batch under 4 h wall at 12 workers; rows byte-identical at 1 and 12 workers.
14. Runs: the smoke run at landing and the first coarse knob batch are automatic; Westley reads the analysis artifact.

## Contract (frozen before any build; the audit checks it against the code)

### Engine (contract piece E0, lands first)
- New tactical parameters appended before `OM_TP__COUNT` in `csrc/ouroboros_sim.h`, rows in `docs/northstar/plans/push/pp_contract.md` and `docs/northstar/spec_factory/signatures.toml`: `OM_TP_FIRING_ARC_DEG` (real, default 360, lo 10, hi 360), `OM_TP_ATTACK_MOVE_RESUME` (bool, default 1; 0 reproduces today's group end for the parity test only).
- New symbols: `int om_roster_set_cone(om_world *w, uint32_t type_id, double range, double half_angle_deg, double share)` (range 0 = off); `int om_combat_can_engage(const om_world *w, uint32_t attacker, uint32_t target)` returning 0 ok, 1 out of reach, 2 out of range at this offset, 3 not seen by the side, 4 line blocked, 5 outside the arc; `int om_unit_transition_progress(...)` is already exported and only needs a lab binding.
- The collision radius: `OM_TP_UNIT_COLLISION_RADIUS` already exists; no contract change, behaviour only (E3).

### Lab data contract (`scripts/skilllab/contract.py`)
- `FightResult` keeps `outcome` in {`side0`, `side1`, `draw` (both die on one tick), `cap`}; `held`, `decided`, `stalemate` removed. Adds `firing_window` (tuple per side over DRAFT-opening-window-ticks after `first_contact_tick`), `weighted_unit_seconds` always populated, `weighted_window`, `predicted_count` and `predicted_type` in {`side0`, `side1`, `tie`}, `agreement` in {`both_right`, `count_wrong_type_right`, `both_wrong`, `count_right_type_wrong`}, `states` per sample with a reason code from `om_combat_can_engage`.
- `Setup` loses `decided_by_survivors`, `silence_ticks`, `hold_*`, `mopup*`, `stalemate_ticks`; gains `point` (the shared attack-move point), `orders_per_unit: bool = True` (one attack-move per unit until E1 lands and the parity test passes; then False), `arc_deg`, `cone` per type.
- CSV columns (rows.py): today's columns minus the retired outcomes, plus `firing_window_0/1`, `weighted_unit_seconds_0/1`, `weighted_window_0/1`, `predicted_count`, `predicted_type`, `agreement`, `knob`, `knob_value`, `baseline` in {`naive_attacker`, `naive_defender_attacker_layer`, `naive_defender_play_layer`}, `ceiling_hit`, `prefight` in {`ok`, `no_fit`, `unroutable`, `no_reach`}.
- The count (replaces harness spec clause 10): a living unit is able when some living enemy is seen by the side (`om_player_can_see`, which includes ping reveals), its layer offset is inside the type's reach, its distance is within attack range times the offset's range multiplier, the line of fire is clear, and (when `arc_deg` < 360) the bearing lies inside the arc about the unit's facing. The effective layer of a unit in transition is what `om_unit_get_layer` returns; no special case. Until E0 lands the count is composed from the public queries; after E0 it calls `om_combat_can_engage` and the composed version becomes the parity check.

## Workflow

Agents: overseer (Sonnet 5.5, this spec); per shard one test writer (engineer), one auditor (engineer, fresh, has not seen the writer's or builder's reasoning), one builder (doer); a ruler (engineer) for test-defect stops; one landing clerk (engineer) per landing; one scale test writer; one analysis agent for the artifact (engineer draft, Architect review). Every spawn names its model; sub-agents never spawn; no agent polls a gate; agents report once in the schema below.

Order and limits (at most two build workflows and one landing in flight):
1. Records: `LAB-REV2*` owner rows, this spec on main. Baseline recorded: the three lab controls' golden hashes and `pytest tests/skilllab tests/csim -x -q` on main.
2. Wave 1 (parallel): E0 contract; test writers for E1, E2, E3, L1, L2, S. Audit each test file against this spec and the bible before any build.
3. Wave 2: E1, E3 build (csrc, disjoint); L1 and L2 build (disjoint files). E2 after E0 lands.
4. Wave 3: L3 (after L2), L4 (after L1 to L3), L6 (after L2). E2 build.
5. Wave 4: L5 (after L4), L7 (after everything), S run on the built tree.
6. Landing: one clerk, pre-gate, merge gate with the `LAB-REV2*` ids, status check; the smoke run (3 layouts, 1 vision point, the controls) as part of the landing check, its rows hashed at 1 and 12 workers.
7. The first coarse knob batch at 12 workers in a night slot, `nice 10`, `MemoryMax=6G`, under 4 h: vision family on `s3_layer_above` and the corrected S3 plays, then range-up, then damage-up, then emitting jointly with vision at 0.125 to 0.25, then arc (360, 180, 120, 90, 60) and cone (range 150, 300, 600 by half-angle 22.5, 45, 90) on `s2_concave` and `s2_mass_one_end`; controls in every family.
8. Analysis: an engineer drafts the artifact from the data (CSV, JSON) in the README section 5 form plus the diagrams; the Architect reviews it against the data; Westley reads it. The artifact names where the lines cross, with the diagram behind each claim, and lists the `both_wrong` fights as findings.

STOP rules (every brief): stop and report when a test needs a threshold nobody set, when a rule's meaning is unclear, when a contract symbol is missing, when the pre-gate rejects twice, when a fight caps under the pre-flight checks, when a mark of ruling 13 cannot be met without a new symbol. A STOP goes to the overseer; rule meaning goes to the Architect chat; numbers and pass marks go to Westley through it. Builders never edit tests; a test is changed only by the ruler as a `test-defect` ledger row.

Report schema (one per agent, at the end): `{piece, branch, sha, tests: {red_before: [...], green_after: [...]}, pre_gate: "OK|REJECT <line>", stops: [...], surprises: [...]}`.
