# The current economic system

As decided by Westley through 2026-10-01. This file states what the economy is now; it is rewritten when a decision changes it. His words are verbatim in `README.md`. Nothing here is bible text yet, and nothing here is being built.

His measure of it: about 1.2x to 1.4x a StarCraft 2 economy. The target is 2x to 3x. The gap is what `chess_layer_options.md` is for.

## The seat

- An optional second player runs the economy for a commander, in the paired queue. The two share one tactical map.
- The skill is holding several things in mind at once. Forgetting one puts the pair behind. Nothing on screen lists what the commander needs.
- Speed of hand is worth a small bounded edge, about 1.1x to 1.2x.

## What the economy player runs

- **Three resources**, each gathered in its own way. Classic Age of Empires Online and Age of Empires 3 are the model.
- **Literal workers**, trained at the base, who walk to a source and gather.
- **Economic buildings**, including drop-off points. The farther workers walk to a drop-off, the less they bring in.
- **Building placement and choice.** Lots are limited and what stands beside a building changes what it yields.
- **One population cap** shared by workers and soldiers. It can be upgraded a long way, up to thousands.
- **Production.** The economy player runs the production buildings and makes the units the commander receives. What to make is agreed by talking.
- **Technology choice.**
- **Expansions.** Settlements out on the map can be grown into further bases. The main base never runs dry, but a side that stays home is out-produced and overrun.
- **Shipments from the civilization.** They travel to the base and the side cannot see them on the way. A shipment that does not arrive gives no explanation.

## The map

- Every side has a base of operations it must protect. It is not always a city. It receives resources from elsewhere, generates them on site, or both, depending on the simulation and the mission.
- Economic locations are spread across the map.
- The significant resources sit opposite the objective, so a side picks one or fights for both.
- A neutral populace lives in settlements on the map and is contested. Winning it is not the economy player's job.
- Seasons and weather. Specific regions have specific environmental troubles.

## Long-term scale

Thousands of units and workers on one map, a busy screen, made possible by efficient simulation and visuals. Recorded in the bible hub under "Reference Hardware".
