## Flagged by one reviewer and verified

- **B F-1-1 / F-9-1 (blocker): the chain head 205a is unsatisfiable by construction.** `om_tick_collisions(const om_world *w, ...)` is a const read-back with a signature fence; the brief orders it to write `w->collisions`; no collision hook exists in `om_world_step`; `card_lint` prints `LINT OK`. 205b has the same symbol. Restarting the chain fails on tick one. Consequence: S-039 needs a wiring card and a re-cut (design lane), and lint gains "acceptance mutates state the card's writable symbols cannot reach".
- **A F-9-2: the wave order is inverted.** Wave 1's scheduler consumes closure and a non-cyclic cut that Wave 4 produces. Consequence: ouroboros side first.
- **B F-3-1: the drive-thru rule needs a process model.** The dispatcher is one process per tick under one `flock`; `_intervene` blocks the run for up to 30 minutes of GPU idle inside "BUILDING". Consequence: a GPU-slot process and a CPU-slot process with separate locks; `build.py` split at intervene.
- **B F-4-6: the agy escalator runs as an agent with `--dangerously-skip-permissions --sandbox` and no verified sandbox semantics**; the fence is prose. Consequence: the seat calls model APIs, not an agentic CLI, or the sandbox is verified to deny the filesystem.
- **A F-3-5 / B F-3-6: prompts can exceed `num_ctx` (16,384) on large files; 166 of 169 parked cards have no header slice so the full 38 KB header is sent; overflow is a "crash" candidate that burns an attempt.** Consequence: slice every card at prep; count tokens before the call; overflow is `HARNESS-ERROR`.
- **A F-8-5: the ctypes struct mirrors are a second platform contract nobody gates.** Consequence: `om_abi_layout_check()` exporting sizeof and offsetof; the Windows gate asserts it first.
- **B F-10-1: runway is dimensionally wrong** (stories times minutes per card). Consequence: cards after transcription; stories times mean cards per story before.

## What the audit changes

1. The fast-track's premise was wrong. The GPU is not the bottleneck; the day side is. About 20 buildable cards exist. A factory that runs 24/7 drains them in a day and then runs the empty-chain list. The cascade is still worth building, for correctness and safety (items 3, 5, 6, 7, 8, 14), not for throughput.
2. The honest throughput statement replaces the projection: 11.5 h for the A9 window under a graph chain with cap enforcement; 110 min per shipped card; zero of five shipped cards without a human fix.
3. Order: ouroboros side first (205a/205b and S-039 re-cut, E-04 re-cut, `om_pow`, missing-file lint, orchestrator list from the profile, JSON DAG emitter, stale docs), then the agora cascade with the dispatcher stopped throughout, in three waves: safety and truth; the seat; the scheduler and process model. Deferred out of the cascade: compiler and sanitizer gates (need a positive-control PX and clang installed), the Windows gate (weekly platform gate, D2 amendment), mutation as a blocker (WARN), shadow fixture (dropped), D1 (own PR), the vision judge.
4. Verification plants one control per confirmed finding class, not only the happy paths: seat down, merge race, bad merge in a different file from its victim, cyclic cut, missing-skeleton card excluded, plateau to diagnosis to repair end to end. The watch is one full epic (E-04), not 24 hours.
5. Three rulings for Westley, Round 9 in DECISIONS.md: D2 amended (Windows as a weekly platform gate, not an epic blocker); the honest interrupt list accepted or trimmed; the day-side plan (who writes the 92 placeholder controls, 100 skeleton test files and 12 missing C files, and at what cadence), which is the real schedule.
