One row per stage 4 fight. The page (`scripts/skilllab/arena/page.py --stage 4 --sentences <this file>`) prints these three sentences under the layout's strip in place of the layout's own keys. Stage 4 judges one thing: who saw whom first, and does the fight follow from it. Beside each fight is its control strip, the same fight with sight off, where everyone sees everyone from tick 0. The percentage is the heuristic target, never scored. The line `first seen A <tick>  B <tick>` under each strip is the first sim second at which that side's units see any enemy.
