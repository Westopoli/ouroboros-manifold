# P4 units mockup — design as built

Rung P4 of the prototype ladder: six units, movement and combat on one layer, toy tactical sim in GDScript at a fixed 20 Hz tick, seeded and replayable from a command list. Built against E-11, E-13, E-18's 60-second paragraphs and Round 1 rules (R-082 through R-100, R-132/R-136).

## Running it

```
~/.local/bin/godot --scene scenes/mockups/units/units_mockup.tscn
```

Hotkeys: `Space` play/pause, `T` step one tick, `R` reset the current scenario, `Tab` toggle kit, `F12` screenshot, left-click select, left-drag box-select, right-click move, `Shift`+right-click attack-move, right-click on an enemy attack-target. Scenario dropdown and kit button are also clickable in the HUD.

This could not be run on Westley's seat from this session; confirmed instead that the QA driver is inert without `--qa-script`: booting the scene through `qa_boot.gd` with `--qa-scene res://scenes/mockups/units/units_mockup.tscn` and no `--qa-script` produces an empty output directory (no `_run.json`, no side effects) — the driver's own `_ready()` returns immediately when that arg is absent.

## Design as built

**Roster and kits.** `roster.gd` loads a kit JSON (`kits/greek.json`, `kits/settled_reach.json`); both carry the identical R-082-01 stats table, differing only in display name, palette, and (deliberately) nothing else in silhouette geometry — the era-agnostic rule (R-145-01) says a Raider reads as a Raider in any era, so shape and size are shared, only colour and name vary. Six roles: `flanker` (Raider/Peltast), `screen` (Interceptor/Skirmisher), `frontliner` (Vanguard/Hoplite), `support` (Warden/Priest), `artillery` (Bombard/Ballista), `anchor` (Bastion/Phalanx). Silhouette shapes: `swept_dart`, `flat_wing`, `wedge_slab`, `spire_radial`, `long_barrel`, `wide_block`, one fixed vocabulary word per role, sizes doubled once during this pass for legibility (see checklist).

**Sim (`toy_sim.gd`, `UnitsToySim`).** Fixed 1/20s tick, in order: commands, targeting, movement, collision, attacks, facing, deaths. Movement is BFS route + line-of-sight string-pulling on a 100wu grid for point-to-point orders, with a per-tick ally-block probe (40wu ahead) and a fixed left/right sweep (seed-biased per unit) for local deflection; a deflected heading holds for 60wu before re-evaluating. A multi-unit move/attack-move order translates a shared destination by each unit's offset from the group's centroid (not a literal shared point — units can't all physically stand on the same coordinate) and paces the whole group to its slowest member's speed, which is how `formation_move`'s "arrive together" requirement (FORM-9, ≤40 tick spread) is actually achieved; this pacing mechanism isn't named by any R-### rule and is flagged below as a design choice, not a guess. Collision is a 3-iteration push-apart pass, 30wu minimum separation, stationary units take zero displacement. Attacks are flat-damage, backswing-gated (10 ticks); every unit's attack timer starts pre-charged so the *first* shot after acquiring a target fires immediately rather than waiting a full interval — a deliberate pacing choice for a 60-second demo, not an R-### requirement, and flagged below.

**View (`unit_view.gd`).** One `MultiMeshInstance3D` per (role, faction), mesh built once per silhouette shape via `SurfaceTool` (a flat-topped extruded polygon, six shape vocabularies), material per (role, faction) carrying the kit's faction colour. Strike roles (`flanker`, `screen`) draw 3-5 small copies in a fixed V-offset pattern to read as a squad. Hit flash and the death sequence (bloom → drift → darken → remove) are driven purely by `current_tick - event_tick`, never wall-clock — deterministic regardless of frame pacing. Death removal is fixed at 80 ticks (4.0s) post-death, chosen to sit inside both the brief's "3 to 5s darkening" and DEATH-8's "60 to 100 tick wreck window" language, which otherwise conflict with a separate "removed at 15s" line — flagged below.

**Camera, HUD, input.** `camera_rig.gd` is fixed top-down (pitch locked at 90°, never tilts, unlike the landscape mockup's dynamic pitch) with pan/edge-scroll/wheel zoom, height range 500-12000wu, canvas 8000x8000wu. `hud.gd` owns the sim instance, kit/scenario loading, the play/pause/step loop, and all input: left-click select, drag-box select, right-click move/attack-move (shift)/attack-target-on-enemy, scenario dropdown, kit toggle (Tab), tick/survivors/selected/command-log readout.

**Scenarios.** Six JSON files at the brief's 60-second numbers: `lineup` (all six roles in a row), `mirror_10v10` (frontliner mirror, 250wu, both attack-move), `bombards_1000` (10 artillery vs. a close point-man frontliner plus two trailing at 1000wu, so all ten bombards concentrate fire on one target the way the E-11 paragraph implies), `flank_4v4` (4 flankers vs. 4 artillery around a routing obstacle), `formation_move` (4 flankers + 4 anchors, 3000wu move), `plain_move_under_fire` (3 frontliners crossing under 2 artillery, testing R-096-01).

## What the report answers (see also the finishing report, delivered as chat output)

1. **Silhouettes.** `frontliner` (wedge) and `anchor` (wide block) read clearly in both kits. `support` reads as round but not obviously "radial with a projection." The two Strike squads read as a scattered cluster, not individual dart/wing shapes, at this camera height. `artillery` (long_barrel) is the weakest reader: its 20wu width means it appears as a small blob rather than "the longest" shape, the opposite of its intended silhouette read.
2. **Fight pace.** `mirror_10v10` (pure frontliner mirror): no death within 1200 ticks (60s) at these R-082-01 numbers — a single 1:1 pairing takes roughly 1600-1700 ticks (~80s) to produce a first kill by hand calculation from the roster's own damage/interval numbers, well past the "obviously working in 60 seconds" bar, though still comfortably inside R-085-01's ≥45s floor. `bombards_1000` (concentrated-fire design): first death ~tick 120-140 (6-7s), matching the "before they close" framing well. 45s does *not* feel visually readable for the pure mirror at these numbers — see the open question below.
3. **Flanking.** The toy steering did not produce a concave flanking front in `flank_4v4`. Over 900 ticks the Raiders approached in a route-following near-single-file line (funnelled by the obstacle corridor) and were picked off by Bombard long-range fire one at a time; net result at tick 900 was 1 Raider dead, 0 Bombards dead — the opposite of the E-11 paragraph's "Raiders kill the Bombards." This is a legitimate finding about the interaction between long Bombard range (1200wu) and Raider approach speed/HP, not a sim bug (SOLID-5 and MIRROR-4 both confirm the underlying mechanics are correct).
4. **Frame/tick time.** `fps_ms` (llvmpipe, 720p, per rendered frame): 217.5ms. `timing_ms.tick_avg` (20 units): 0.36ms, well under the 2ms budget.
5. **Under-defined points** — see below.

## Questions for Westley

Built the simplest thing that shows each question; no candidate was picked silently.

- **Formation-pace mechanism.** `formation_move`'s "arrive together" requirement isn't specified by any R-### rule in the toy_sim section. Implemented: a multi-unit move order paces the whole group to its slowest member's speed (simplest thing that satisfies FORM-9). Candidates: (a) keep this as the toy's answer; (b) a real formation system with per-unit slots/offsets held relative to a leader (E-13 S-095, explicitly out of Round 1 scope); (c) no cohesion at all — accept "arrive together" only holds for same-speed groups.
- **Pre-charged attack timer.** Every unit's first shot after acquiring a target fires immediately rather than waiting a full interval. Candidates: (a) keep pre-charged (used throughout this build, makes `bombards_1000`'s pacing work); (b) require a full interval before the first shot too (more "realistic," but pushes `bombards_1000`'s first death well past 60s using the same concentration design); (c) split the difference with a shorter fixed first-shot delay.
- **Death-visual removal timing.** Brief text conflicts: "3 to 5s darkening" / DEATH-8's "60 to 100 tick wreck window" vs. a separate "removed at 15s" line. Built at 80 ticks (4.0s), inside both the darkening range and DEATH-8's window; the "15s" line is either a different (longer, colder-linger) phase this toy doesn't implement, or a stale number. Candidates: (a) keep 80-tick removal as the whole lifecycle (this toy); (b) darken over 80 ticks then hold a "cold" wreck until 300 ticks (15s) before removal, matching the 15s line literally; (c) treat 15s as superseded by DEATH-8's window.
- **Silhouette proportions for legibility, especially `artillery`.** `long_barrel`'s 20wu width reads as a small blob rather than "the longest, one linear axis" shape at any of the smoke script's camera heights. Candidates: (a) widen it (loses "dominated by one linear axis" purity but reads better); (b) keep the thin proportions and rely on a much closer default camera zoom than this mockup uses; (c) accept it reads poorly at "map" zoom and reads fine at "micro" zoom (untested — no micro-zoom shot in this pass).
- **Projectile look.** Round 1 of this mockup has no projectile/attack-visual layer at all (E-18 S-134 is a separate story, not in this ladder rung's scope per the brief's "abilities off in Round 1" framing extended informally to attack visuals); combat reads only via hit-flash and death. Candidates: (a) add a placeholder projectile line/dot for a future pass; (b) leave attack visuals to the eventual real renderer, this toy only needs to prove movement/combat/death.
- **Flinch (R-087-01) and the `stop` command (R-096-06).** Both explicitly skipped/deviated per the brief's own instruction ("Flinch may be skipped, list it"; `stop` implemented as "clear order" rather than literally rejected). Listed here as instructed, not re-litigated.
- **Damage scale for the toy.** R-084-01's full damage model (Brace/Fortify/Warden-aura reduction, R-084-03's AoE cap) is not implemented — flat base × matchup(1.0) only, no falloff, no AoE. Reasonable for Round 1 (abilities off), but Bombard's `AoE wu: 150` stat is present in the roster and silently unused. Candidates: (a) leave AoE for a later combat-visuals pass; (b) implement the AoE cap now since it's cheap and would change `bombards_1000`'s pacing materially.
