# P1 galaxy mockup

Rung P1 of the prototype ladder (`docs/northstar/PLAN.md:162`). Throwaway Godot mockup for E-16 S-106/S-107: the galaxy between missions. Scene: `scenes/mockups/galaxy/galaxy_mockup.tscn`.

## Running it

`~/.local/bin/godot --scene scenes/mockups/galaxy/galaxy_mockup.tscn` from the repo root. Hotkeys: `R` regenerate (from the seed spinbox), `J` toggle the jump-log replay. Left-drag pans, wheel zooms, right-drag orbits, left-click a system to focus it (tweens into the Local band over 1.5s). Hover a faction row in the left list to brighten it and dim the rest; click a row to pin that until it's clicked again or empty space is clicked. Hover a system for a name/owner tooltip.

This could not be run on Westley's seat from this session; confirmed instead by reading `scenes/mockups/_shared/qa_driver.gd`'s `_ready()` that a launch without `--qa-script` in the user args returns immediately, before any output directory or `_run.json` is touched — the driver is inert on a plain interactive `--scene` launch.

## Design as built

One seeded galaxy: 40 to 80 systems on a disc (radius 100wu, denser core), placed by rejection sampling at minimum 6wu spacing. Each system links to its 3 nearest neighbours within 30wu, symmetrised, with isolated systems attached to their nearest neighbour regardless of distance. 4 to 8 factions, capitals chosen by farthest-point sampling, territory grown by a seeded round-robin breadth-first flood over that adjacency until 85 percent of systems are owned; the rest stay neutral. An edge is a bright, thick "border" filament when both ends share a non-neutral owner, otherwise a thin, dim "trade" filament. Faction 0 is the player's; its capital is the single brightest point in the galaxy.

Points are one `MultiMeshInstance3D` of small spheres, scaled by significance (capital 1.0 for the player, 0.85 for other capitals, seeded 0.3-0.8 otherwise) and coloured by a golden-angle hue step per faction id (neutral is grey). Filaments are bezier-arc tube meshes (`ArrayMesh`, 8 sides, 16 segments, arc lifted 0.15 of the chord — numbers carried over from the legacy `constellation_renderer.gd`). An ambient field of 400 dust and 800 star quads plus a faint polar grid, built once, fills the space behind the territory. Faction names float as `Label3D`s at each faction's system centroid, visible only in the Overview band.

Three zoom bands (`OrbitCamera.get_band()`): Overview (distance >= 75, roughly 70+ split with the transition gap), Regional (40-65, the default), Local (< 25, roughly under 32.5 with the gap). Between the plateaus, point scale, filament alpha, and name alpha interpolate continuously through the gap; inside a plateau they hold steady. In Local, the focused system brightens and widens its filaments (via a small overlay tube, not a full geometry rebuild, for performance); everything further than 30wu from it desaturates toward grey. Left-drag pans, wheel zooms (10 percent per notch, clamped 15-120), right-drag orbits (pitch 20-85 degrees), left-click on the nearest projected system within 14px tweens the camera to it over 1.5s and drops it into Local. The faction list (always visible, left side) hovers-to-brighten one faction and dim the rest to 25 percent, click pins that at 5 percent until re-clicked or empty space clicked. Hovering a system shows a name/owner tooltip. Play (J) or three `qa_replay_step()` calls advance a 12-event hand-authored jump log (`fixtures/jump_log_seed11.json`) at two events per second: attack flashes both ends, conquest flashes then recolours the target to the attacker's owner (rebuilding only that system's touching filaments), revolt flashes grey.

Two real bugs were found and fixed while building this (full detail in `qa/checklist.md`): the brief's own hash-to-hue formula collapsed every faction to the same green for small sequential ids (switched to a golden-angle step), and `Label3D`'s separate fill/outline alpha channels left a readable ghost of every faction name visible outside the Overview band (both channels now driven together). A third bug — click-to-focus silently never working over the Xvfb driver, because `_unhandled_input` receives mouse positions in a different pixel space than `project()`/`qa_state()` report — was also found and fixed; see the checklist for the exact mechanism.

Two harness-level defects (Xvfb screensaver blanking after 600s, and the `SimBridge` autoload surviving `--script` boot and routing to an error screen mid-run) were hit repeatedly during this session's QA runs, root-caused and fixed by the design lane on `main` (`a98c47f`, `b65afa8`) and merged in here rather than patched locally, per the brief's rule not to touch `scenes/mockups/_shared/`.

## Questions for Westley

Five points the brief asked to be raised, not decided:

- **Band distances.** 25 / 40-65 / 75 (with the two transition gaps) are this mockup's numbers, taken directly from R-106-03's own values. Candidates if these read wrong in practice: (a) keep as built; (b) widen the Local plateau (say, < 35) since a single system fills most of the frame very quickly at distance 20 on a 100wu-radius galaxy; (c) tie the bands to galaxy radius rather than fixed world units, so a denser or sparser seed doesn't change how "zoomed in" a given distance feels.
- **Shell treatment.** Shells are explicitly out of scope for this mockup (per the brief). The reference captures (`E-09/time_jump_galaxy_and_scroll.png`) lean heavily on soft translucent shell overlap for the "territory" read; this mockup's filament-only look is flatter and more line-graph-like at Regional zoom (see Q7 in the checklist). Candidates for the next pass: (a) add per-faction translucent shells as billboarded blob sprites around each system, cheap but low-fidelity; (b) a proper volumetric/soft-particle shell (higher cost, better fidelity); (c) skip shells entirely and lean harder into filament brightness/width for the "territory" read, accepting the flatter look.
- **Significance formula.** Capital 1.0 (player) / 0.85 (others) / seeded 0.3-0.8 (everyone else) is a direct reading of R-106-01's "brightest for the player's core, dim neutral for uncolonized" plus the brief's own numbers section. Candidates: (a) keep as built; (b) derive significance from something in-fiction (population, tech, whatever a real snapshot eventually carries) instead of a flat random draw, so significance means something beyond "is a capital"; (c) make neutral systems visibly dimmer than owned-but-minor systems (right now a neutral system's significance is drawn from the same 0.3-0.8 range as an owned minor system, so "dim neutral for uncolonized" only shows up via the grey colour, not brightness).
- **Right-drag orbit in Round 1.** Built as specified (right-drag orbits yaw/pitch). Candidates: (a) keep it; (b) cut it for Round 1 — the reference captures never show orbit, only pan/zoom/focus, and one fewer interaction is one fewer thing to test; (c) keep it but disable pitch beyond the current 20-85 degree clamp questions (e.g., should straight-down top-down ever be reachable, matching the reference's near-top-down polar-grid framing more closely than the default 55-degree pitch does).
- **Flash duration and replay pace.** Flash is 0.6s (point 3x scale pulse + an expanding ring quad); replay advances at two events per second when playing. Measured finding: three `qa_replay_step()` calls fired back-to-back (no `wait_frames` between them, matching the brief's own smoke-script shape) leaves the transient flash already faded or past its peak by screenshot time — only the persistent recolour is reliably visible in a script-driven shot (see FLASH-X in the checklist). Candidates: (a) keep 0.6s/2-per-second, accept that only interactive play (not scripted screenshots) shows the flash cleanly; (b) lengthen the flash (say 1.0-1.2s) so a screenshot taken a beat later still catches it; (c) slow replay pace (one event/second) so consecutive events don't visually stack.
