## About this game

**The pitch.** You are a commander, not a king, handed a task force and an impossible job by a civilization with its own plans. The galaxy does not wait for you: factions rise and fall, borders move, alliances break, and none of it is scripted. Missions are full real-time, thirty to sixty minutes of continuous play, and the game is won on the ground you read, not on a stat sheet. A smaller force that understands surrounds, high ground and the layers beats a larger one that does not.

**Layers and the ping.** Every map is a stack of three to five layers. A unit sees its own layer fully, an adjacent layer at half its sensor range, a layer two away at a fifth, and nothing three or more away. In space eras you can ping a layer and reveal the enemies around a point for a few seconds, at a long reach that stops well short of the whole map. Six unit types sit in a soft rock-paper-scissors: Strike flanks Siege, Siege crushes Line, Line absorbs Strike.

**Eras.** Between missions, twenty to a hundred years pass and a chronicle prints what happened, different every campaign. You return as a descendant, an old enemy's heir or a stranger raised by circumstance, in a world that remembers what happened even if your new character does not. The game ships across eras on one shared skeleton; the beta has a space era (the Settled Reach) and a placeholder ground era, and more are planned [TBD: what the public page says about the era count].

**Multiplayer.** Play with friends in a team of 2 to 4 sharing one mission and one civilization. Combat is real-time and a multiplayer match never pauses. Co-op only at launch [TBD: platforms, and whether the economy seat is in the first public build].

## Feature bullets

- Full real-time tactical missions, 30 to 60 minutes, no pause in multiplayer
- Three to five layers per map; positioning decides engagements
- The ping: reveal a layer around a point (space eras)
- Six unit types in a soft rock-paper-scissors triangle
- A living, stochastic galaxy simulation that keeps turning between missions
- A generated chronicle of each campaign; you return as someone new
- Multiple eras on one skeleton: space (the Settled Reach) and ground
- Co-op multiplayer for 2 to 4 friends over Steam, lockstep-synchronised
