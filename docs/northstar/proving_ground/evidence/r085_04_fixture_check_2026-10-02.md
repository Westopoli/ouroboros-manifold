## Verdict
Fixture is skewed (separation, spacing, HP pool, ability state; the rule is also vague), but fixing every skew does not rescue the order. Strike < Line holds in every variant. Line < Siege fails in every variant: Siege takes 420 to 1380 ticks against Line's 1608 to 2472. The cause is balance (Bombard 17.5 dmg and 150 wu AoE against 400 HP, with a 1.5 category multiplier on Line), not the fixture. This is a tactical-balance finding for Westley, as A110 says. Nothing was tuned.
