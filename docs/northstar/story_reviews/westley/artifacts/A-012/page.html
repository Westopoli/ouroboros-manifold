Problems the recording turned up

How to read a clip

  • The white triangle on a unit is its front; the thin outline fanning from it is its firing arc.
  • A ring flashes on a unit when it is hit: white on its front, orange on its side, red on its rear. The running count is at the top left.
  • One second of clip is 120 ticks, six times game speed. Every fight here ends in a wipe.
