Findings behind the clips

All 54 give-ups had a friend directly ahead, and the nearest enemy was 335 to 428 wu away, just past the 330 wu at which an idle unit takes a target. The game data shows no colour change on living units at that moment; the units simply stop while the rest move on.

When the idling began: the history comparison puts it at one change, the Round 5 acquisition rule (2026-11-17, "a unit acquires within its notice distance, never the sensor radius"). Before it, L-0054 ended in a wipe with almost nobody idle; at and after it 82 to 87 units per side stand idle for 200 ticks or more.
