Beta stage B5: one role, in groups

Built overnight. Eight clips of the real game below. Nobody has watched them yet, including me: they were checked by the positions the game reports while recording, so you are the first eyes on them.

Three things I need from you

  1. Yes or no on the pipe contract change (H-089). The link between the game and the simulation had no way to start a field with one role and no enemy. I added two commands to it. They add; they change nothing that existed. It is a change to a frozen contract, written up first as its own story, and yours to veto.
  2. Your verdict after watching. What looks right, what looks wrong.
  3. Whether to merge. Everything is on the branch s/B5-one-role-groups. Nothing is on main and nothing was pushed.

What was built

  • The stage. On mission preparation you pick a ground, press DEPLOY FORCES, and a group of 16 or 32 Vanguards stands on a field with no enemy.
  • Orders. Click or drag a box to select. Right-click to move. Press A, then click, to attack-move.
  • What you see. 3D box bodies, the facing triangles you approved, a small mark on the ground at each unit's own slot, and a camera that zooms with the wheel and pans with the arrow keys or the screen edge.
  • The movement is the engine's, over the real pipe. The stage holds no movement rule of its own, so your rulings (keep walking, the lock, a walker side-step) need no change here when they reach the engine.
  • Five stand-in grounds, because stages B2 to B4 are not built: open ground, a hill, a wall with one gap, and city blocks with streets (16 units and 32 units). Units cannot walk through the walls or buildings and they climb the hill.
  • The B1 placeholder you approved is untouched. It is still what default gives you.

In every recorded play every unit reached its place on every order, and no unit was ever inside a wall or a building.

Known gaps

  • You have not played it. The stage counts as Seen only when you press F5 and walk it. The Mac was not tried.
  • The grounds are stand-ins, not the real layouts of B2 to B4.
  • Sending one unit alone shoves its idle neighbours aside (one moved 76 wu) and they do not walk back. Watch for it at the end of each play clip.
  • Walkers have no side-step, so the group queues and funnels at the gap.
  • Attack-move looks the same as move, because there is no enemy.
  • The distance dim is barely visible on a field this small.
  • There is no way back from the stage to the menu.
  • The close-up gap clip starts on an empty opening for about 15 seconds before the group arrives.

The clips

Each play clip runs the same script: a box is dragged round the whole group, the group is sent across the ground, then attack-moved to another far point, then one unit is picked and sent off alone. All clips run at the game's own speed.

To play it yourself (Linux box)

  1. cd ~/Projects/om-wt/b5 and run scripts/setup_editor.sh. It builds the pipe with the two new commands.
  2. Open project.godot there in Godot and press F5.
  3. NEW CAMPAIGN, a mission, then on mission preparation pick hill-16, gap-16 or street-16 in the variant picker and press DEPLOY FORCES.
  4. Drag a box round the group, right-click somewhere far, then press A and left-click somewhere else.

Decisions I made alone

  • Changing the frozen pipe contract with two additions, not faking "no enemy" by hiding a mission's 221 other units and its enemy.
  • Stand-in grounds behind one seam, so B2 to B4 can replace them without touching the stage.
  • A new scene for B5; B1 stays as you approved it.
  • Slot marks are read from the simulation, not worked out by the game.
  • A camera rule (zoom and pan) that the staircase row does not list.
