## Why two thresholds were revised, with the evidence

**SHAPE-7.** The brief's candidate oracle was `fraction_within_r_of_core` (cells with `district >= 1` within 2000wu of the core, over all such cells), expecting clean < 0.7 and planted (city-disc) > 0.9. Measured: clean 0.4148, planted **0.3104** — planted is *lower* than clean, and neither ever approaches 0.9. Reason: `city-disc`'s population is `clamp(1 - dist/CITY_REACH_WU, 0, 1)` with `CITY_REACH_WU = 4500`, and the `district >= 1` cutoff is `pop >= 0.2`, i.e. `dist <= 3600wu`, regardless of the plant. For a uniform disc of radius 3600, the area inside a concentric 2000wu circle is a fixed `(2000/3600)^2 ≈ 0.309` — the metric is bounded below 0.31 by geometry no matter how "disc-like" the shape is, so it can never reach the brief's 0.9 candidate with these constants. `footprint_aspect` (the bounding-box max/min of `district >= 1` cells) does discriminate cleanly: 1.4565 clean vs **1.0000 exactly** under the plant (true circular symmetry). Verify.gd's city-disc check now asserts `footprint_aspect > 1.15`. This needed a second line-fix too: the hard `water > 0.5 -> pop = 0` zero-out was still active even under the plant at first, clipping the "disc" into a coastal crescent against the shoreline (aspect only moved 1.4565 -> 1.5217, not circular at all) since the core sits near water by `_find_city_core`'s own scoring. Gating that line on the plant too (`and not QaPlants.is_active("city-disc")`) is what produced the exact 1.0.

**CTRL-3 and the diff fuzz.** At the brief's own default fuzz (5%), a pixel-identical pair (verified by hash-equality *and* visual inspection — same terrain, only the HUD's timing-label text and stability-slider handle differ) measured **0.995** different — essentially "completely different image" for two renders of literally the same data. Root cause under test: `compare -metric AE -fuzz 5%` on this scene is dominated by sub-perceptual noise (edge anti-aliasing / dithering across ~12,000 small mesh pieces rasterized twice, once per regenerate) that a 5% per-channel fuzz tolerance does not absorb. Swept fuzz 5/10/15/20/30 on the identical-hash pair: 0.995 / (not swept) / 0.000197 / 0.000079 / 0.000001 — it converges to near-zero by fuzz 30 while a genuinely different pair (two separate clean processes, same seed, for DET-1) stays at 0.000000 at fuzz 30 too, and CTRL-3's real clean signal (vegetation-density change from the stability slider, confirmed by eye: more small trees/props at stability 90) holds at a small but clearly non-zero 0.001715 — a ~1700x separation from the planted 0.000001. **Recommendation: fuzz 30, not the brief's default of 5**, for this scene; `mockup_qa.sh diff`'s own default argument was left at the brief-specified `5%` (a spec'd CLI default, not mine to silently change) — every number in this checklist that used `diff` was measured by passing `30` explicitly.

**TIME-5.** The brief's candidate ("total <= 2000ms clean") doesn't match this scene's real cost: clean `timing_ms.total` measured 3799-4797ms across many runs (FieldMap.generate + GroundBuilder.build + Placer.place for a 24000wu canvas and ~12,000 pieces, CPU-bound, no shortcut). A first attempt at the `regen-stall` fault site placed `OS.delay_msec(3000)` *before* `t0 := Time.get_ticks_usec()` in `hud.gd:_regenerate`, so the stall never showed up in `timing_ms.total` at all (only an external wall-clock measurement, like verify.gd's own check around the whole `qa_seed` call, caught it: 7086-8357ms vs <6000ms). Fixed by moving the delay to right after `t0` is captured; `timing_ms.total` now correctly reads ~7164ms under the plant vs ~4797ms clean. Recommend clean <= 6000ms, planted > 6000ms as the revised candidate (matches verify.gd's own threshold, which was calibrated against the same real numbers).

**ERR-0.** Every single run — clean or planted, whichever mockup — printed the exact same 10-line `errors.txt` (4x `RID allocations ... were leaked at exit`, 2x `Pages in use exist at exit in PagedAllocator`, 1x `shaders ... were never freed`, 3x `Buffer with GL ID of ... leaked ... bytes`). This is Godot's own Compatibility-renderer shutdown noise on `quit()` under llvmpipe, not a script/logic error (no `SCRIPT ERROR`, `USER ERROR`, or `Node not found` ever appeared in any clean run). The brief's candidate ("exact: 0") never holds in practice; recommend either accepting count==10 as the clean baseline, or narrowing `scan`'s error regex to exclude this specific known GL-shutdown signature.

This is the same fault class `objectives`' pass 2 found and fixed (`docs/northstar/research/mockups/objectives/README.md`) — `landscape/camera_rig.gd` itself is untouched by this brief, still polling `get_viewport().get_mouse_position()` every frame.

**Why `changed`, not `increased`, on all four edges.** An early draft used `increased` for right/top (their calibrated pan direction) and `changed` for left/bottom. Two problems, both from chaining four edge tests on one running camera with no reset verb available to `firstlook` scripts: (1) the ground-follow height lerp (`camera_rig.gd:_update_ground_follow`) leaves a real but tiny (<2px) residual on `objective_px` for a few frames after any camera move settles, which could tip a strict `increased` check either way — fixed by widening the idle settle before each baseline `state` from a few frames to 35, which measurably drives the lerp to bit-exact convergence (proven: two `state` dumps 15 real frames apart were bit-identical to the last printed digit; a broken edge's "before" and "after" then compare exactly equal, not almost-equal). (2) With the noise fixed, `right` then `left` then `top` then `bottom` still share one camera position across groups, so `top`'s real projected direction depends on where `right`+`left` left the camera — under the clean code (both work, camera returns near its start) `top` reads one sign; under the `right`-only mutation (right is a no-op, left still moves the camera to a different resting spot) `top` read the opposite sign at first, printing a spurious `FAIL` on an edge the plant never touched. `changed` (any non-zero move counts, either direction) is immune to that sign flip and, given the now-real zero noise floor, is exactly as strict at catching a fully dead edge (bit-identical before/after) as `increased` was — so every edge check in the final script uses `changed`.

## Space kit mode (2026-10-03, pass 6)

Kit `space` builds a `SpaceFieldMap` (`field_map_space.gd`): five sheets of plates, each from its own seed. A plate is one polygon with straight edges, drawn as one mesh per layer; the cell masks (`masks`, the fine fracture) are filled from the polygons. A size field removes some lattice centres (large floes) and splits others into four (small floes). Shared density is on by default: every layer uses the same density noise and a plate's keep roll is a hash of its centroid snapped to a coarse lattice seeded from the seed alone. Where a cell is blocked on a layer and the one above, the outline of those cells is traced into walls that fill the gap (outline only; no per-cell slabs).

Pass 5 adds two functional maps. `masks_ordinary` is the fine mask opened with a 3 by 3 block (300 wu, `MIN_PASSAGE_WU`): anything narrower is solid for ordinary units, and reachability, pocket filling and joining run on it. `small_masks` is what small craft can pass: inside small-craft sections the fine mask (a disc 100 wu across, `SMALL_PASSAGE_WU`, is one cell on the 100 wu grid, so the opening is the mask itself) united with the ordinary map, outside sections the ordinary map. Sections are batches of up to 6 adjacent plates per layer: seeds are plates where the density noise is in its top quartile, in hash order (or, with "Sections near layout", the plates at the objective, staging and vantage nodes, grown in turns), until `small_share` percent of the layer's cells are covered. Key `V` cycles the view: `visual`, `ordinary` (blocked cells of `masks_ordinary` as flat grey tiles, the pretty mesh hidden), `small` (the same for `small_masks`, plus the cells open to small craft and not to ordinary units in orange); each section is outlined in orange in every view. `hab_ring` is drawn 12 by 6 instead of 64 by 32: the hidden crossing marks (hundreds of toruses at 4,096 triangles each) were 2.9 of the 3.2 million triangles at density 70; the gap fill was already outline walls, about 120,000 triangles.

Pass 6 changes how a cut-off region is joined to the main one (ordinary-unit map). Default `widen`: for every region of 40 cells or more the main region does not reach, the pair of cells (one in the region, one in the main region, same layer) nearest in the plane is found (exact Euclidean, from a distance transform of the main region per layer), the Bresenham line between them is opened with the 3 by 3 block (the block slides inward at the canvas edge so it stays whole), and the plate polygons of that layer lose the same area by clipping, not by re-deriving them from the mask: the channel is the union of the opened cell squares, and each polygon minus a rectangle is up to four convex pieces, so plate edges stay straight and mask and picture agree. Rounds repeat (at most 8) until no region of 40 cells or more is cut off; pockets under 40 cells are filled solid first. `drop` is the pass 5 method (the plates on the shortest plate path become open water, outlined in the teal debug frame). The debug frames (teal outlines of plates dropped for reachability) are hidden unless key `F` or `qa_set frames true`; the orange section outlines are always drawn. The HUD button "Join: widen | drop" and `qa_set join` switch the method.
