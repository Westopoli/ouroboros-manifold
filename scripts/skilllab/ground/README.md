# Ground lab, parked

Parked until the sim has ground (era kind ground, E-01). Nothing here is a ground answer: it was built on the space engine, where there is no ground.

- `highground.py`: the height-rule sweep. A slope is stood in for by a timed layer change at one place, so it measures layer-change time and reach rules, not terrain slopes.
- `scenes_ground.py`: `tall_cover` and `tall_cover_counter`, nine cover discs between a blob and guarding defenders (unconfirmed draft). The batches in `scripts/skilllab/batches.py` still import them for the landscape batch.

Tests: `tests/skilllab/ground/`.
